Your first hours in GTA 6 set the tone for everything after. Here are the
beginner-friendly habits that pay off immediately.

## Get the basics right

1. **Tweak your settings first.** Aim sensitivity, driving camera and subtitles —
   small changes, big comfort.
2. **Learn the wanted system.** Know how to lose the cops before you need to.
3. **Explore the opening area.** The first district teaches you the world's rhythm.

## Stay alive and solvent

4. **Keep a getaway plan.** Always know where the nearest vehicle is.
5. **Use cover.** Rushing gunfights is the fastest way to a hospital bill.
6. **Save often.** Protect your progress and your cash.

## Build good habits early

7. **Follow the story.** It unlocks mechanics and income streams.
8. **Try every vehicle type.** Cars, bikes and boats each have a role in Leonida.
9. **Talk to the world.** Side characters and events lead to opportunities.
10. **Have fun first.** GTA rewards curiosity — wander, experiment, enjoy it.
