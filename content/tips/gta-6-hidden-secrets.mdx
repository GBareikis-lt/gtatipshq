Half the magic of a GTA map is what Rockstar *doesn't* tell you about. GTA 6 will be
packed with easter eggs, hidden references and genuine mysteries. Here's how to start
hunting.

## Where secrets usually hide

- **Map edges and peaks.** The highest and most remote points often hide rewards.
- **Underwater.** Wrecks, hatches and oddities reward divers.
- **Interiors.** Look for unmarked doors and out-of-place rooms.

## How to hunt efficiently

1. **Explore at night.** Some secrets only appear after dark or in specific weather.
2. **Listen.** Audio cues — strange sounds, radio chatter — often point the way.
3. **Read the world.** Graffiti, posters and signage hide clues and references.

## The big mysteries

Every modern GTA has a flagship mystery (think Mount Chiliad). Expect GTA 6 to ship
with at least one community-defining puzzle that takes weeks to crack.
