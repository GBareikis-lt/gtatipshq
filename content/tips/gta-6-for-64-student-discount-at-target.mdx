## Save $16 on GTA 6 With Target's Student Discount

With GTA 6 priced at $80 for the standard edition, every dollar off counts. If you're currently enrolled in school, Target's Circle Student program can knock 20% off the price of GTA 6's code-in-the-box version, bringing it down to **$64 before taxes** — a solid saving on one of the most anticipated games in years.

The deal was flagged by deal-tracker Wario64 and applies to both the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S versions available at Target.

## How to Qualify

Getting the discount is straightforward, but you do need to jump through a couple of hoops:

- **Sign up for Target Circle** — it's completely free to join
- **Verify your student status** by submitting a school-issued document that confirms you're currently enrolled
- Once verified, the 20% student discount should apply to the GTA 6 code-in-the-box listing

> Keep in mind this deal applies only to the physical code-in-the-box edition. The $100 Deluxe Edition is a digital-only purchase and is not available in stores, so that version won't be eligible.

## Preorder Perks Still Apply

Beyond the price cut, preordering GTA 6 through Target still gets you the bonuses Rockstar has attached to early purchases:

- **Vintage Vice City Pack** — exclusive cosmetic content for pre-order buyers
- **One free month of GTA+** — Rockstar's subscription service for online benefits

Those extras sweeten the deal further, especially if you were already planning to dive into [GTA Online](/news) content on day one.

## Is It Worth It?

Take-Two has maintained that $80 is a fair price for what GTA 6 delivers, and industry analysts suggest this pricing could set a new benchmark across AAA releases. If you qualify for the student discount, $64 is a genuinely competitive price — and one of the easiest legitimate ways to save on the game ahead of its **November 19, 2026** launch.
