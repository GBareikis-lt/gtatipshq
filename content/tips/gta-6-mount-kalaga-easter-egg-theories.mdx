## A Rockstar Tradition: Mountains and Mysteries

Rockstar has a well-documented love of hiding supernatural secrets on prominent peaks. GTA 5 players who climbed Mount Chiliad at the right time and weather conditions were treated to a UFO hovering in the fog, while Red Dead Redemption 2 tucked a similar encounter near Mount Shann. With GTA 6 arriving on November 19, 2026, fans are already mapping out where the next big secret might be hiding — and Mount Kalaga is looking like a strong candidate.

## The Brown Mountain Lights Connection

Mount Kalaga is widely believed to be modelled on Table Rock Mountain in North Carolina, and that real-world location has its own strange legacy. For decades, witnesses have reported glowing orbs drifting above the ridgeline — a phenomenon known as the Brown Mountain Lights. Explanations range from the mundane (reflected headlights bent by atmospheric conditions, according to the US Geological Survey) to the outlandish (aliens, ball lightning, and beyond).

The mystery even inspired a Jules Verne novel featuring a mad scientist secretly constructing an airship beneath Table Rock Mountain — exactly the kind of colourful lore Rockstar's writers love to riff on.

> This is unconfirmed speculation from the GTA 6 community, not anything Rockstar has announced or hinted at officially.

## Why This Theory Has Legs

- Rockstar has placed UFO or supernatural events at major mountains in at least two previous titles, suggesting a deliberate pattern.
- The in-game character Cal Hampton appears to have conspiracy-theorist leanings based on early details, which could tie him narratively to whatever Kalaga is hiding.
- The real location's ambiguous, unsolved backstory gives Rockstar creative room rather than forcing them to adapt settled history.

## What to Watch For

If the pattern holds, expect any Mount Kalaga secret to be time- or weather-gated — something only visible at night, during a storm, or after a specific in-game trigger. Keep an eye on our [secrets and tips](/tips) section as players begin hunting after launch.
