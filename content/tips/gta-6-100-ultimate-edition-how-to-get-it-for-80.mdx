## The Deal at a Glance

If you have your eye on GTA 6's $100 Ultimate Edition but would rather not pay full price, Costco currently has a way to close that gap. The retailer is offering four $25 PlayStation Store gift cards bundled together for $80 — that's $20 in savings right off the top. A second option bundles two $75 cards for $120 if you need more credit.

Both deals are listed as **members-only**, so you'll need an active Costco membership (currently $65 per year) to take advantage. The offer runs through **October 11, 2026**, or while supplies last, so there isn't much time to act.

## What's Inside the Ultimate Edition

The $100 tier isn't just a vanity purchase. According to available details, it includes:

- Exclusive weapon variants
- A retro-styled car and a boat
- Special clothing items
- Exclusive access to in-game businesses — **Rideout Customs** (vehicle upgrades), **Sara's Unisex Salon** (haircuts), and **Electric Fang Tattoo**

That last point is worth noting: some in-game services appear to be locked behind this edition, which could matter for players who want full access to customisation options from day one.

## Store Availability

Unlike the standard $80 edition — which will be available as a code-in-box at physical retailers — the Ultimate Edition is expected to be **digital only**. That makes the Costco gift card route one of the few realistic ways to buy it at a discount.

> **Quick tip:** Use the four $25 cards to hit exactly $100 in PSN credit, then purchase the Ultimate Edition directly from the PlayStation Store at launch.
