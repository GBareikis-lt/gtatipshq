## Early-game priorities

1. **Finish story missions first.** Early missions are the most reliable income and
   unlock the systems (businesses, properties, vehicles) that pay off later.
2. **Don't overspend early.** Resist buying the flashiest car day one — invest in
   things that *generate* income.
3. **Loot opportunistically.** Stores, NPCs and stashes add up between missions.

## Smart habits that compound

- **Keep your wanted level low.** Hospital bills and lost time are silent money
  drains.
- **Bank your cash.** Don't carry everything if the game punishes you on death.
- **Learn the map.** Knowing routes saves time, and time is money.
