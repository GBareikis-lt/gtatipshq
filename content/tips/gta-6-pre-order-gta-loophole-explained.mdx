## What the Claimed Loophole Is

A user on r/GTA6 says they accidentally stumbled onto an interesting quirk while managing their GTA 6 Ultimate Edition pre-order. According to them, when you cancel a pre-order that includes a free month of GTA+, the associated subscription licence is removed from your account. If you then place the pre-order again, a fresh licence is generated — effectively restarting that free trial.

The poster, based in Germany, notes the Ultimate Edition costs around €100 and bundles one free month of GTA+, which normally runs €7.99 a month. Their logic: cancel and re-order once a month between now and the **November 19, 2026** launch, and you could stack roughly four free months of GTA+ without ever paying for the subscription directly.

## Important Caveats

This is an **unverified Reddit claim** — treat it as a rumour, not a confirmed exploit.

- We have not tested this ourselves and cannot guarantee it works.
- Platform storefronts (PlayStation Store, etc.) may flag repeated cancel/re-order cycles on the same account.
- Rockstar or the relevant store could patch or block the behaviour at any point.
- There may be regional differences in how pre-order licences are handled.
- Repeatedly cancelling a pre-order could theoretically affect your order history or payment method standing.
