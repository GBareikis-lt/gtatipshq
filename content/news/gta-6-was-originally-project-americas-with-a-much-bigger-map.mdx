## From Codename to Leonida

In a wide-ranging interview with Eurogamer published in October 2026, Rockstar Games lifted the lid on some fascinating behind-the-scenes details about GTA 6's long development journey. Perhaps the biggest revelation: the game was originally known internally as **Project Americas**, and its early vision was genuinely enormous — covering "big swaths" of land spanning both the north and south of the American continent.

That scope is a long way from what we now know as the game's confirmed setting: the fictional state of Leonida, a sun-soaked take on Florida with the sprawling city of Vice City at its heart.

## An Ambitious Vision That Shifted Over Time

It's not unusual for major games to start life with grander ambitions that get refined as development matures. What's striking here is just how far-reaching Project Americas apparently was. A dual-continent map would have been an almost incomprehensible undertaking, even for a studio of Rockstar's scale and resources.

The same interview also touched on other sensitive topics that have surrounded GTA 6's development:

- **Crunch** — working conditions and long hours during production
- **Delays** — how the project's scope evolved over time
- **The 2022 leak** — one of the largest in gaming history, which saw early footage spill online

> Rockstar has rarely spoken openly about its internal development process, making this interview a genuinely rare look behind the curtain.

## What It Means for the Final Game

None of this changes what's coming on **November 19, 2026** — GTA 6 is still launching as confirmed. But understanding Project Americas gives fans useful context for why development took so long and why Rockstar ultimately focused its energy on making Leonida as rich and detailed as possible rather than chasing a bigger footprint.
