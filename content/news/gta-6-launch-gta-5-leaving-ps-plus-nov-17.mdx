## GTA 5 Getting Pulled from PS Plus Right Before Launch

If you have been meaning to revisit Los Santos one last time before heading to Vice City, your window is closing fast. According to reports from PlayStation Plus subscribers on Reddit, Sony is removing GTA 5 from the PS Plus Extra and Premium catalogues on **November 17, 2026** — just 48 hours before [GTA 6 releases on November 19](/news).

The notification reportedly includes a direct link to the GTA 6 pre-order page, making the intent pretty clear: Sony and Rockstar appear to be nudging longtime GTA 5 players toward picking up the new game rather than staying in the old one for free.

## What This Means for PS Plus Subscribers

- **PS Plus Essential members** are unaffected — GTA 5 was never part of that tier's monthly lineup in this instance.
- **Extra and Premium subscribers** have until November 17 to finish up anything they want to do in GTA 5 or GTA Online before it disappears.
- If you want to **keep access** after that date, you'll need to purchase the game separately.
- GTA 5 is also **not currently on Xbox Game Pass**, so Microsoft subscribers are already without it.

> The timing is unlikely to be a coincidence. Removing a free alternative right before a major paid release is a well-worn industry tactic, but it does create a clean break and a clear reason to upgrade.

## Should You Bother Replaying GTA 5 First?

That's entirely up to you. GTA 6 is set in a brand-new version of Vice City with new protagonists Jason and Lucia, so there's no direct story continuity that makes a GTA 5 replay mandatory. That said, if you've never experienced the Franklin, Michael, and Trevor storyline, the next two weeks are your last chance to do so at no extra cost.
