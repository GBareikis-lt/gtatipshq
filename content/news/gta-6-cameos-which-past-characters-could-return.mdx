## Rockstar's History of Surprise Cameos

One of Rockstar's favourite storytelling tricks is quietly dropping familiar faces into new games. When GTA V launched, sharp-eyed players spotted returning figures from GTA IV and its DLC episodes — sometimes in unexpected, even grim, circumstances. Characters like Packie McReary, Johnny Klebitz, and Lazlow all resurfaced years after their Liberty City adventures. With GTA 6 arriving on November 19, 2026, the question of who might pop up in Leonida is a natural one for the community.

## Fan Favourites in the Frame

A recent discussion on the GTA 6 subreddit highlighted some popular community picks for potential cameos. It's worth stressing these are purely fan wishes, not confirmed by Rockstar in any way. The most commonly mentioned names include:

- **Martin Madrazo** — the volatile cartel boss from GTA V would fit naturally into a Florida-adjacent crime story
- **Jimmy De Santa** — Michael's layabout son, now older and presumably no wiser
- **Yusuf Amir** — the flamboyant, outrageously wealthy Dubai dealmaker from The Ballad of Gay Tony
- **Luis Lopez** — Tony's capable enforcer, who many fans feel deserved more screen time
- **Phil Collins** — one commenter's wildcard suggestion, though licensing a real celebrity cameo is a very different challenge for Rockstar

## Fresh Start or Familiar Faces?

There's a genuine debate here. GTA 6 is centred on new protagonists Lucia and Jason, and Rockstar may prefer a clean slate rather than leaning on nostalgia. It has also been over a decade since GTA V, meaning several Liberty City veterans would realistically be approaching retirement age — or worse.

> Cameos work best when they feel organic rather than forced. Rockstar tends to get that balance right, but nothing has been confirmed for GTA 6 yet.
