## Another Wave of GTA 6 Leak Footage

A social media account operating under the name CyberLeek has been posting what appear to be clips from a working build of GTA 6, and the latest footage is drawing significant attention. In one video, protagonist Jason Duval fires at a wall and the bullet holes form the word "LEEK" — a stunt seemingly designed to demonstrate that CyberLeek is interacting with a genuinely playable version of the game rather than simply sharing pre-recorded material.

## What the Clips Reportedly Show

According to reporting from GameSpot, the leaked footage includes several notable details:

- A blue-and-yellow prop plane flying over what looks like Vice City
- A pause menu used to browse radio stations, including V-Rock and Back Country Radio
- Recognisable licensed tracks such as "For What It's Worth" (attributed to Stevie Nicks) and "Rhinestone Cowboy" by Glen Campbell
- A radio station called Symphony FM also appears to be present
- Earlier clips from the same account reportedly showed Jason playing basketball and using a taser-style weapon on enemies

CyberLeek has also teased releasing story-ending footage involving Lucia Caminos — the game's female lead — and ran a public poll asking followers whether they should post it.

> **Spoiler warning:** If further footage from CyberLeek surfaces, it may contain major story spoilers for GTA 6. Browse carefully.

## What This Means (and What It Doesn't)

None of this material has been verified or acknowledged by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive, so treat everything here as unconfirmed. Leaked builds do surface ahead of major releases, but footage can also be staged or manipulated. Rockstar has aggressively pursued legal action following past leaks, so the situation could change quickly.

With GTA 6 still scheduled for **November 19, 2026**, there is plenty of time for official information to emerge through proper channels. Keep an eye on our [news section](/news) for any verified updates.
