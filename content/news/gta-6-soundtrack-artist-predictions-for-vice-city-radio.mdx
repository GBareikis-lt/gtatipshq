## Fan Speculation Heats Up Around the GTA 6 Soundtrack

With GTA 6 still months away from its November 19, 2026 launch, the community is already dreaming about one of the series' most beloved features: its radio soundtrack. A Reddit post from u/BE4STGAM3R on r/GTA6 put together a bingo-style prediction chart listing artists they believe could land spots on the Vice City stations — and it's sparked some genuinely interesting debate.

It's worth stressing this is pure fan speculation, not anything confirmed by Rockstar. Take it as a fun community exercise rather than a leak.

## What Makes a GTA Soundtrack Pick Likely?

The original poster laid out a reasonable framework for their choices:

- **Historical precedent** — Rockstar has a habit of revisiting artists from past GTA soundtracks, so names that appeared in GTA IV or GTA V have a natural edge.
- **Geographic fit** — GTA V leaned heavily on LA-based hip-hop to match Los Santos. If GTA 6's Vice City mirrors Miami and South Florida, expect Florida artists and Southern rap to get prominent placement.
- **Genre variety** — The post openly acknowledges gaps in synthwave, metal, and Latin music, all of which feel like near-certainties given the setting.

## Genres Worth Watching

Vice City's Miami backdrop practically writes the brief for several genres:

- **Latin and reggaeton** — Miami's cultural identity makes this almost mandatory.
- **Synthwave and 80s throwbacks** — Even if GTA 6 is set in the present day, Rockstar loves a retro station.
- **Southern hip-hop and trap** — Florida has produced some of the most distinctive rap voices of the last decade.

> Rockstar has never officially confirmed any soundtrack artists. Everything here is community guesswork until an official announcement.
