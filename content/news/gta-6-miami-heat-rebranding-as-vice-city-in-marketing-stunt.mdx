## Miami Heat Goes Vice City

In one of the more eye-catching promotional moves ahead of GTA 6's release, the Miami Heat NBA franchise is reportedly set to temporarily rebrand under the Vice City name — the fictional Miami-inspired city at the heart of the upcoming game. According to VideoGamesChronicle, the stunt is timed to coincide with the eve of GTA 6's launch on November 19, 2026.

It's a natural fit. The Heat already have a long-running 'Vice' aesthetic — their Vice Nights alternate uniforms have been fan favourites for years — so leaning into the GTA 6 connection feels organic rather than forced.

## What We Know So Far

- The rebrand is described as a marketing stunt, meaning it is temporary and tied specifically to the GTA 6 launch window
- The timing lines up with early November NBA regular-season games, giving the promotion real broadcast visibility
- No official statement from Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive has been published at the time of writing, so the full scope of the deal remains unclear

> This is reported by a high-trust outlet but has not yet been formally confirmed by Rockstar or the Miami Heat organisation. Treat details as subject to change.

## Why It Matters for GTA 6 Fans

Cross-promotions at this scale signal that Take-Two is pulling out all the stops for what is shaping up to be the biggest entertainment launch of 2026. Real-world brand tie-ins could also hint at the kind of living, pop-culture-aware world players can expect inside the game itself.
