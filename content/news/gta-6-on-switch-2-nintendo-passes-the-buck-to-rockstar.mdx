## Nintendo Stays Quiet on a Potential GTA 6 Port

When asked directly whether GTA 6 would arrive on the Switch 2, Nintendo of America's president gave a notably non-committal answer, essentially pointing reporters toward Take-Two and Rockstar as the parties who would make that call. It's a diplomatic dodge, but it does keep the door open — Nintendo didn't say no.

## What This Actually Tells Us

Reading between the lines, a few things stand out:

- Nintendo has no announcement to make, at least not yet.
- The phrasing suggests there may be ongoing conversations, or at minimum that Nintendo isn't actively blocking the idea.
- Take-Two has historically been willing to bring its titles to Nintendo platforms when the hardware can handle them — several GTA titles appeared on older Nintendo handhelds.

That said, nothing here is confirmed. This remains firmly in rumour territory until Rockstar or Take-Two speaks up directly.

## The Hardware Question

The bigger unknown is whether the Switch 2 can run a game of GTA 6's scale at an acceptable quality level. The console is significantly more capable than its predecessor, but it will still face a tough comparison against PS5 and Xbox Series X versions. A port is technically plausible, though it could require meaningful compromises in resolution or detail.

> GTA 6 is officially set for release on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. No Switch 2 version has been announced.

If a Switch 2 version is in the works, expect Take-Two to announce it separately, likely after the main launch. Keep an eye on our [news section](/news) for any updates as they come.
