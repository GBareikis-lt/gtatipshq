## Free GTA+ Month Comes With Your Preorder

If you've already put in a GTA 6 preorder, there's a small bonus tucked into the deal: a complimentary one-month GTA+ membership. On the surface that sounds great, but there's an important string attached — the subscription auto-renews once that free month is up, and you'll be charged $8 if you haven't cancelled beforehand.

> **Heads up:** It's currently unclear exactly when the free month starts counting down. Rockstar is expected to clarify this as the November 19, 2026 launch gets closer, so keep an eye on official channels.

## What Is GTA+?

For those unfamiliar, GTA+ is Rockstar's optional subscription tier for GTA Online. Paying members receive:

- **$500,000 in-game currency** deposited each month
- **Access to The Vinewood Club**, where one free vehicle is available to claim monthly
- **Rotating bonus perks** such as discounts, property bonuses, and XP boosts

It can offer decent value if you're a regular GTA Online player, but it's easy to forget about a trial and end up billed for a service you don't intend to use long-term.

## How to Cancel Before You're Charged

Cancelling is straightforward once you know where to look:

- **PlayStation / Xbox console** — cancel through the platform's own subscription management settings
- **PC (Steam, Epic, Xbox app)** — cancel through whichever storefront you used to purchase
- **Rockstar Games Launcher** — also supports cancellation directly

Full step-by-step instructions are available on Rockstar's official support site if you get stuck.

## Bottom Line

The free trial is a nice perk for anyone curious about GTA Online's subscription perks, but make sure you set a reminder to cancel well before the month ends if you'd rather not commit to a recurring charge.
