## Two States on One Licence Plate

Eagle-eyed fans combing through GTA 6 trailer footage have noticed something small but telling: two distinct licence plate designs — one reading **LEONIDA** and one reading **GLORIANA**. In GTA history, plates have always reflected the in-game state rather than individual cities (San Andreas being the classic example), which strongly suggests Gloriana is a fully separate state bordering Leonida, not just a city or region within it.

This is unconfirmed speculation at this stage, so treat it as an interesting fan theory rather than hard fact.

## What It Might Mean for the Map's Northern Edge

Most of the trailer footage focuses on central and southern Leonida — coastal areas, wetlands, and Vice City itself. The north of the map, beyond Mount Kalaga, remains largely unseen. That gap is where Gloriana likely sits, and it raises a genuine question: how will Rockstar handle the boundary between the two?

Possible approaches fans are discussing include:

- A hard invisible wall or impassable terrain (mountains, dense forest)
- A "wanted level" border crossing that punishes players for straying too far
- A driveable but empty buffer zone that simply runs out of road

## The Southern Edges Are Easier to Explain

Florida's geography does Rockstar a favour on three sides. A peninsula surrounded by ocean means water boundaries feel completely natural for the south, east, and west coasts. Rockstar could lean into the setting with fuel limits for boats, severe storms, or other environmental deterrents — nothing that breaks immersion.

> The northern border, however, is the real puzzle. An invisible wall would feel jarring; a mountain range or state line checkpoint could work much more naturally.

## Why It Matters

If Gloriana is real and accessible in some form, the playable world could be larger than any previous GTA map. Even if it remains off-limits at launch, its existence on licence plates suggests Rockstar has built out the lore of a wider fictional America around Leonida. Keep an eye on any future trailers for more northern landscape footage — that's where the answers are most likely hiding.
