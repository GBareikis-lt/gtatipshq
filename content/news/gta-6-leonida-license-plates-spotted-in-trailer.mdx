## Leonida Plates on Full Display

A sharp-eyed member of the GTA 6 community has spotted what looks like the complete lineup of in-game Leonida license plates tucked into the Bikers in Ambrosia scene from the trailer. It's the kind of blink-and-you'll-miss-it detail that Rockstar tends to layer into promotional footage, and it's already got fans talking about just how much regional variety the game might feature on the road.

## A Tourism Hub Needs Out-of-State Visitors

The observation makes a lot of sense within the context of GTA 6's setting. Vice City has always been framed as a glamorous tourist destination — think South Beach energy — so it would be realistic for the streets to be packed with visitors from across the fictional United States. The Reddit post specifically calls out the hope of seeing plates from familiar Rockstar locations:

- **San Andreas** – home of Los Santos from GTA 5
- **Liberty City** – the GTA 4 setting inspired by New York
- **Las Venturas** – the Vegas-style city from San Andreas

Having out-of-state plates cruising through traffic would be a subtle but effective way to make the world feel genuinely connected to the broader GTA universe.

## What to Realistically Expect

> This is an unconfirmed fan observation from trailer footage. Rockstar has not officially detailed how license plate customisation or NPC traffic variety will work in GTA 6.

The original post suggests these plates probably won't be available through a custom shop, but may simply appear organically on NPC vehicles in traffic. That kind of passive world-building would fit Rockstar's usual approach to environmental storytelling. Whether or not you can slap a Liberty City plate on your own car remains to be seen when the game launches on **November 19, 2026**.
