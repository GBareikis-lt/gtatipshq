## Miami Beach Opens the Door to Rockstar

In a move that blurs the line between GTA 6's fictional Vice City and its real-world inspiration, the City of Miami Beach has officially approved entering negotiations with Rockstar Games over a promotional partnership ahead of the game's release. According to Eurogamer, the deal would see Rockstar pay the city millions of dollars in exchange for advertising space in and around the iconic seaside destination.

It's a fitting arrangement — Vice City has always drawn heavily from Miami's culture, architecture, and coastline — but not everyone on the city council appears thrilled about it.

## "Not Worth Any Amount of Money"

The approval didn't come without pushback. At least one official voiced strong opposition, reportedly arguing that lending Miami Beach's image to a game franchise known for violence and controversy wasn't justified regardless of the financial return. That dissenting view didn't block the negotiations, but it signals the deal could still face friction before anything is finalised.

Importantly, the city placed a notable condition on any agreement: GTA 6 branding may only appear **discreetly**. Large-scale, in-your-face advertisements appear to be off the table, which may shape how Rockstar ultimately structures the campaign.

## What This Could Mean for the GTA 6 Launch

With GTA 6 set to launch on **November 19, 2026**, a real-world Miami marketing push would fit neatly into a late-summer or autumn promotional window. If finalised, fans visiting Miami Beach could encounter subtle nods to the game in the actual city that inspired it — a genuinely novel way to build hype.

> Keep in mind: negotiations have only been approved, not concluded. No campaign is confirmed yet.
