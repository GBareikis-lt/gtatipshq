## Fan Maps GTA 6's Urban Freeway to Real Miami

A dedicated member of the GTA 6 community over on r/GTA6 has put together an interesting geographical comparison, using mile markers from the official State of Leonida promotional website to trace the freeway corridor that runs through Hamlet, Vice City, and Waning Sands — then overlaying that stretch onto Miami's real-world Interstate 95.

The result is a rough but compelling scale comparison that gives players a sense of just how faithfully Rockstar appears to have modelled the Florida coastline into the game's map.

## How the Mapping Was Done

The user, /u/dooIittIe, pulled distance data directly from Rockstar's own State of Leonida website rather than relying on guesswork. By identifying where each district begins and ends along the urban freeway and converting those measurements into mile-marker equivalents, they were able to:

- Pin the southern boundary of Hamlet to a corresponding I-95 entry point
- Align the Vice City urban core with Miami's downtown freeway section
- Place Waning Sands at the northern end of the corridor, matching a distinct stretch of I-95 above the city centre

## What This Tells Us About the Map's Scale

This kind of fan research — while unverified and unofficial — suggests the Leonida freeway system is built to a recognisable real-world scale rather than being dramatically compressed, as is common in open-world games. If the mile-marker comparison holds up, players commuting across Vice City may experience distances that feel genuinely Miami-sized.

> Keep in mind this is fan-made analysis based on promotional material, not confirmed data from Rockstar. Treat it as an informed estimate rather than hard fact.
