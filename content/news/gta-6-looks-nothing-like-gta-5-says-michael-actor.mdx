## Ned Luke Is Not Having It

Ned Luke, the actor who brought GTA 5's Michael De Santa to life, has come out swinging against a vocal group of doubters who argue that GTA 6 looks too similar to GTA 5. According to a report from IGN, Luke thinks anyone making that comparison is, to put it plainly, completely wrong.

It is a sentiment a lot of longtime fans will probably agree with. The two trailers Rockstar has released for GTA 6 showcase a level of visual detail, environmental density, and animation fidelity that represents a clear generational step beyond anything in GTA 5 — a game that originally launched back in 2013.

## Why the Criticism Exists

The skepticism is not entirely surprising. GTA 5 has been re-released and updated so many times over the past decade that many players are still actively running around Los Santos today. When a new game arrives in the same broad visual style — open-world, third-person, modern setting — surface-level comparisons are inevitable.

That said, the jump from GTA 5 to GTA 6 involves:

- A brand-new location in the fictional Leonida state, based on Florida
- A dual-protagonist setup featuring Lucia as the lead character
- Significantly overhauled physics, NPC behavior, and environmental systems
- Next-gen console hardware as the primary target platform

## What This Means Heading Into Launch

> GTA 6 is currently scheduled for release on **November 19, 2026**, targeting PS5 and Xbox Series X/S.

With launch still months away, it is natural for pre-release skepticism to circulate online. Rockstar has historically let the final product speak for itself, and cast members like Luke clearly believe it will do exactly that.
