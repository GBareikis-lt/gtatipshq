## Stop Killing Games Speaks Out Against GTA 6 Leak

Stop Killing Games — the consumer advocacy campaign best known for pushing back against publishers who shut down online titles and render them unplayable — has issued a public statement making clear it has no connection to, and does not condone, the group responsible for leaking GTA 6 gameplay footage.

The leak surfaced overnight, and the individuals behind it reportedly cited "gamers' rights" as their justification. Stop Killing Games moved quickly to shut down any perceived association with that framing.

## Why the Distinction Matters

Stop Killing Games has spent considerable effort building credibility with both players and regulators around the idea that consumers deserve fair treatment from publishers. Having a separate group invoke "gamers' rights" language to justify leaking unreleased footage — potentially causing real harm to developers — is exactly the kind of association the campaign cannot afford.

The two causes are fundamentally different:

- **Stop Killing Games** targets publisher decisions made *after* a game's commercial life ends.
- **The leakers** obtained and distributed private, unreleased material without consent.

One is a legal consumer rights campaign; the other raises serious questions around intellectual property and the wellbeing of the people who made the game.

## What This Means for GTA 6 Players

For fans waiting on GTA 6 — still on track for its [official November 19, 2026 release](/news) — leaked footage always comes with caveats. Early material may show unfinished builds, placeholder assets, or content that has since changed significantly. Rockstar has not commented on the leak at time of writing.
