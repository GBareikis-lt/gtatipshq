## Take-Two Steps Up Its Legal Campaign

Rockstar's parent company Take-Two Interactive has reportedly escalated its ongoing legal efforts to identify those responsible for a wave of Grand Theft Auto 6 leaks that surfaced roughly two weeks ago. According to Eurogamer, the publisher has filed a fresh subpoena request targeting Discord — and this time it has asked the court to keep the filing sealed from public view.

Unlike earlier, more openly documented legal moves, this new request reportedly names specific individuals rather than casting a broad net. Keeping the subpoena sealed would prevent those targets from being tipped off before Discord is compelled to hand over any relevant account data.

## Why the Secrecy Matters

Sealed subpoenas are a recognised legal tactic when investigators believe that public disclosure could allow suspects to destroy evidence or disappear. By requesting confidentiality, Take-Two is signalling that it believes it has narrowed its focus considerably and wants to act before any named parties can react.

- The leaks in question reportedly contained significant GTA 6 material shared without authorisation
- Discord has been a common hub for distributing and discussing leaked content
- A sealed filing means most details — including exactly who is named — remain unknown for now

> **Note:** This is based on reporting from Eurogamer. The full contents of the subpoena are not public, so specifics remain unconfirmed.
