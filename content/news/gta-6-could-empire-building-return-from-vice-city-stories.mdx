## A Fan Favourite Mechanic Worth Revisiting

Over on r/GTA6, one community member recently sparked a lively discussion by expressing hope that Rockstar revives the empire-building system from *GTA: Vice City Stories* in the upcoming GTA 6. It's an idea that has clearly resonated with a lot of players, and it's easy to see why.

For those who never played Vice City Stories, the mechanic let players take over rival gang territories and convert them into various revenue-generating businesses — from protection rackets to drug operations. It added a persistent, strategic layer to the open world that kept players engaged well beyond the main story.

## Why It Would Work in GTA 6

GTA 6 returns to a Vice City-inspired setting, which makes the idea feel especially fitting. A few reasons why empire building could shine here:

- **Endgame depth** — once the story wraps, players would have a meaningful goal to work toward rather than aimless free-roam
- **Economic progression** — managing and expanding businesses ties naturally into GTA's money-and-power themes
- **Territory rivalry** — competing factions and turf wars could add dynamic tension to the open world
- **Co-op potential** — with GTA Online almost certainly returning, shared empire management with friends could be genuinely compelling

## Nothing Confirmed Yet

It's worth being clear: this is purely fan speculation at this stage. Rockstar has not announced or hinted at any empire-building feature for GTA 6. The game is set to launch on **November 19, 2026**, and meaningful gameplay details beyond what the trailers have shown remain scarce.

> That said, the setting alone makes it one of the more plausible fan wishes circulating right now.

Whether Rockstar takes inspiration from its own back catalogue remains to be seen. Keep an eye on our [news section](/news) for any official reveals as launch approaches.
