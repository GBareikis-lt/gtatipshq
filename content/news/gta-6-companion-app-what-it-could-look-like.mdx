## The iFruit Legacy and What Comes Next

When GTA V launched back in 2013, Rockstar released the iFruit companion app alongside it. The app let players customise Los Santos Customs vehicles, care for Chop the dog, and interact with the game world from their phone. It was a neat idea that never quite reached its full potential — but with GTA 6 doubling down on in-universe social media as a core part of its world, the groundwork for something far more ambitious already exists.

A concept circulating on r/GTA6 lays out one fan's vision for what a next-generation companion app might look like, and it's worth thinking through.

## A Phone Within Your Phone

The core idea is that the app would mimic GTA 6's in-game smartphone interface. Rather than a standard utility menu, opening it would feel like unlocking Lucia or Jason's phone — complete with fictional social media platforms instead of real-world ones. Some possibilities floated in the concept:

- A TikTok-style video platform where players upload Director Mode clips, edited in-game and shared via Social Club
- An Instagram-style photo feed built around GTA 6's Photo Mode, letting players show off custom cars, scenic shots, or funny moments
- Trending feeds driven by community reactions, so the best content surfaces organically

## Why This Makes Sense for GTA 6

Rockstar has already signalled that social media culture is woven into GTA 6's setting and story. An app that extends that fiction into the real world would be a natural fit — and a smart way to keep players engaged between sessions.

> This is an unconfirmed fan concept, not an announced Rockstar feature. Treat it as wishful thinking until Rockstar says otherwise.
