## Official GTA 6 DualSense Controllers Announced

Rockstar Games and PlayStation have teamed up to reveal two limited edition DualSense controllers designed around the GTA 6 brand. The announcement was reported by IGN and marks one of the more tangible pieces of official merchandise to arrive ahead of the game's **November 19, 2026** release date.

While full design details are still emerging, the reveal confirms that Sony is treating GTA 6 as a major platform event — not just a software launch, but a hardware moment worth celebrating with bespoke peripherals.

## What We Know So Far

- **Two controllers** are part of the lineup, suggesting distinct designs rather than a single variant.
- The collaboration is official, coming directly from Rockstar and PlayStation — not a third-party licensee.
- No pricing or exact availability window has been confirmed at the time of writing.
- It is currently unclear whether the controllers will ship alongside the game on launch day or arrive separately.
