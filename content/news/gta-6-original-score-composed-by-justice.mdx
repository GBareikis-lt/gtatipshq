## French Electronic Icons Behind GTA 6's Sound

Rockstar has confirmed that Grammy-nominated French electronic duo Justice — Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay — will compose the original score for GTA 6. The announcement arrived alongside a new launch trailer reminding fans of the game's November 19, 2026 release date.

Justice built their reputation with the landmark 2007 album *Cross*, a record that blended rock energy with disco sensibility in a way few electronic acts had managed before. They followed it with *Audio, Video, Disco* (2011), *Woman* (2016), and most recently *Hyperdrama* in 2024 after a seven-year gap. Their catalog of awards and Grammy nominations makes them one of the most decorated acts in modern electronic music — a serious pedigree to bring to a flagship open-world game.

## GTA 6: The Album and the New Single

Beyond the in-game score, Justice has contributed a brand-new track called **"Flower in a Hurricane"** to *GTA 6: The Album*, the game's official soundtrack release. The song is a notable collaboration, featuring:

- **Tame Impala** — Australian psychedelic rock project led by Kevin Parker
- **SZA** — Grammy-winning R&B/pop artist
- **King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard** — prolific Australian psych-rock band

That is a genuinely eclectic mix of artists, suggesting the album will aim for broad appeal rather than sticking to a single genre.

## What This Means for the Game

GTA titles have always treated their soundtracks as a core part of the experience, and hiring Justice to craft original music rather than simply licensing existing tracks signals real creative ambition. Given the series' famously detailed nightlife settings, an original Justice score could make Vice City's clubs and late-night drives feel unlike anything in previous entries.
