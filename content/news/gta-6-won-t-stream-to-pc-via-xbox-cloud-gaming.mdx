## Microsoft Sets the Record Straight

If you were holding out hope of jumping into GTA 6 on day one through Xbox Cloud Gaming on your PC, that door has officially been closed. Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Ball stepped in to correct a recent report from The Verge, which had claimed Microsoft secured exclusive game-streaming rights for Rockstar's upcoming title.

In a public statement, Ball acknowledged that enthusiasm around GTA 6 and Xbox had contributed to some inaccurate coverage, and made clear that the game would not be available to stream — exclusively or otherwise — to PC at launch.

## What Is Actually Confirmed

Here's what we know for certain heading into the November 19, 2026 release:

- GTA 6 **will** launch on Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026
- The game **will not** be available via Xbox Cloud Gaming at launch
- There is no PC streaming workaround through Xbox at this time
- A native PC version has not been announced for launch day

> PC players will need to wait for an official PC release from Rockstar — no streaming shortcut is on the table right now.

## What This Means for PC Players

Unfortunately, this puts PC-only players back at square one. Rockstar has not announced a simultaneous PC launch, which is consistent with the studio's historical pattern — GTA V, for example, arrived on PC roughly 18 months after its console debut.

For now, the only confirmed way to play GTA 6 at launch is on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. Keep an eye on our [news section](/news) for any updates around a potential PC release window as we get closer to launch.
