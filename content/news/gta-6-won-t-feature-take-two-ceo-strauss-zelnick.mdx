## The WWE 2K26 Cameo That Started the Rumours

When Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick showed up as a fully playable character in WWE 2K26, it naturally got people wondering whether the executive might sneak a similar appearance into GTA 6. After all, with Rockstar's biggest release ever arriving on November 19, 2026, a cheeky CEO cameo seemed like exactly the kind of Easter egg fans would lose their minds over.

## How the WWE Cameo Actually Happened

Zelnick explained to IGN that the WWE 2K26 appearance was entirely his own idea — no groundswell of fan demand, no corporate strategy behind it. He simply asked the development team if they could make it happen, headed to a soundstage for motion capture, and everyone had a good time with it. He said he genuinely enjoys WWE and figured it would raise a smile among industry insiders and maybe entertain a few players too.

The response online was, by his own admission, more of a collective "huh?" than a roar of excitement — something he acknowledged he probably deserved.

> Zelnick noted the feature performed well in terms of player engagement, even if the internet reaction was decidedly muted.

## Don't Expect Him in Vice City

Despite the curiosity his WWE appearance sparked, Zelnick was direct when asked about GTA 6: he will not be in it. No cameo, no Easter egg, no hidden mission involving the boss of Take-Two.
