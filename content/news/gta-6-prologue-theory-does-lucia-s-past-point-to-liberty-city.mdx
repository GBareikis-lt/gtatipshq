## A Pattern Worth Noticing

Rockstar has a habit of cold opens — literally. Both GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 kicked off with snow-covered prologues that set the tone before dropping players into the main world. Fans on r/GTA6 are now wondering whether the studio will follow that same playbook for GTA 6, and one theory making the rounds offers a compelling — if entirely unconfirmed — answer.

## The Liberty City Idea

The theory, posted by Reddit user gulagsurvivor2019, proposes that GTA 6 opens not in sunny Leonida but in a wintry Liberty City. The reasoning draws on official character details Rockstar has shared about Lucia:

- Her bio states her father taught her to fight from a very young age
- It also notes that "fighting for her family" is what landed her in Leonida Penitentiary
- Lucia is said to have roots in Liberty City

Putting those pieces together, the fan imagines a prologue set in Lucia's teens or early twenties — perhaps showing a violent act of revenge following her father's death — that ultimately leads to her arrest and transfer south to Leonida.

## How Jason Fits In

The theory also attempts to explain Lucia and Jason's pre-existing connection. The idea is that Lucia's father made regular business trips to the Leonida Keys, bringing a young Lucia along, where she first crossed paths with Jason as a child. It's speculative, but it would give the duo a shared history that feels earned rather than convenient.

> Keep in mind this is pure fan speculation based on publicly available character bios. Nothing here has been confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two.

## Worth Watching

Whether or not this specific theory holds up, the broader question of *where* GTA 6 begins is one of the more interesting story puzzles heading toward the [November 2026 launch](/news). A Liberty City cold open would be a bold nod to the series' roots before diving into Leonida's heat. We'll be watching for any official hints as release approaches.
