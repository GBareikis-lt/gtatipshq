## A Bird's-Eye View of Vice City

A fresh leak circulating online — covered by PC Gamer — claims to show aerial footage of GTA 6's version of Vice City, and if it's genuine, the scale on display is remarkable. The city appears far more densely packed than anything Rockstar has shipped before, with distinct urban districts, sprawling suburbs, and what looks like a convincing coastal geography stretching out in every direction.

As always with pre-release leaks, treat this with caution. Nothing here has been verified by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive, and fabricated or manipulated footage does circulate ahead of major releases.

## What the Footage Suggests

Assuming the leak is legitimate, a few things stand out:

- **Density**: The city blocks appear tightly detailed even at altitude, hinting at a level of world-building well beyond GTA V's Los Santos.
- **Scale**: The sheer footprint of the metropolitan area looks substantially larger than previous GTA maps.
- **Variety**: Distinct neighbourhoods, waterways, and what appear to be outlying areas beyond the city core are all visible, suggesting a diverse open world.

This would align with earlier unconfirmed reports that Leonida — the fictional Florida-inspired state surrounding Vice City — will be the most ambitious setting Rockstar has ever attempted.

> Worth noting: GTA 6 is currently scheduled for **November 19, 2026**. Rockstar has not officially released any new map details beyond the two trailers.

## Why It Matters

Map scale directly affects how players experience exploration, mission design, and eventually [GTA Online](/news) content. A denser, larger world typically means more emergent moments and longer-lasting replayability. If this leak reflects the final product, expectations for the [open world](/map) are well-founded.

We'll keep an eye on any official reveals as the release date approaches.
