## Rockstar Goes Big in Miami

With GTA 6 set to launch on **November 19, 2026**, Rockstar's real-world marketing push is ramping up fast — and it's hard to miss if you're anywhere near Miami. Building on an earlier stunt that saw the GTA 6 logo airlifted onto the Kaseya Center arena, the campaign has since expanded to include branded blimps cruising the skyline, themed beach chairs, real cars wrapped in GTA livery, and a tie-in with the Miami Heat. It's one of the most ambitious location-based game promotions in recent memory.

All of this makes sense given that GTA 6 is set in Vice City, Rockstar's fictional reimagining of Miami, situated within the broader state of Leonida — the game's stand-in for Florida.

## Local Officials Voice Concerns

Not everyone is rolling out the welcome mat, though. Several Miami-Dade County commissioners have spoken out against the partnership, raising concerns about the message it sends.

- Commissioner René Garcia put it bluntly: *"We're depicting Miami like a murder capital."*
- Commissioner Micky Steinberg said she was philosophically opposed to the county moving forward with the deal.

The pushback centers on the idea that lending official credibility to a game built around crime, violence, and general mayhem could reflect poorly on the city's image.

## Money vs. Message

On the other side of the debate, Miami-Dade County Mayor Daniella Levine Cava and her office are reportedly hoping the Rockstar partnership brings in millions of dollars for the region. It's a classic tension between economic opportunity and public perception — and one that's likely to keep generating headlines as the November release approaches.

> Whether the controversy helps or hurts GTA 6 is an open question, but if history is any guide, the extra attention probably doesn't hurt Rockstar.
