## A Cheeky Corner of Leonida

A new batch of leaked GTA 6 footage circulating online this week includes a short clip that gives us a glimpse at what appears to be a nudist colony somewhere in Leonida. The clip follows co-protagonist Jason as he wanders around a pool and hot-tub area populated by dozens of unclothed NPCs who are socialising, dancing, and generally going about their day.

> **Reminder:** This footage is unverified leaked material, not an official Rockstar reveal. Details could change before the game's November 19, 2026 release.

## NPCs That Push Back

What makes the clip particularly interesting from a game-design standpoint is how the NPCs respond to Jason. Rather than being passive background characters, they actually react to him being there:

- Some NPCs appear largely unbothered by the fully clothed intruder.
- Others visibly show annoyance at Jason's presence.
- One woman spots Jason spying through binoculars and physically confronts him.

That last detail hints at a reactive NPC system where your behaviour — not just combat — can trigger real consequences from the world around you. It lines up with what Rockstar has long suggested about making GTA 6 feel more alive than previous entries.

## The Bigger Leak Picture

This clip is part of a broader wave of leaked footage that has appeared over the past week, apparently from the same unidentified source. Take-Two is reportedly pursuing legal avenues to identify the leaker. A former Rockstar technical director has publicly suggested the leaks are unlikely to cause serious harm to the game's reception — framing them as far less damaging than the infamous Hot Coffee controversy that surrounded GTA: San Andreas years ago.
