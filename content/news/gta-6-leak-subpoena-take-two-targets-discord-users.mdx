## Take-Two Casts a Wide Legal Net

Rockstar's parent company Take-Two Interactive is escalating its ongoing effort to identify the individuals responsible for leaking GTA 6 material. According to a report from PC Gamer, the company has sought a court subpoena that would compel Discord to hand over identifying information — including IP addresses and phone numbers — for potentially thousands of users connected to the investigation.

The scale of the request is notable. Rather than targeting a handful of named suspects, the subpoena appears to cast a broad net across communities where leaked content was discussed or shared.

## Discord's Position

Discord has indicated it has not yet been formally served with the order, and a company spokesperson noted that when it is, the platform will evaluate both the validity and the scope of the request before deciding how to respond. That cautious framing suggests Discord may push back on at least portions of the demand — a not uncommon outcome when platforms receive sweeping data requests.

## What This Means for the GTA 6 Community

For everyday fans, this situation is largely a background legal matter that won't affect your day-to-day engagement with GTA 6 news and community discussion. However, it does serve as a reminder of a few things worth keeping in mind:

- Sharing or distributing leaked game assets carries real legal risk
- Platform privacy protections are not absolute when courts are involved
- Take-Two has a demonstrated track record of pursuing leak-related legal action aggressively
