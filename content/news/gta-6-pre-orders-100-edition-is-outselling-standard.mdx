## Fans Are Spending More Up Front

In a notable signal about player enthusiasm heading into GTA 6's November 19, 2026 release, Take-Two leadership has revealed that a larger share of pre-orders are coming in for the premium $100 Ultimate Edition than for the standard version of the game. That is a striking trend — premium editions typically sell in smaller numbers than base copies.

## What Does the Ultimate Edition Include?

While Rockstar has not published an exhaustive breakdown of every bonus tied to the Ultimate Edition, premium versions of GTA titles have historically bundled extras such as:

- Early access windows ahead of the wide launch
- In-game currency or bonus items for GTA Online
- Exclusive cosmetics, vehicles, or character content
- Digital or physical collector bonuses depending on the retailer

If you are weighing whether the upgrade is worth it, keep an eye on the [official GTA 6 news](/news) as Rockstar is expected to detail exactly what each edition contains closer to launch.

## What This Tells Us About Demand

The fact that a $100 SKU is outselling the cheaper standard edition suggests demand for GTA 6 is exceptionally strong — players are willing to invest more before they have even played a single minute. It also gives Take-Two a healthy revenue signal that could influence post-launch content planning.

> This is based on statements from Take-Two leadership reported by IGN. Exact edition contents have not been fully confirmed by Rockstar at the time of writing.
