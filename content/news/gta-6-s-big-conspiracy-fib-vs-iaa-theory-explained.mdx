## The Theory Taking Over r/GTA6

Rockstar's official GTA 6 synopsis teases that after a job goes sideways, protagonists Jason and Lucia get pulled into a criminal conspiracy stretching across the entire state of Leonida. That's intentionally vague — and the GTA community is already filling in the blanks.

One theory gaining real traction on Reddit proposes that the conspiracy isn't just about street-level crime. Instead, it could be a direct continuation of the bitter, bloody cold war between the FIB and the IAA — the two corrupt federal agencies that turned GTA 5 into a spy thriller with guns.

## Why the FIB-IAA Angle Makes Sense

In GTA 5, the feud between these agencies was anything but subtle. Steve Haines, Dave Norton, the Mr. K interrogation subplot, and the shadowy ULP all painted a picture of federal bodies that behave more like warring cartels than law enforcement. GTA Online continued that thread, with players running covert errands for ULP well into the current timeline.

The theory argues that the battlefield has simply shifted south to Leonida, with Jason and Lucia unknowingly caught between two agencies still fighting for dominance. A line from Trailer 2 — a cop saying something to the effect of *"us cops, we protect each other"* — hints that law enforcement corruption runs deep in Vice City, which would fit neatly into this framework.

## What It Would Mean for the Story

- Jason and Lucia start as small-time operators, not federal targets
- The "easy score" that unravels could inadvertently expose an inter-agency operation
- Corrupt agents from either side might manipulate the duo as expendable assets
- The scale of the conspiracy grows organically as the pair dig deeper

> Keep in mind this is unconfirmed fan speculation. Rockstar has revealed very little about the plot beyond the broad synopsis.

It's a compelling framework precisely because it's grounded in established HD universe lore rather than invented from scratch. Whether it turns out to be accurate or not, we'll find out when GTA 6 launches on **November 19, 2026**. Check back on [/news](/news) as more story details surface.
