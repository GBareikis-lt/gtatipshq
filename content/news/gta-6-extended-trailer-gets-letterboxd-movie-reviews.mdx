## The GTA 6 Trailer Has a Letterboxd Page Now

The GTA 6 extended look that premiered on Netflix has taken on a life of its own — not just as a gameplay reveal, but as a fully-fledged entry on Letterboxd, the social platform normally reserved for logging films. That means players and fans can rate the 25-plus minute trailer like a movie, leave written critiques, and add it to watchlists alongside actual cinema.

## The Reviews Are Exactly What You'd Expect

The results are, predictably, brilliant. Some of the most-liked reviews lean into the joke with complete commitment:

- One user slammed the "film" for having a messy structure and a vague, confusing narrative — then admitted the gameplay looked incredible.
- Another reviewer raised an eyebrow at a scene referencing the real-world GTA 6 leaks, noting it was wild that the trailer "starts with Cyberleek's execution" — a nod to the mysterious figure(s) behind the recent wave of leaked footage.
- At least one commenter questioned the whole exercise outright, asking whether Letterboxd was now logging advertisements.
- Another simply observed that GTA had landed on Letterboxd before GTA 6 itself had even launched.

> The whole moment is a reminder of just how culturally enormous GTA 6 already is — a trailer treated like prestige cinema is not something that happens for most games.

## What It Says About the Hype

With the [official release still set for November 19, 2026](/news), this kind of fan-driven creativity is a sign of how much anticipation has built up around the game. Whether or not you think a trailer belongs on a movie-review site, the community engagement around every scrap of GTA 6 content — official or otherwise — shows no sign of slowing down.
