## Fresh Allegations Surface at Rockstar UK

A new investigative report from Game Developer has brought serious workplace concerns at Rockstar Games back into the spotlight. Multiple staff members — primarily based at the company's UK offices — have come forward with claims covering three main areas: enforced crunch culture, the deliberate use of bonus payments as leverage over employees, and a gender pay gap that is reportedly growing rather than shrinking.

These revelations come as Rockstar workers take steps toward formal union recognition, a push that gained momentum following a string of high-profile sackings in 2025.

## What the Allegations Involve

- **Crunch culture**: Employees allege that long, intensive working hours are actively encouraged or enforced, particularly as GTA 6 heads toward its November 2026 launch window.
- **Bonus manipulation**: Staff claim that discretionary bonus payments are being used as a tool to discourage workers from speaking out or organising collectively.
- **Gender pay gap**: Female employees reportedly face a measurable and widening pay disparity compared to their male counterparts, with little evidence of meaningful corrective action from management.

> These are allegations made by current and former employees to Game Developer. Rockstar has not publicly confirmed or denied the specific claims at the time of writing.

## Why This Matters for GTA 6

Workplace conditions at a studio directly affect the quality and stability of its output. Crunch has long been a contentious issue across the games industry, and with GTA 6 representing one of the most anticipated releases in gaming history, scrutiny of how Rockstar treats the people building it is entirely warranted.

The unionisation effort underway could mark a significant shift in how major studios operate — something worth watching as the [November 2026 release date](/news) draws closer.
