## Sony Unveils GTA 6-Themed DualSense Controllers

During a recent State of Play broadcast, Sony pulled back the curtain on two limited-edition DualSense controllers designed specifically around Grand Theft Auto 6. Available in either black or white, these are shaping up to be a must-have for any serious fan looking to celebrate the biggest game release of 2026.

## What Do They Look Like?

Both colour variants share a consistent Leonida-inspired design language, with a few standout details:

- **Pastel pink button icons** on the face buttons give the controller a distinctly Vice City-esque Florida vibe
- A **blue-purple iridescent shimmer** runs along the lower portion of the controller body
- The **GTA 6 logo is etched into the touchpad**, while the Grand Theft Auto wordmark is embossed on the rear
- **Palm-tree-inspired shapes** are pressed into the grip texture, replacing the standard PlayStation icon pattern on that section

The overall aesthetic is flashy without being gaudy — very on-brand for Leonida.

## Price and Release Date

Both controllers launch on **November 19, 2026**, the same day as GTA 6 itself, and will retail for **$85 each**. That's a modest premium over a standard DualSense, and given the detailed artwork and embossing involved, it seems reasonable for a collector's item.

> If you're planning to pick one up, keep an eye on retailer listings early — limited-edition Sony controllers tend to sell out quickly around major game launches.
