## The Question the Community Is Asking

A lively discussion is circulating on r/GTA6 around one surprisingly specific question: will Rockstar give players a full wardrobe of face coverings for heists and robberies, or will GTA 6 take a more restrained approach similar to the bandana-only system in Red Dead Redemption 2?

It's a fair thing to wonder about. RDR2's mask system was deliberately minimal — a handful of bandanas and a few unique pieces — which fit the frontier setting perfectly. GTA 6, however, is set in modern-day Leonida, where tactical gear, hockey masks, and elaborate disguises feel far more at home.

## What GTA 5 and GTA Online Got Right

GTA 5's heist missions leaned hard into the mask fantasy. Players could equip balaclavas, clown masks, skull masks, and plenty of other options before a job, which added real personality to each crew. GTA Online expanded that further with a huge range of face coverings tied into the game's deep clothing customization.

Given that GTA 6 appears to be doubling down on crime, heists, and character expression — based on what the trailers and various unconfirmed leaks suggest — there's a reasonable expectation that Rockstar won't scale back in this area.

## How It Could Tie Into Wanted Mechanics

Beyond style, mask options could carry real gameplay weight. If Rockstar builds a more sophisticated police detection system — something the community widely hopes for — face coverings might actually influence whether witnesses or cameras can identify your character, adding a tactical layer to what you wear on a job.

> None of this is confirmed. Rockstar has not officially detailed GTA 6's clothing or wanted systems ahead of the November 19, 2026 release.

## Bottom Line

The smart money is on GTA 6 offering more mask variety than RDR2, not less — but until Rockstar shares more, it remains speculation. Keep an eye on [/news](/news) for any official reveals.
