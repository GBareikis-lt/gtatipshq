## Sony Unveils GTA 6 DualSense Controllers at State of Play

Sony used its September 2026 State of Play broadcast to pull back the curtain on two limited edition DualSense controllers themed around Grand Theft Auto 6. The reveal adds to the growing wave of official merchandise and hardware tie-ins building ahead of the game's [November 19, 2026 launch](/news).

## Two Colorways to Choose From

According to the announcement, fans will have a choice between two distinct designs:

- **White variant** – a cleaner, lighter aesthetic that likely draws on GTA 6's sun-drenched Leonida setting.
- **Black variant** – a darker, sleeker look for players who prefer a bolder style.

Beyond the color differences, specific design details — such as custom button artwork, textured grips, or GTA 6 branding placement — have not yet been fully detailed based on the initial reveal.

## What This Means for GTA 6 Players

Limited edition controllers tied to major releases tend to sell out quickly, so if either colorway catches your eye it's worth keeping an eye on retailer pre-order pages sooner rather than later. These kinds of hardware bundles can also signal that Sony is investing heavily in GTA 6 as a marquee PS5 title, which bodes well for platform-specific optimisation and features like DualSense haptic feedback and adaptive triggers being put to good use in-game.
