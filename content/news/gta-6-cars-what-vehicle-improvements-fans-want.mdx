## The Vehicle Balance Problem in GTA Online

A recurring complaint in the GTA community is that the car lineup in GTA Online feels unbalanced. Fictional hypercars like the FMJ and Banshee GTS sit at the top of the performance ladder, while vehicles clearly inspired by real-world icons — think Bugatti and Koenigsegg equivalents — lag behind despite carrying massive price tags.

Top speeds across the board are also capped well under 200 mph, which feels artificial when the real-world machines these cars are based on push 250–300 mph or beyond. Players are now wondering whether Rockstar will rethink this approach for GTA 6.

## What the Community Is Hoping For

A popular Reddit thread on r/GTA6 summed up the wishlist well. Common requests include:

- **More realistic top speeds** — not necessarily requiring players to drive at 300 mph on every street, but a better reflection of real-world performance potential
- **Fairer price-to-performance ratios** — if a car costs tens of millions in-game, it should at least be competitive with cheaper alternatives
- **Better differentiation between car classes** — so that Sports, Super, and Hyper vehicles each feel meaningfully distinct
- **Improved handling models** — more nuance in how weight, drivetrain, and aerodynamics affect driving feel

## What GTA 6 Could Realistically Deliver

Rockstar hasn't confirmed specifics about vehicle mechanics for GTA 6, so everything here is speculation and community wishlist territory. That said, the jump from GTA V to GTA 6 is expected to be substantial across the board, and the new Vice City setting — with its long coastal highways and open roads — certainly creates opportunity for faster, more varied driving experiences.

> Whether Rockstar leans into realism or keeps its arcade-style approach, the community clearly wants vehicle progression to feel fair and meaningful.
