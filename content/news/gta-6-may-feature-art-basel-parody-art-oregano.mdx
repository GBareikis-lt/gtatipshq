## A Shirt With a Story

Eagle-eyed players digging through the latest GTA Online DLC have spotted a small but interesting detail: a new character is wearing a shirt bearing the logo of something called **Art Oregano**. According to the [GTA Wiki](https://gta.wiki/Art_Oregano), this appears to be Rockstar's satirical take on Art Basel Miami Beach, the prestigious annual art fair held at the Miami Beach Convention Center every December.

The same character was previously noted for wearing a necklace linked to Cal Hampton — another apparent GTA 6 reference doing the rounds on r/GTA6 — suggesting Rockstar may be layering multiple world-building details into a single NPC.

## What Is Art Basel, and Why Does It Fit Vice City?

Art Basel Miami Beach is one of the most high-profile contemporary art events in the world, drawing collectors, celebrities, and plenty of spectacle to Miami each year. It's exactly the kind of glamorous, over-the-top cultural fixture that fits perfectly into Rockstar's satirical vision of Vice City.

If Art Oregano does appear in GTA 6, it could show up as:

- A recurring in-world event on the calendar
- A mission or side-story backdrop
- Ambient set dressing in an upscale Vice City district
