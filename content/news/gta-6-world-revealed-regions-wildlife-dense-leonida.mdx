## Rockstar Pulls Back the Curtain on Leonida

With the November 19, 2026 launch now well within sight, Rockstar Games has given fans a much closer look at the open world waiting for them in GTA 6. A new Game Informer feature — accompanied by 13 fresh screenshots — breaks down the scale and variety of Leonida, the sun-soaked fictional state at the heart of the game.

## What We Know About the Map

The coverage confirms that Leonida is divided into distinct regions, each carrying its own visual identity and atmosphere. Rather than a single sprawling cityscape, players can expect a genuine mix of environments — everything from dense urban centres to more rural and wilderness areas in between.

- **Multiple regions** with their own character and design language
- **Wildlife** playing a meaningful role in the open world, suggesting ecosystems that feel lived-in
- **A variety of activities** scattered throughout, pointing to a world designed to reward exploration

The 13 new screenshots themselves highlight the visual density Rockstar is going for — streets packed with pedestrians and vehicles, lush natural landscapes, and interiors that hint at the level of detail the team has put into ambient world-building.

## Why This Matters

> This is the most detailed official look at Leonida's open world structure we've had so far, and it paints a picture of a map that could rival — or surpass — anything Rockstar has built before.
