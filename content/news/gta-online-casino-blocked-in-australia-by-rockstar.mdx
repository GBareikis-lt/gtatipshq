## What Has Changed

Australian players of GTA Online have lost access to the Diamond Casino's gambling features, with Rockstar Games quietly pushing a code-level restriction that blocks participation in casino activities for accounts based in the country. Players noticed the change themselves and began discussing it on Reddit before leaker Tex2 confirmed the update was deliberate, pointing to modifications in the game's backend code.

## What Australians Can No Longer Do

The block is broad. Affected players cannot participate in any of the casino table games introduced back in 2019 as part of the Diamond Casino & Resort update, including:

- Blackjack
- Roulette
- Three Card Poker
- Slot machines

Perhaps more surprisingly, even the Lucky Wheel — a free daily spin that rewards items like cars and clothing without requiring any chips at all — has been disabled for Australian accounts.

## The Context Behind the Decision

The restriction appears to be a response to Australia's ongoing legislative push to tighten controls around online gambling. The timing aligns with increased government scrutiny in the country.

It is worth noting that GTA Online's casino chips are in-game currency and cannot be exchanged for real-world money. While players can purchase chips using real cash through Shark Card microtransactions, there is no mechanism to reverse that process and extract funds. Even so, that distinction has not been enough to keep the feature available under the current regulatory climate.

> Australia is not alone here — several other countries, including some in the Middle East and parts of Asia, have had Diamond Casino access restricted for years.
