## A Common Concern Among Fans

GTA 5's gunplay has long been a point of mild frustration for players who came to the series after experiencing tighter, more responsive shooters. Games like Max Payne 3 — developed by Rockstar itself — set a high bar for fluid, cinematic combat that GTA 5 never quite matched. Naturally, many fans are wondering whether GTA 6 will finally close that gap.

## What the Early Footage Suggests

Based on the extended gameplay look Rockstar has shared, plus additional details circulating online, there are a few encouraging signs:

- **No cap on handgun firing speed** — players appear able to fire semi-automatic pistols as quickly as they can pull the trigger, rather than being throttled by an artificial rate limiter.
- **Reduced screen shake** — a change that should make sustained gunfights feel cleaner and more readable, without sacrificing intensity.
- **Adjustable default movement speed** — the ability to switch your default from walking to jogging is a small but welcome quality-of-life improvement that affects how combat flows in open spaces.

These are relatively small mechanical tweaks on paper, but together they point toward a more responsive and player-driven combat feel.

> Keep in mind none of these details are officially confirmed by Rockstar. Treat them as credible rumours until the studio says otherwise.

## Why It Matters

Combat is central to almost everything in GTA — missions, escapes, and eventually GTA 6 Online encounters. If Rockstar has genuinely tightened the shooting mechanics, it would benefit both casual players and those who push the game's systems to their limits.
