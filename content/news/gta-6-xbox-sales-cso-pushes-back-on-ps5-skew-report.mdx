## The Report That Sparked the Debate

A report from The Verge claimed that GTA 6 pre-order activity is leaning heavily toward PlayStation 5, raising concerns internally at Xbox about the platform's standing heading into one of the biggest game launches in history. The story arrived at an already difficult moment for the brand, which had just been dealing with a fresh round of layoffs.

## Xbox's Official Pushback

Matthew Ball, Xbox's Chief Strategy Officer, didn't let the narrative go unchallenged. Taking to social media shortly after the report surfaced, Ball stated that Microsoft is "very happy" with how GTA 6 is performing on Xbox and that the title is actually setting records for the platform.

He stopped short of sharing specific numbers, so it's worth keeping that in mind — record-breaking on Xbox could still be consistent with PS5 capturing a larger overall share of the market.

## What This Means for Players

For fans who've already locked in their pre-order on Xbox, Ball's comments are reassuring. A few things worth noting as we approach the [November 19, 2026 launch](/news):

- **Both versions are confirmed** — GTA 6 is coming to Xbox Series X|S and PS5 at launch.
- **Performance parity** has not been officially detailed, so keep an eye on any technical breakdowns closer to release.
- **Sales skew toward PS5** wouldn't be unusual given the console's larger install base, regardless of how Xbox performs in absolute terms.

> The full picture won't be clear until official post-launch sales data arrives. For now, both camps have reason to be optimistic.

Whether the PS5 version genuinely dominates or Xbox closes the gap, the real winner is anyone picking up GTA 6 this November.
