## What Is the GTA 6 Extended Look?

Rockstar Games has announced something it's calling an **Extended Look** for GTA 6 — a preview showcase set to drop on Netflix ahead of the game's November 19, 2026 launch. While Rockstar hasn't spelled out exactly what the Extended Look will contain, the name alone suggests this will be a substantially longer and more detailed glimpse at the game than anything we've seen so far. Think gameplay, story context, or both — though nothing specific has been confirmed yet.

> This is expected to be the most in-depth official look at GTA 6 before release, so it's worth planning ahead.

## How to Watch

The Extended Look is being made available through **Netflix**, meaning you'll need an active Netflix subscription to catch it at launch. If you're already a subscriber, simply search for GTA 6 or browse the games/video section when the time comes. No additional purchase should be required beyond a standard plan.

- **Platform:** Netflix (all regions with Netflix access)
- **What you need:** An active Netflix subscription
- **When:** Rockstar has tied the release to their pre-launch promotional window ahead of November 19, 2026 — check Netflix and Rockstar's official social channels for the confirmed date and time as details are announced

## Why This Matters

Rockstar partnering with Netflix for a major preview event is a notable move. It puts GTA 6 footage in front of Netflix's massive global audience, not just the gaming press. For fans who have been waiting patiently since the first trailer, this Extended Look could be the clearest signal yet of what to expect when the game finally arrives.
