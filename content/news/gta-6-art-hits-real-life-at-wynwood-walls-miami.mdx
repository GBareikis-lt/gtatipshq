## Vice City Street Art Goes on Sale

Rockstar Games has teamed up with Miami's iconic Wynwood Walls Museum for a real-world merchandise drop that blurs the line between the game and the city that inspired it. The **Art of Vice City Collection** features wearable pieces created by artists whose murals and street art you'll actually encounter on the walls of Vice City in GTA 6.

Pre-sale is already live over at the [Wynwood Walls Shop](https://shop.thewynwoodwalls.com/collections/gtavi), so fans don't have to wait until the game's **November 19, 2026** launch to own a piece of the world Rockstar has built.

## The Artists Behind the Designs

The lineup is a who's-who of internationally recognised street and contemporary artists, including:

- **1010, AIKO, Ahol, DENIAL, FAILE**
- **Flmingo, Gons x Audet, Leon Keer, Queen Andrea**
- **Ron English, Sonni, AES, D*Face, Tristan Eaton, Typoe**

These aren't just names slapped onto branded merch — their actual design language reportedly appears in-game, meaning picking up a piece now gives you a tangible connection to Vice City's visual identity.

## Why This Is Worth Paying Attention To

> This kind of real-world collaboration suggests Rockstar put serious effort into making Vice City's art scene feel authentic, drawing from genuine Miami cultural roots rather than generic fictional graffiti.

For fans already excited about the game's world-building, this partnership is a small but telling signal of how much care has gone into the environmental detail. It's also a rare chance to own officially licensed GTA 6 artwork well before launch.
