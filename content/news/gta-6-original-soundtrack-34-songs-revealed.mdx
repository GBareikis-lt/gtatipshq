## A Full Original Soundtrack Is Coming With GTA 6

Rockstar's upcoming open-world crime game won't just rely on radio stations and licensed hits — GTA 6 is arriving with a dedicated original soundtrack featuring 34 purpose-built tracks. The album was partially unveiled in September 2026, with six songs made immediately available to stream ahead of the game's November 19, 2026 release.

This marks a notable step up from previous entries in the series, where original scores played a supporting role behind the wheel of licensed music. Having nearly three dozen original compositions suggests Rockstar wants the game's cinematic moments and open-world atmosphere to carry a strong, consistent musical identity.

## Real-World Artists Are Involved

The soundtrack isn't handled by a single composer working in isolation. Multiple real-world artists contributed original material specifically for GTA 6, though the full list of names has only been partially confirmed at this stage. The involvement of recognisable musicians points toward a soundtrack with genuine crossover appeal — not just background texture, but music people might seek out independently.

> Six tracks are already streamable now if you want a taste of GTA 6's sound before launch day arrives.

## What This Means for Players

Beyond the vibe, a rich original score has practical implications for how the game feels moment to moment:

- **Story missions** may carry more emotional weight with bespoke compositions scoring key scenes.
- **Exploration** across GTA 6's open world could feel more cinematic with a dynamic original score underneath.
- **Replayability** often improves when music holds up on repeated listens — 34 tracks gives Rockstar plenty to work with.
