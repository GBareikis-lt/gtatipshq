## The $1.5 Billion Rumour Gets Shot Down

For months, one of the most widely repeated figures in gaming circles has been that GTA 6 cost a staggering $1.5 billion to develop — a sum comparable to building the Burj Khalifa. Rockstar has now pushed back on that claim directly, telling Bloomberg the number is inaccurate. The studio stopped short of providing a correction, though, declining to reveal what the real budget actually is.

So the rumour is debunked, but the mystery remains open.

## What We Do Know About the Scale of Development

Even without a confirmed figure, there are plenty of reasons to believe GTA 6 is among the most expensive games ever produced:

- Development has spanned many years across multiple Rockstar studios worldwide.
- One dedicated Los Angeles-based division focused entirely on NPC behaviour and crowd systems.
- A vast open world covering a fictionalised version of Miami and surrounding Florida environments.

All of that points to a production budget that is almost certainly enormous — just not necessarily $1.5 billion.

## Preorders Suggest It Will Recoup Costs Quickly

Whatever Rockstar and Take-Two actually spent, early signs suggest they won't be waiting long to see a return. Reports indicate the game has already crossed five million preorders, translating to over $500 million in potential revenue before launch. Some analysts project that figure could reach 25 million units by the time GTA 6 releases on November 19, 2026 — potentially pushing pre-launch revenue toward $2 billion.

> It's worth keeping in mind that preorders are not guaranteed sales, and cancellations can occur before a game ships.
