Rockstar's GTA 6 trailers are dense with detail, and every rewatch reveals
something new. Here's our breakdown of the moments that matter most.

## A living, breathing Vice City

The trailer opens on a sun-drenched, neon-lit reimagining of Vice City. Crowded
beaches, dense traffic and reactive crowds hint at a simulation far beyond anything
in GTA 5. Watch the background: pedestrians film on phones, vendors work the
boardwalk, and wildlife moves through the wetlands.

## Meet Lucia and Jason

We get our clearest look yet at the dual leads. **Lucia** is the first playable
female protagonist of the modern GTA era, and her chemistry with **Jason** frames a
Bonnie-and-Clyde story that drives the campaign.

### Details worth a second look

- **Vehicles:** A mix of exotic supercars, muscle cars, boats and bikes.
- **Map scale:** Aerial shots suggest a world spanning city, suburbs, swamp and keys.
- **Tech:** Improved lighting, water and physics define the visual leap.
