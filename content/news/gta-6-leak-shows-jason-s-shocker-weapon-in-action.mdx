## Another Day, Another Apparent GTA 6 Leak

A second wave of unverified GTA 6 footage has surfaced online, following a busy first day that reportedly included clips of protagonist Jason Duval playing basketball solo and what some claimed was the game's full map. As always, treat everything here as unconfirmed rumour until Rockstar says otherwise.

The latest clip, reportedly titled "Taser Video" and flagged by Kotaku, runs through a short but eventful sequence starring Jason in what looks like typical open-world GTA chaos.

## What the Clip Apparently Shows

- **Driving gone wrong:** Jason is behind the wheel of a car with Tate McRae's *Sports Car* audible in the background — suggesting licensed music is in the mix — before getting into a fender bender.
- **On-foot brawling:** After stepping out of the vehicle, Jason pistol-whips several enemies in close-quarters combat.
- **The Shocker weapon:** Jason picks up a taser-style weapon from a downed enemy (named generically "Shocker" in the UI) and proceeds to electrocute multiple foes, including two who charge at him. The weapon's range appears impressively long.
- **A quiet ending:** The clip cuts abruptly to a brief cutscene of Jason sharing a beer on a boat with a character named Cal Hampton.

## What This Could Mean for GTA 6

If authentic, the footage hints at a combat system that rewards improvisation — looting weapons mid-fight and chaining melee into ranged attacks feels very on-brand for the series. A stun-based weapon with solid range could also open up new non-lethal play styles, something GTA has only dabbled with previously.

> **Remember:** Leaked footage can be staged, edited, or taken out of context. Nothing here is confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two. GTA 6 is officially set to launch on **November 19, 2026**.
