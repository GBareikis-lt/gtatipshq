## Lana Del Rey Heads to Vice City

Rockstar Games has pulled back the curtain on GTA 6's in-game radio lineup, and it is stacked with real-world talent. Among the highlights, Grammy-nominated singer-songwriter Lana Del Rey is set to co-host **Back Country Radio** under the character name Delta Dawne. Her co-host is country superstar Morgan Wallen, who steps into the role of MW. Del Rey's husband, Jeremy Dufrene, also makes an appearance on the same station as a character called Mud Dog Mike.

Del Rey — whose early hit "Video Games" gave her an unlikely connection to the gaming world long before this moment — told Rolling Stone she is genuinely thrilled about the collaboration: the opportunity felt special "on so many different levels," and she said she cannot wait to actually sit down and play the game herself.

## A Massive Roster Across the Dial

Back Country Radio is just one piece of a much larger picture. Other confirmed names tied to GTA 6's radio include:

- **Bad Bunny**, continuing Rockstar's tradition of featuring global Latin artists
- **Kerry King and Tom Araya** of thrash-metal legends Slayer

Rockstar has not yet revealed the full station list, so expect more announcements between now and the [November 19, 2026 release](/news).

## Music On the Move — and On Foot

GTA 6 looks to evolve how players consume in-game audio. Beyond the classic experience of scrolling through radio stations behind the wheel, the game will apparently let players listen to podcasts while exploring Vice City on foot — a notable quality-of-life addition that keeps the world feeling alive even when you step out of a car.

> Music has always been central to the GTA experience, and the calibre of talent attached to GTA 6 suggests Rockstar is treating the soundtrack as seriously as any other part of the game.
