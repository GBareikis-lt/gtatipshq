## A Unique Condition for Japanese Buyers

If you're picking up a physical copy of GTA 6 in Japan, there's one extra thing to keep in mind: the redemption code included in the box won't last forever. According to a FAQ published on Rockstar Games' official support page in late June 2026, product codes sold in Japan will expire **170 days after the game's launch date of November 19, 2026** — putting the cut-off somewhere around mid-May 2027.

Rockstar attributes this to regional regulations, making Japan the only territory with a publicly stated code expiry window so far.

## What Does This Mean in Practice?

170 days works out to just under six months. For most players who buy the game around launch and play it within that window, this won't be an issue at all. Where it could matter:

- Collectors who buy a physical copy to store unopened for years
- Anyone planning to give the game as a gift well after launch
- Importers purchasing Japanese editions for use in Japan

If any of those scenarios apply to you, it's worth redeeming the code promptly rather than leaving it sitting in the box.

## Not Entirely Unusual, But Worth Knowing

Digital codes with expiry dates aren't unheard of in the games industry — many promotional and bonus codes come with time limits baked in. What makes this notable is that it applies to the primary redemption code in a full-price retail product, and that Japan appears to be the only region affected by this stipulation at launch.

> Rockstar has not announced similar expiry conditions for physical copies sold in North America, Europe, or other regions.
