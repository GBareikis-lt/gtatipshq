## Florida Locals Have a Wishlist for Leonida

Rockstar has a long track record of packing its open worlds with obscure local detail — RDR2's version of the American frontier is loaded with folklore, historical nods, and regional oddities most players never stumble across. Now, ahead of GTA 6's [November 2026 launch](/news), Florida residents are hoping the same level of care gets applied to Leonida.

A popular thread on r/GTA6 has residents sharing hyper-local landmarks and stories they'd love to see reimagined in the game. The consensus seems to be that Vice City's urban core will track closely with real Miami, while the Everglades and rural north offer the richest opportunities for deep-cut Florida lore.

## Landmarks Worth Watching For

Some of the most-requested real-world locations include:

- **Peanut Island** — a small barrier island reportedly home to a Cold War-era JFK bunker
- **Coral Castle** — a mysterious stone structure built solo by one man over decades, with origins that remain genuinely unexplained
- **Stiltsville** — a cluster of wooden houses built directly over Biscayne Bay, now mostly gone but still iconic
- **Jupiter Lighthouse** — one of Florida's oldest working lighthouses, sitting at the inlet north of Palm Beach

## Stranger History Beneath the Surface

Beyond landmarks, Florida's weirder historical chapters could make for great mission backdrops or environmental storytelling:

- The **Ashley Gang**, Prohibition-era outlaws who ran rum through the Everglades and have been called Florida's answer to Bonnie and Clyde
- **Trapper Nelson**, an eccentric woodsman who built a private alligator zoo on the Loxahatchee River — tours of his old settlement still run today
- A flooded ghost town referencing the **1928 Lake Okeechobee hurricane**, one of the deadliest disasters in U.S. history

> None of this is confirmed content — these are fan hopes, not leaked details. That said, Rockstar's history suggests the team does this kind of homework.

Whether or not any specific landmark makes the cut, the [Leonida map](/map) is shaping up to be one of the most densely detailed settings Rockstar has ever built. Keep an eye on our [news section](/news) as more details emerge closer to release.
