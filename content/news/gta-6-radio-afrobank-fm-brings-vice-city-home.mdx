## Rockstar's Music Page Is Worth Your Time

Rockstar quietly launched a dedicated Music section on the official GTA VI website, letting fans stream snippets from the game's radio stations ahead of the November 19, 2026 release. It's a smart move — and if you haven't explored it yet, carve out twenty minutes and work through the lineup from the top.

The page opens with Cocoteo FM, moves into the twangy comfort of Back Country Radio, and eventually lands on Afrobank FM, a station rooted in sounds from Lagos, South Africa, and Ghana. On paper it might not be everyone's first pick, but the station's presenters deliver a line that apparently stopped more than a few listeners cold.

## "All the Way to Vice City"

After the male host name-drops the African cities the music draws from, his co-presenter follows with a simple closer: *"all the way to Vice City."* According to a post making the rounds on r/GTA6, those four words were enough to bring a grown adult — someone who spent their childhood roaming the original Vice City because their PC couldn't even run San Andreas for months — to the edge of tears.

It's a small moment, but it captures something real. For a certain generation of players, Vice City isn't just a setting. It's a time and a feeling, and waiting 13-plus years for it to return has carried genuine emotional weight.

> The radio stations aren't just background noise — they're Rockstar's way of world-building before you ever load a save file.

## What to Expect from the Soundtrack

- Multiple genre-spanning stations are already accessible on the official site
- Afrobank FM leans into Afrobeats and West African sounds — a first for the series
- Back Country Radio offers classic country vibes for a different corner of the map
- The full in-game lineup will almost certainly be larger than what's previewed online
