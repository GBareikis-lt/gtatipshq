## US Players Could Be Among the Last to Jump In

With GTA 6 launching on November 19, 2026, one of the biggest remaining questions has been simple: *what time* does it actually unlock? Some early answers may have surfaced — though nothing is confirmed by Rockstar just yet.

Fan research account GTA Vice combed through PlayStation Store listings across 63 countries and regions and found that the game appears set for a rolling midnight local-time release rather than a single global unlock. If accurate, that structure puts New Zealand players at the very front of the queue, and US players near the back.

## How the Timeline Breaks Down

Based on the PlayStation Store data gathered by GTA Vice:

- **New Zealand** unlocks first at local midnight — roughly **5 AM PT / 8 AM ET on November 18**
- **Australia** follows shortly after
- **Europe** gets access through the early hours of November 19 (local time)
- **United States** would unlock at midnight local time — nearly **16 hours after New Zealand**

That's a long wait for North American fans watching social media fill up with reactions, clips, and inevitable story spoilers.

> This information comes from unofficial PlayStation Store listings only and has not been confirmed by Rockstar Games. Treat it as a strong rumour rather than a done deal.
