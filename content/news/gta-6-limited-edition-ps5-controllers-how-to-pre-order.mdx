## Two Special DualSense Controllers Are Coming

Sony and Rockstar have joined forces to release a pair of limited edition PS5 DualSense controllers timed to coincide with GTA 6's launch on **November 19, 2026**. If you want a little extra Vice City flair at your fingertips, these could be worth grabbing early.

## What Do They Look Like?

Both controllers share a cohesive tropical theme that fits the game's sun-soaked Florida-inspired setting:

- **Two colourways** — one black, one white
- A **blue/purple faceplate** surrounding the thumbsticks and touchpad
- The **GTA 6 logo** printed directly on the touchpad
- **Palm tree indentations** on both handles for a subtle but distinctive touch

The design is understated enough for everyday use while still being a clear nod to the game.

## When and Where to Pre-Order

Both controllers launch on the **same day as GTA 6 — November 19, 2026** — so stock could move quickly once pre-orders open widely. Push Square has compiled retailer links for the US and UK if you want to lock one in ahead of launch. It is worth checking major gaming retailers and the PlayStation Direct store in your region, as limited edition hardware tends to sell out fast around big game releases.

> **Tip:** If you are already planning to buy a PS5 bundle or upgrade your setup for GTA 6, pairing one of these controllers with your order could save you a separate shipping cost.

## Worth It?

Limited edition controllers tied to major game launches are always a gamble on long-term availability. If the GTA 6 aesthetic appeals to you, pre-ordering sooner rather than later is the safer move.
