## The First Official Cast Confirmation

For months, fans have been trying to piece together who would be voicing GTA 6's characters — scanning trailers for familiar voices, combing through credits, and following every rumour online. Now, for the first time, an actor has stepped forward and publicly confirmed their involvement.

Speaking at the BAFTA TV Tea Party in Beverly Hills, character actor Stephen Root let slip that he does indeed appear in GTA 6. When a reporter asked about the longstanding speculation surrounding his involvement, Root gave a straightforward answer: he's in the game, and suspects his distinctive voice gave it away long before any official word came out.

## Who Is Stephen Root?

If the name doesn't immediately ring a bell, the voice probably will. Root has an enormous body of work spanning decades, but he's perhaps best known to a certain generation as the voice of Bill Dauterive in the animated series *King of the Hill*, and to another as the soft-spoken Milton Waddams in the office comedy classic *Office Space*. His voice is genuinely one of the most recognisable in the business, which Root himself acknowledged — joking that fans who grew up with *King of the Hill* spotted him in GTA 6's early promotional material almost immediately.

His character in the game is named **Brian Heder**, though Root didn't go into further detail about the role during the interview.

## What This Means for GTA 6

> This is the first casting confirmation to come directly from a talent themselves — neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has made any official announcements about the voice cast to date.

Root's enthusiasm was clear, describing GTA 6 as "an amazing thing to look at" and expressing genuine excitement about being part of it. With the game still scheduled for release on **November 19, 2026**, expect more cast details to emerge as Rockstar's promotional campaign ramps up.
