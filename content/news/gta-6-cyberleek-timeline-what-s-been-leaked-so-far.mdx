## A New Wave of GTA 6 Leaks

Starting around mid-August 2026, an anonymous individual or group operating under the name **CyberLeek** began releasing unverified GTA 6 gameplay clips and map imagery. The situation is still developing, and nothing from this leak has been officially confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two. Treat everything below as unverified rumour.

Before any footage surfaced publicly, Reddit users noticed that several domain names associated with CyberLeek — including backup spelling variants — appeared to have been registered as early as August 14. A crypto token, liquidity pool, and a voting program were also reportedly created around August 15, suggesting some level of pre-planning behind the release.

## What the Footage Reportedly Shows

The first clip emerged on August 18 and ran for roughly a minute. It purportedly featured protagonist **Jason Duval** outside a coastal property already glimpsed in official Rockstar marketing. Key apparent details from this clip:

- A **Focus mechanic** tied to activities like playing basketball — successfully scoring a basket appeared to raise Jason's Focus level
- The Focus stat was visible on the HUD during gameplay
- The setting matched locations fans have already spotted in the official trailer

Subsequent leaks allegedly included additional gameplay snippets and images said to depict sections of the [GTA 6 map](/map).

## Why You Should Stay Cautious

> Leaks of this nature are sometimes genuine, sometimes fabricated, and frequently a mix of both. Until Rockstar speaks, nothing here should be treated as confirmed.

The pre-registration of domains and the creation of a cryptocurrency token before any footage appeared raises questions about the motivations behind the leak. Whether this is a disgruntled insider, an external hack, or something else entirely remains unknown.
