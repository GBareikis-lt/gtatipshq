## Rockstar Unveils a Pricey GTA 6 Merch Drop

Rockstar Games has announced a limited merchandise collection tied to GTA 6, dubbed the **Goodtime State Vice City** set. Priced at £349, it's aimed squarely at dedicated fans with deep pockets — and a sense of humour. The collection is available to pre-order now, though stock will presumably be limited given the premium price point.

One important caveat worth flagging upfront: the bundle does **not** include a copy of GTA 6 itself. If you're planning to pick this up, you'll still need to budget separately for the game when it launches on **November 19, 2026**.

## The "Wouldn't Clear Airport Security" Angle

Rockstar is leaning hard into GTA 6's irreverent tone with the items on offer. The set reportedly includes pieces that cheekily reference the game's Vice City lifestyle — think the kind of accessories that raise eyebrows, not eyebrows. Eurogamer's headline nod to airport security says it all: this is merchandise designed to be provocative and on-brand, not subtle shelf decoration.

Specific items in the collection include a mirror keychain and a spoon, both unmistakably styled after certain Vice City vices. It's bold product design that fits perfectly with the game's satirical take on Florida excess.

## Is It Worth It?

At £349, this is firmly collector territory. A few things to weigh up:

- **No game included** — purely cosmetic/lifestyle merchandise
- **Limited availability** — likely to sell out or appreciate in value
- **Strong thematic identity** — very on-brand for GTA 6's tone
- **Conversation starter** — though maybe not at customs

> If you're a serious GTA collector, pre-ordering sooner rather than later is probably wise given the limited nature of the drop.
