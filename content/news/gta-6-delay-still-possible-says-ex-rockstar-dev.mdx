## Former Dev Weighs In on Delay Risk

Mike York, an animator who spent several years at Rockstar Games before departing in 2017, has told Indy100 that he would not be surprised if GTA 6 slips past its current **November 19, 2026** launch date. York was careful not to claim inside knowledge — he has been away from the studio for nearly a decade — but pointed to Rockstar's established pattern of holding a game back whenever the team feels it needs more polish.

"They could delay it again," York said, adding that Rockstar has the clout to "do whatever they want" and that it "wouldn't hurt them" in the long run.

## A Pattern Worth Noting

GTA 6 has already moved twice since its original Fall 2025 window:

- **Fall 2025** → pushed to May 2026 for quality reasons
- **May 2026** → pushed to November 2026, again citing quality improvements

York suggested a further six-month shift is one scenario that would not shock him, though he stopped well short of calling it inevitable or likely.

> This is one former employee's opinion, not a Rockstar statement. The official release date remains **November 19, 2026**.

## What to Watch Next

The clearest signal of whether that date is safe may come on **August 27**, when Rockstar is set to air an extended look at GTA 6 through Netflix. A confident, gameplay-heavy showcase would ease delay fears; radio silence or a short teaser might do the opposite.

For now, the November 19 date stands. Keep an eye on our [news section](/news) for any official word from Rockstar as the showcase approaches.
