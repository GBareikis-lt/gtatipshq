## A New GTA 6 Trailer Is Coming

Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive have officially announced that an "extended look" at Grand Theft Auto 6 will debut on **Thursday, August 27, 2026**, premiering exclusively on Netflix before going wider. The showcase is scheduled for **3pm ET / 8pm BST**, so mark your calendars.

This is the first major new footage reveal since the original trailers, and the timing makes sense — with the game's [release date](/news) locked in for November 19, 2026, Rockstar is entering the final marketing push.

## What to Expect

The wording "extended look" suggests something more substantial than a standard hype trailer. That could mean:

- A longer gameplay or cinematic sequence (potentially several minutes)
- A deeper dive into the story, characters, or setting
- Possible details on features like the open world, online mode, or progression systems

Of course, until August 27 arrives, we're speculating. Rockstar keeps its cards close, so treat anything beyond the official announcement as rumour for now.

## Why Netflix First?

The Netflix premiere is an interesting choice. Rockstar and Take-Two have been building closer ties with streaming platforms, and Netflix's gaming arm continues to expand. Premiering there likely guarantees a high-quality, controlled viewing experience before the trailer hits YouTube and social media.

> **Tip:** Set a reminder for 3pm ET / 8pm BST on August 27 — the Netflix stream may be the cleanest early way to watch without compression artefacts.
