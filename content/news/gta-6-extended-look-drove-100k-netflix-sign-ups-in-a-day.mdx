## The Numbers Behind the GTA 6 Extended Look

Rockstar's partnership with Netflix to host the 26-minute GTA 6 Extended Look trailer appears to have been a significant win for the streaming giant. According to analyst firm Ampere Analysis, the exclusive trailer window drove more than 100,000 new Netflix subscriber sign-ups in the United States alone — all within a single day.

Ampere ranked the Extended Look as the third-largest Netflix event of 2026 for new subscriber acquisition, behind only the final season of Stranger Things and a high-profile MMA bout between Ronda Rousey and Gina Carano.

## Lapsed Subscribers Came Back Too

New sign-ups were only part of the story. Ampere also noted a strong "reactivation" effect among existing Netflix members who had gone quiet on the platform:

- More than 5% of US Netflix members who hadn't watched anything in the prior 30 days returned specifically to watch the Extended Look.
- That reactivation rate reportedly outpaced every other piece of content Netflix released in that same 30-day window.
- All of this activity came from a six-hour exclusivity window before the trailer became more widely available.

> These figures are analyst estimates from Ampere Analysis, not officially confirmed data released by Netflix or Rockstar Games.
