## Pre-Orders Explode Across the Globe

It should surprise absolutely nobody: Grand Theft Auto 6 is dominating PlayStation Store pre-order charts in virtually every regional storefront Sony operates. Since pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026, the game has climbed straight to the number-one spot across countries in North America, Europe, Asia, and beyond.

The sheer breadth of the chart dominance is still striking — it is rare for a single title to hold the top position simultaneously across so many different regional stores, each with its own distinct audience and purchasing habits.

## Why This Matters

Pre-order chart performance is one of the clearest early indicators of a game's commercial trajectory. For GTA 6, the numbers reinforce what most analysts already expected — this is shaping up to be one of the biggest entertainment launches in history, not just in gaming.

A few reasons the momentum makes sense:

- **Years of anticipation** — Rockstar's first mainline GTA entry in over a decade has been one of the most-discussed upcoming releases since its first trailer dropped.
- **Expanded audience** — GTA 6 introduces a playable female lead for the first time in the series, potentially broadening its appeal.
- **Holiday runway** — With the [November 19, 2026 release date](/news) locked in, players have time to plan purchases ahead of the holiday season.
