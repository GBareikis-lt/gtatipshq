## WWE Made a Play for 'Vice City'

In an unexpected legal footnote ahead of GTA 6's November 2026 launch, professional wrestling giant WWE filed to trademark the phrase 'Vice City' — only to abandon the attempt shortly after. The move caught the attention of gaming and legal observers alike, given how closely the term is associated with Rockstar's long-running Grand Theft Auto franchise.

## Why Does This Matter for GTA 6?

Rockstar and Take-Two have historically guarded their Vice City branding carefully, and GTA 6 is confirmed to be set in a fictional version of Miami heavily inspired by that iconic location. A successful third-party trademark on 'Vice City' — even from a major entertainment brand like WWE — could theoretically have created complications around marketing, merchandise, or branding tied to the game's setting.

That said, it's worth noting:

- WWE ultimately **abandoned** the filing, so no conflict materialised.
- Trademark disputes in entertainment are common and rarely affect game releases directly.
- Take-Two's own legal team is well-resourced and experienced in protecting its IP.

> It's unclear what WWE's original intentions were — whether for a game, an event, or another product — but the timing relative to GTA 6's release window made it an eyebrow-raising move.

## What Happens Next?

With the trademark abandoned, there's no known obstacle to Rockstar freely using Vice City branding in its [upcoming marketing push](/news). Fans can expect Rockstar and Take-Two to continue building hype around GTA 6's setting as the November 19, 2026 release date approaches. This small legal episode is essentially a non-story in practical terms — but it does highlight just how valuable the Vice City name has become in popular culture.
