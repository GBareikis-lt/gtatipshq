## Six Stations, Endless Vibes

Rockstar Games has pulled back the curtain on GTA 6's audio landscape, officially confirming six radio stations that players will be able to tune into while cruising through Leonida ahead of the game's **November 19, 2026** launch. As expected, the lineup spans a broad range of genres:

- **Cocoteo FM** – Modern Latin music hosted by El Martillo and La Gata
- **Back Country Radio** – Renegade country vibes with DJs MW and Delta Dawne, plus roving reporter Mud Dog Mike
- **AfroBank FM** – African music curated by a dedicated DJ

Three more stations are still to be detailed, so the full musical picture isn't complete yet.

## Star Power Behind the Mic

Rockstar has once again leaned on celebrity talent to bring its fictional DJs to life. Grammy-winning artist **Bad Bunny** and singer **Lana Del Rey** are both confirmed as voice talent on Cocoteo FM. Over on Back Country Radio, Del Rey's husband **Jeremy Dufrene** voices the colourful field correspondent Mud Dog Mike — a fun real-world connection that adds some extra personality to the station.

## A Series First: On-Foot Podcasts

Perhaps the most exciting audio announcement is something entirely new to the franchise. GTA 6 will feature **on-demand podcasts** that protagonists Jason and Lucia can stream through their smartphones — meaning you don't need to be behind the wheel to enjoy commentary, interviews, or whatever in-world opinions Rockstar has cooked up. It's a natural extension of the smartphone system and adds another layer of world-building to Leonida.

> This is the first time in GTA history that audio content can be consumed on foot, not just in vehicles.

With six stations confirmed and more likely on the way, GTA 6's soundtrack setup is shaping up to be the most flexible in the series. Keep an eye on our [news](/news) section as Rockstar reveals more details in the months ahead.
