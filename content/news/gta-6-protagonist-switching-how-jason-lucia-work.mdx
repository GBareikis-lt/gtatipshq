## Two Characters, One Seamless Experience

Rockstar Games has been gradually pulling back the curtain on how GTA 6 plays, and a new feature in Dazed magazine's Autumn 2026 issue offers some of the most telling detail yet. Members of the development team spoke about what it actually feels like to jump between the game's two leads — Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos — as you move through Leonida.

The headline takeaway: character switching is designed to feel natural and situational, not like a menu option bolted onto the side of the game.

## Switching on the Fly

The example described by Rockstar is telling. Picture a highway chase: Jason is behind the wheel, weaving through traffic, while Lucia — riding shotgun — can be doing something as mundane as scrolling her in-game phone. The moment things escalate, the game lets you slide between them fluidly:

- **Control Jason** to navigate the road and dodge vehicles
- **Switch to Lucia** to handle combat and shoot at pursuers
- **Transition organically** without breaking the pace of the scene

It sounds less like GTA V's character wheel and more like the game dynamically responding to what each protagonist is best positioned to do at any given moment.

## Couple or Solo — Your Call

Beyond the chaos, Rockstar confirmed that players aren't locked into running Jason and Lucia as a pair at all times. You can separate them and experience Leonida independently through each character's perspective, which suggests meaningful differences in how they interact with the world.

> After a job well done, the two can reportedly unwind together on a jet ski — a small detail that says a lot about the tone Rockstar is going for with this relationship.
