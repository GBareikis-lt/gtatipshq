## A Shorter Wait for PC Players This Time?

PC gamers have historically been the last in line when it comes to Rockstar releases. GTA 5 took 18 months to arrive on PC after its console debut, and Red Dead Redemption 2 kept PC players waiting nearly a year. With GTA 6 confirmed for a [November 19, 2026 console launch](/news), many PC fans were bracing for a similarly long gap — but a new report suggests that wait could be dramatically shorter.

According to industry sources speaking to GamesBeat, Rockstar Games is planning to bring GTA 6 to PC within the first three months of 2027. If accurate, that would mean a window of roughly four to five months between the console and PC releases — a significant shift in strategy for the studio.

## Why the Console-First Approach Still Makes Sense

Rockstar has not officially announced a PC version of GTA 6, so treat this as an unconfirmed rumour for now. That said, there are logical reasons for the staggered release:

- Launching on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S first lets Rockstar focus development resources and polish the experience for a fixed hardware target.
- A console head-start historically drives enormous early sales and controls the conversation around the game.
- A later PC release gives the team time to properly optimise for the wide variety of PC configurations.

## Take-Two's Growing Focus on PC

What lends some credibility to the shorter turnaround is Take-Two Interactive's own comments on the platform. CEO Strauss Zelnick has publicly noted that PC game sales have grown substantially for the publisher in recent years, signalling that the platform is no longer an afterthought.

> If the Q1 2027 window holds, PC players would be waiting months rather than years — a genuine improvement over Rockstar's past track record.
