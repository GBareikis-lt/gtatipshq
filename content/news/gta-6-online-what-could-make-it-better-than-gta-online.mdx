## The GTA Online Bar Is High — But Flawed

GTA Online became one of the most profitable live-service games ever made, but it has always carried baggage: clunky gunplay, aggressive monetisation, and a learning curve that alienated newcomers. With GTA 6 launching on November 19, 2026, the community is already debating what Rockstar needs to fix or reinvent for the online component.

A recent discussion on r/GTA6 captured a lot of what players are feeling, and the wishlist goes well beyond a visual upgrade.

## Gunplay Is the Biggest Talking Point

One of the most common complaints about GTA Online is that firefights feel loose and unsatisfying compared to dedicated shooters. Many fans point to Red Dead Redemption 2 as proof that Rockstar can deliver weighty, responsive gunplay — and they want that energy brought into GTA 6's online mode. Better hit feedback, more meaningful recoil, and guns that feel distinct from one another would go a long way.

## A Reputation System Could Change Everything

The in-game social media mechanic teased in GTA 6's trailers hints at some kind of reputation or fame system for the single-player story. If Rockstar extends something similar into online — where your character builds a reputation based on what you do and how you play — it could create a much more personalised and socially dynamic experience than GTA Online ever managed.

## Monetisation Remains the Elephant in the Room

No confirmed details exist yet on how GTA 6 Online will be monetised. Whether Rockstar keeps the shark card model, introduces a subscription, or bundles online access differently is still unknown. How they handle this will likely shape player reception more than any gameplay feature.
