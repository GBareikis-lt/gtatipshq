## No Disc, and a Ticking Clock

If you're picking up GTA 6 on PS5 in Japan, you already know the physical edition comes with a download code rather than a disc — that's been the case for some time. What buyers may not have clocked, however, is that this code carries an expiry date.

According to language spotted on Rockstar's website, Japanese players will have just **170 days** to redeem their voucher before it becomes worthless. Regional regulations are cited as the reason, though no further detail has been given.

## When Does the Clock Start?

Here's the part worth paying close attention to: the countdown appears to begin from GTA 6's **official release date of November 19, 2026** — not from whenever you personally buy the game. That means:

- Someone buying a copy at launch gets the full 170 days.
- Someone picking up a discounted copy six months later could find their code has already expired, or is close to it.
- Second-hand buyers are at the greatest risk of receiving a completely dead code.

> This is an unconfirmed reading of Rockstar's wording, so Japanese buyers should verify the exact terms before purchasing, especially through resellers.

## What This Means for Buyers

This situation is a practical reminder that "physical" doesn't always mean permanent when a code is involved. If you're in Japan — or importing — a few things to keep in mind:

- **Redeem promptly.** Don't sit on a sealed box assuming the code stays valid indefinitely.
- **Be cautious with second-hand copies.** Verify the code status before handing over cash.
- **Check Rockstar's regional pages** for the definitive redemption window as launch approaches.
