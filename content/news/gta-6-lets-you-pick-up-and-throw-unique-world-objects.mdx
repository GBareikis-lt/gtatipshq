## A More Interactive World Than Ever

A detail spotted in GTA 6's extended trailer footage has sparked discussion in the fan community: unique items in the world — things like basketballs — appear to have their own dedicated pick-up icons, and those icons disappear once the object is thrown. This suggests Rockstar is treating certain props not just as set dressing, but as actual interactable items the player can carry and use.

## Beyond the Minigame

Basketball has already been hinted at as a playable minigame in GTA 6, but what's interesting here is that the ball itself seems to function as a persistent, physical object in the world. According to the Reddit observation, you might be able to:

- Pick up a basketball and stash it in your vehicle's trunk
- Take it anywhere in Leonida and shoot hoops on your own terms
- Use it in unintended ways, like lobbing it at unsuspecting pedestrians

It's a small detail, but it hints at a sandbox layer that goes well beyond scripted activities.

## Dog Poop and the Chaos Factor

YouTuber TGG has reportedly mentioned that even dog poop can be picked up and thrown at NPCs — which, if accurate, is exactly the kind of absurd, player-driven chaos Rockstar has always quietly encouraged.

> Keep in mind these are unconfirmed observations and educated guesses from trailer analysis — nothing here is officially confirmed by Rockstar.

## What This Could Mean for Players

If Rockstar has genuinely built out a system of unique, carriable world objects each with their own icons and physics interactions, it would represent a meaningful step up in roleplaying and general mayhem potential. It lines up with the broader impression that GTA 6's version of Leonida is designed to reward curiosity.
