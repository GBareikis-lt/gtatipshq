## Console Sales Surging Ahead of GTA 6

With GTA 6 still a couple of months away from its [November 19, 2026 release date](/news/release), shoppers in the UK are already heading to stores and clicking 'buy' on new hardware. According to Eurogamer, citing retail data, both PS5 and Xbox Series consoles have seen sales climb by roughly 30% — a notable uptick that analysts are linking directly to anticipation surrounding Rockstar's upcoming title.

It is not unusual for a major exclusive or multiplatform blockbuster to nudge hardware numbers upward, but a lift of this size, this far out from launch day, speaks to just how much excitement GTA 6 is generating.

## Why Buyers Are Upgrading Now

A few factors are likely driving people to pick up new hardware ahead of the game's arrival:

- **Future-proofing**: Players want to be ready on day one rather than scrambling closer to release.
- **Bundle deals**: Retailers often pair anticipated titles with console bundles, making the timing attractive.
- **Trade-in momentum**: Those on older hardware may be using the GTA 6 announcement window as the nudge they needed to finally upgrade.

> This is a UK-specific data snapshot, so the trend may not reflect global figures equally — though similar patterns are common in other major markets.

## What This Means for GTA 6 Players

A broader installed base heading into launch is generally good news for the community. More players at launch typically means healthier online lobbies, a more active player economy, and a stronger incentive for Rockstar to support [GTA 6 Online](/news/online) content over the long term.

If you are still deciding whether to upgrade before November, it is worth keeping an eye on retailer bundle announcements — history suggests deals tend to appear in the weeks immediately before a game of this scale ships.
