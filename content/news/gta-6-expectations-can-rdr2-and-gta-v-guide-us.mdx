## The Comparison Everyone Is Making

As GTA 6's [November 2026 release](/news) approaches, fans are naturally trying to set their expectations somewhere sensible. The most common approach doing the rounds in the community is to look at Rockstar's own back catalogue — specifically GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 — and use them as a rough blueprint for what's coming.

It sounds reasonable on the surface. Both games are Rockstar titles, both raised the bar significantly for open-world design, and RDR2 in particular still holds up as a benchmark for world detail and physics. If GTA 6 represents a similar leap forward from those titles, we could be in for something genuinely extraordinary.

## Why It's Not a Perfect Science

There are some real problems with leaning too hard on this method, though:

- **GTA V is over a decade old.** The gaming industry has transformed since 2013 — engine technology, player expectations, and hardware capabilities have all moved on dramatically.
- **RDR2 is a different genre.** Its slow-burn, simulation-heavy design suits a Western setting. GTA 6's Miami-inspired chaos will almost certainly demand a different design philosophy.
- **Rockstar has evolved as a studio.** Team size, tools, and ambitions have all grown. Past outputs are an imperfect indicator of what a much larger, more experienced team can deliver in 2026.

## What RDR2 Does Tell Us

That said, ignoring RDR2 entirely would be a mistake. It remains the clearest public evidence of what Rockstar prioritises: environmental storytelling, NPC behaviour, and tactile world interaction. Those values almost certainly carry over.

> The jump in quality from GTA V to RDR2 is real and measurable — it's reasonable to expect GTA 6 continues that upward trajectory, even if the exact results are impossible to predict.

## The Sensible Middle Ground

Using past Rockstar titles as a *floor* for quality rather than an exact template is probably the healthiest approach. Expect the attention to detail, expect ambitious world-building — but stay open to GTA 6 doing things neither predecessor ever attempted. Check back at [/news](/news) as we get closer to launch for any confirmed details that can sharpen those expectations.
