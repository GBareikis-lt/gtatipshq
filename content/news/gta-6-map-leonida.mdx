GTA 6 is set in **Leonida**, a fictional state inspired by Florida and anchored by a
modern **Vice City**. Early footage and official material point to a map that dwarfs
previous entries in both size and density.

## The regions of Leonida

While Rockstar hasn't published an official map, footage suggests several distinct
areas:

- **Vice City** — the neon metropolis at the heart of it all.
- **Beaches & keys** — coastal stretches and island chains.
- **Wetlands & swamps** — Everglades-style backcountry.
- **Suburbs & rural towns** — quieter areas between the action.

## Why scale matters

A bigger map only works if it's worth exploring. The signs are promising: denser
crowds, more interiors, and dynamic events that make each district feel alive.
