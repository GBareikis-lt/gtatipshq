## Record-Breaking Before It Even Launches

With GTA 6 still months away from its [November 19, 2026 release date](/news), the game has already made financial history. According to data published by a video game market research firm and reported by Eurogamer, pre-order revenue for GTA 6 has surpassed **$260 million** — a figure analysts are describing as the strongest pre-order campaign ever recorded for a video game.

That is a staggering number, and it reflects just how much anticipation has built up around Rockstar's long-awaited open-world title.

## Not All the Numbers Floating Around Are Real

Despite the genuinely impressive data, the same analyst group was quick to push back on some of the more extreme figures circulating on social media and gaming forums. Certain claims about GTA 6's pre-order totals have been called "incorrect" and outright "absurd" by these researchers.

The takeaway: the real numbers are already historic — there is no need to exaggerate them. If you see eye-popping figures being shared without a credible source attached, treat them with healthy scepticism.

## What This Means for Players

From a fan perspective, the sheer scale of pre-order interest signals a few things worth keeping in mind:

- **Server demand at launch will be enormous.** GTA Online's rocky launch in 2013 is still talked about today. Rockstar will be aware of the pressure.
- **Retailer bonuses may still be available.** With pre-orders this active, some platforms and shops are likely still offering early-access incentives worth checking out.
- **Post-launch support should be substantial.** This level of upfront revenue gives Rockstar and Take-Two strong motivation to invest heavily in ongoing content.

> These are unconfirmed analyst estimates, not official figures from Rockstar or Take-Two. Treat them as informed projections rather than verified sales data.
