## Rockstar Brings Vice City to the Real World

GTA 6's fictional setting of Vice City is bleeding into reality in a big way. A 'Welcome to Vice City' sign has reportedly been installed on the roof of the Kaseya Center, the NBA arena in downtown Miami that serves as home to the Miami Heat. It's one of the first confirmed examples of physical, real-world GTA 6 marketing appearing in Miami — the very city that inspired Vice City.

The timing is notable: with the game's release set for **November 19, 2026**, Rockstar and Take-Two appear to be ramping up their promotional push in earnest.

## Sahara Arena: The In-Game Version

For those who want to see the fictional counterpart, the Kaseya Center maps directly to the **Sahara Arena** inside GTA 6's Vice City. You can find details about the location on the [GTA 6 map](/map). The real-world tie-in is a neat nod to just how closely Rockstar has modelled the game's world on Miami and its landmarks.

## Why This Matters

- Real-world location branding like this signals a serious marketing escalation ahead of the November launch.
- It reinforces the deliberate, city-specific connection Rockstar has built between Miami and Vice City.
- Fans visiting Miami now have an unmissable photo opportunity before release day.
