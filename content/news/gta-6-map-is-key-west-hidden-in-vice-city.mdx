## The Debate Dividing the GTA 6 Community

Ever since Rockstar released its extended gameplay preview for GTA 6, the community has been combing through every frame for clues about the game's full map. The latest hot-button topic: whether a location inspired by Key West — the iconic island city at the southern tip of Florida — is somewhere in Leonida.

Fans are split. Some point to visual details in the preview footage that suggest a chain of islands stretching south from the main landmass, consistent with the geography of the Florida Keys. Others argue the evidence is too thin and that Rockstar may have kept the map's southern edge deliberately vague.

## What the Evidence Suggests

Those in the "Key West is in" camp highlight a few points:

- Distant landmasses visible on the southern horizon in certain outdoor scenes
- Road geometry that appears to extend toward a causeway-style structure
- Rockstar's track record of faithfully mirroring real Florida landmarks throughout the Leonida setting

Sceptics, however, caution that horizon details in game previews can be misleading — decorative backdrops rather than accessible areas — and that no confirmed map outline has been officially released.

> **Remember:** No full official map for GTA 6 has been published by Rockstar. Everything here is community speculation based on preview footage.

## Why It Matters

Key West is a culturally distinctive place — think sunset festivals, historic architecture, and a very different vibe from Miami. If Rockstar has included an analogue, it could mean a meaningfully varied southern region on the [GTA 6 map](/map), adding atmosphere that feels distinct from the neon-soaked Vice City core.
