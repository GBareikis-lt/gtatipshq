## What the Leak Contains

A substantial data dump — reportedly close to 200GB — has been circulating online, apparently originating from GTA 5's source code. According to reporting by Eurogamer, the files suggest Rockstar Games at one point had serious plans to bring Liberty City into GTA 5 as a major expansion before ultimately shelving the idea. The same leak allegedly contains very early development material connected to GTA 6.

As always with leaks of this nature, nothing here is officially confirmed. Treat all details as unverified rumours until Rockstar or Take-Two addresses them publicly.

## The Liberty City DLC That Never Was

For years, fans speculated that a Liberty City return could come to GTA 5 — and this leak adds fuel to that long-running theory. If the files are genuine, they would indicate the project got far enough along to leave a meaningful footprint in the codebase before being cancelled. Possible reasons for scrapping it include:

- The studio shifting full focus toward GTA 6 development
- Technical challenges porting the older city into the RAGE engine's newer iterations
- Business decisions around GTA Online's live-service roadmap

## Early GTA 6 Glimpses

Perhaps more intriguing to fans looking ahead is the reported presence of early GTA 6 work within the same leak. "Early" is doing a lot of heavy lifting here — pre-production assets and placeholder files rarely resemble a finished product, so temper expectations accordingly. These fragments are unlikely to reveal anything meaningful about the game launching on **November 19, 2026**.
