## A Series First: Radio Without a Car

Ever since the early days of Grand Theft Auto, tuning into the in-game radio has meant sitting behind the wheel of a vehicle. GTA 6 is set to change that in a pretty meaningful way. For the first time in the series, players will be able to listen to radio stations while on foot, thanks to headphones and earbuds connected to their character's smartphone.

This small but satisfying quality-of-life addition means you no longer have to jump into a car just to catch your favourite in-game station. Whether you're wandering through Leonida's streets or waiting to meet a contact, the music keeps playing.

## On-Demand Podcasts Are Joining the Lineup

Alongside the portable radio feature, GTA 6 is also introducing something entirely new to the franchise: on-demand podcasts. Rather than tuning into a live-style broadcast, players will be able to queue up podcast episodes and listen at their own pace.

Given GTA's long tradition of brilliantly written radio content — from talk shows to fictional adverts — this feels like a natural evolution. True crime podcasts, in a game where you're committing crimes, seem like an obvious and very on-brand addition.

## What This Means for the Overall Experience

Taken together, these audio features suggest Rockstar is investing heavily in making the world of GTA 6 feel more alive and immersive beyond just driving around:

- Radio companions you on foot, not just in vehicles
- Podcast content gives players something to dig into at their own pace
- Smartphone integration ties the feature into the broader in-game tech ecosystem
