## Fans Are Spending Big Before Launch

With GTA 6 arriving on **November 19, 2026**, pre-order momentum is already telling an interesting story. Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick has described the game's pre-order numbers as "exceptional" — and the breakdown between editions is raising eyebrows across the industry.

According to Zelnick, the majority of players on PS5 are opting for the pricier **Ultimate Edition at $100** rather than the standard version. That's a notable trend, suggesting fans are willing to invest more up front for whatever additional content or perks the premium tier includes.

## Why This Isn't Entirely Surprising

There are a few reasons this split makes sense:

- GTA 6 is arguably the most anticipated game in a generation, and dedicated fans tend to go all-in.
- Ultimate or deluxe editions often bundle in early access, bonus in-game currency, or cosmetic content — things the GTA Online crowd values highly.
- After more than a decade of waiting, many players likely see the extra cost as worth it for day-one bragging rights.

> Keep in mind these are unverified internal figures shared by a company executive — take the framing with appropriate skepticism, as executives naturally present data in a positive light.

## What It Means for the Community

Strong pre-order numbers across both editions point to a healthy launch window for GTA 6. A larger day-one player base typically means smoother GTA Online matchmaking and a more active community from the start — good news if multiplayer is your focus.
