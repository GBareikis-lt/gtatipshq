## Vice City Comes to Life

With just over a month to go before GTA 6 hits shelves on **November 19, 2026**, Rockstar Games and parent company Take-Two are making sure nobody forgets it. A bold real-world marketing campaign has begun rolling through Miami, Florida — the city that inspired the game's iconic Vice City setting — and it's already turning heads.

According to reports from Push Square, in-game vehicles have been spotted on actual Miami streets as part of the stunt, blurring the line between the fictional world and the real one in a way that feels very on-brand for a game of this scale.

## Why Miami Makes Perfect Sense

GTA 6's setting draws heavily from Miami's culture, architecture, and atmosphere, so staging a marketing campaign there isn't just clever — it's thematic. Seeing cars that fans recognise from trailers and screenshots cruising real boulevards adds a layer of excitement that a TV spot simply can't match.

- The campaign reinforces Vice City's real-world roots
- It builds organic social buzz as bystanders film and share encounters
- It signals just how much confidence Take-Two has in the title

> This kind of large-scale, location-specific marketing is typically reserved for the biggest entertainment releases of any given year — GTA 6 clearly qualifies.
