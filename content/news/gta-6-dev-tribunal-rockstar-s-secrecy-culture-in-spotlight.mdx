## The Tribunal Gets Under Way

A long-running employment dispute involving Rockstar Games has reached its final stage, with a tribunal hearing now underway in the UK. The case centres on the dismissal of 31 Rockstar North employees — the Edinburgh-based studio behind GTA 6 — which occurred roughly a year ago. Both Rockstar and the Independent Workers Union of Great Britain (IWGB), which is representing the affected workers, have delivered combative opening statements to kick things off.

## What Each Side Is Arguing

Rockstar's position, as reflected in the reported opening remarks, is that the company felt compelled to act and that doing nothing would have been an unacceptable risk. The IWGB, on the other hand, is pushing back hard, framing the firings as unjust and pointing to what it describes as a deeply entrenched culture of secrecy within Rockstar — one that, the union argues, made it difficult for workers to organise or challenge decisions.

Key themes emerging from the opening day include:

- The extent to which Rockstar's internal confidentiality practices affected the dismissed employees
- Whether the dismissals were handled fairly under UK employment law
- The role the IWGB played in representing workers at one of gaming's most secretive studios

## Why This Matters for GTA 6

With GTA 6 scheduled to launch on **19 November 2026**, Rockstar North remains a critical part of the development pipeline. Any significant disruption to studio culture or staffing could carry real implications for the game's final stretch of production, though neither side has suggested delays are on the table.
