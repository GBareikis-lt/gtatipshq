## A Smarter Wanted System for GTA 6

Rockstar Games has revealed that GTA 6 will go well beyond the classic Wanted star system seen in previous entries. Confirmed in an interview with IGN, Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson explained that the game introduces a **Criminal Profile system** — a dynamic mechanic that tracks just how violently players behave during crimes and police chases.

The traditional Wanted system does return: get spotted breaking the law and the police will come after you. But how you respond to that heat is now recorded and remembered.

## How the Criminal Profile System Works

While eluding police, Jason and Lucia have options that go beyond simply outrunning cops. Players can:

- Switch vehicles and change clothing to throw off pursuit
- Split up and flee in separate directions
- Avoid escalating confrontations — no shooting, no additional violence

According to Nelson, players who commit a crime and slip away quietly, without opening fire or ramping up the chaos, will be treated differently by the system than those who leave a trail of destruction behind them.

> "If you hit and run, commit a crime and run, get into trouble and move on before the cops get there, and you don't start shooting people, don't start escalating... but if you don't, you're gene" — Rob Nelson, Rockstar North

## A System Built Around the Story

Rockstar's stated goal is for the Criminal Profile system to reinforce the game's central narrative — a story about a couple on the run. Nelson noted the team deliberately designed gameplay systems to support that storytelling wherever possible. The parallel to Red Dead Redemption 2's Honor system is clear: your choices shape not just the immediate moment, but potentially how the world perceives and reacts to you over time.
