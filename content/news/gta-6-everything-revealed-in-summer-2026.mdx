## The Biggest GTA 6 Summer on Record

Summer 2026 turned out to be a landmark season for GTA 6 fans. Between June and September, Rockstar and Take-Two kept up a steady drumbeat of reveals that answered some of the community's biggest outstanding questions — and raised plenty of new ones ahead of the [November 19, 2026 launch](/news).

Here's a rundown of everything that dropped before the season officially closed on September 21.

## Marketing and Media Highlights

- **Cover art and pricing** were finally confirmed, giving buyers a clear picture of what to expect at checkout.
- **Pre-load date** was set for November 12 — a full week before release — so players can be ready to jump in at launch.
- **Ultimate Edition and pre-order bonus details** arrived alongside 63 screenshots and two video clips showing off the game in action.
- **27-minute Extended Look** gameplay video gave the clearest window yet into Vice City, complete with new artwork.
- **Dazed Magazine** ran an exclusive Rockstar interview paired with seven new screenshots and a fresh artwork reveal.
- A **PlayStation commercial** highlighted the game with live-action Miami footage, and a branded PS5 welcome page went live for pre-orders alongside a "Plays Best on PS5" tagline.
- Two **limited-edition DualSense controllers** were announced with a dedicated trailer.

## Vice City Goes Real-World

The marketing push extended well beyond screens. A **"I ❤️ Vice City" billboard** appeared in Los Angeles the day before the Extended Look, courtesy of Netflix. Spotify-sponsored billboards featuring six new artworks from various artists went up in Miami, LA, and New York City. An early look at a real **"Welcome to Vice City VI" sign** — planned for the roof of Miami's Kaseya Center — made the rounds online.

> Miami Beach officials formally approved GTA 6 marketing activity in the city, with signage and billboards confirmed to begin rolling out from October 15.

## What's Next

With pre-orders open, pre-load a week out, and a full marketing campaign about to hit the streets, the road to November 19 is well and truly underway. Keep an eye on our [news section](/news) for any further reveals between now and launch.
