## Rockstar Drops an Official GTA 6 Album

If the in-game radio isn't enough, Rockstar Games has teamed up with Atlantic Records to release a proper GTA 6 album — and you can already lock in a copy ahead of launch. The announcement confirms several tracks are included, though the full tracklist hasn't been revealed just yet.

## How to Pre-Order

Pre-orders are live now through the official GTA 6 site via Atlantic Records. Here's what's available:

- **CD** – standard physical edition
- **Vinyl (Standard)** – classic pressing for collectors
- **Vinyl (Limited Edition)** – a more exclusive variant for the dedicated fans
- **Digital pre-save** – available on Apple Music, Spotify, and other major streaming services

> If you're a vinyl collector or just a big fan of GTA's legendary music culture, the limited edition pressing is probably worth grabbing sooner rather than later.

## Why It Matters

GTA has always treated its soundtrack as a core part of the experience — from Vice City's neon-soaked radio stations to GTA V's genre-spanning roster. A standalone album release suggests Rockstar is putting real weight behind GTA 6's music identity, which bodes well for the in-game radio and overall atmosphere when the game arrives on **November 19, 2026**.
