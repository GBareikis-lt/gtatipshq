## The Hacker Behind the Infamous 2022 Leak

In late 2022, Rockstar Games suffered one of the most significant leaks in gaming history when footage and assets from the then-unannounced GTA 6 were posted online. The person responsible was Arion Kurtaj, a member of the prolific Lapsus$ hacking collective. At the time, the leak gave the world its first real look at the game well over a year before Rockstar officially revealed it.

## What Happened at Trial

Kurtaj was found criminally responsible for the Rockstar breach and other cyberattacks. Because he was deemed a continued risk — reportedly unwilling or unable to stop hacking — a UK court handed him an indefinite hospital order in 2023 rather than a fixed custodial sentence. That type of order keeps someone detained until medical authorities are satisfied they no longer pose a danger.

## Where Things Stand Now

According to reporting by PC Gamer, Kurtaj has since been discharged from the hospital facility and is now awaiting a retrial. The legal process is ongoing, and no new verdict has been reached at this stage.

> This is an active legal matter. Details could change significantly as the retrial progresses.

## What This Means for GTA 6 Fans

For most players, this story is largely background noise at this point. The leaked footage is years old, and Rockstar has since officially revealed the game — still on track for its **November 19, 2026** release. If anything, the case serves as a reminder of the extraordinary circumstances surrounding GTA 6's early exposure.
