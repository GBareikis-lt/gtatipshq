## Where the Rumour Started

Earlier this week, The Verge reported on an internal Xbox all-hands meeting in which CEO Asha Sharma allegedly hinted that the platform holder was planning something around GTA 6 that "no other platform holder is doing." That phrasing was vague enough to send the internet into a spin, with many readers jumping to the conclusion that Xbox had quietly locked down exclusive game-streaming rights for Rockstar's most anticipated release.

For PC players in particular, the idea was tantalising. GTA 6 is currently confirmed only for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S at launch on November 19, 2026, with no PC release window announced. If Xbox Cloud Gaming could stream the title exclusively, it would effectively offer a back-door route to playing it on a laptop or desktop from day one.

## Xbox Pours Cold Water on the Story

Xbox moved quickly to shut the speculation down. A spokesperson confirmed to Eurogamer that the company has **not** acquired any exclusive streaming rights for GTA 6. Whatever Sharma's comments were referring to internally, they were not signalling a deal that would lock the title's cloud streaming to one platform.

> This means PC players are still in the same position they were before: waiting on Rockstar to announce a PC version separately.

## What This Means for GTA 6 Players

- **Console players** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S remain the confirmed day-one audience.
- **PC players** have no confirmed path to the game at launch — cloud or otherwise.
- Xbox Cloud Gaming and PlayStation's own streaming services could still carry the title in the future, just without any exclusivity attached.
