## What's Being Claimed

With GTA 6's [release date](/news/release) locked in for November 19, 2026, the last thing Rockstar needs is a major leak — and a figure going by the name CyberLeek is reportedly threatening exactly that. According to a report from Eurogamer, this leaker has made noise about putting out a complete, playable build of the game, not just screenshots or video clips, but something you could theoretically run from start to finish.

It is worth stressing that this is an unverified claim. No build has surfaced publicly as of this writing, and threats of this kind have historically not always followed through.

## Why This Is a Big Deal

Most leaks in the gaming world amount to concept art, early footage, or internal documents. A full playable build would be a different category entirely. If real, it could mean:

- Significant story spoilers ahead of launch
- Potential exposure of unfinished or cut content
- Legal action from Rockstar's parent company Take-Two Interactive, which has pursued leakers aggressively in the past

> Take-Two successfully pursued legal remedies against leakers following the 2022 GTA 6 source code breach, so any attempt to distribute a playable build would carry serious risk for the person responsible.

## What You Should Do

Our advice is straightforward: if a so-called playable build does surface online, avoid it. Beyond the obvious legal grey areas around downloading unreleased commercial software, early builds are often unstable and may contain malware distributed alongside them by bad actors.
