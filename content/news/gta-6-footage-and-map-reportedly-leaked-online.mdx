## What Surfaced Online

A wave of apparent GTA 6 material began circulating in mid-August 2026, reportedly including in-game footage and map details. The clips spread quickly before being pulled down across multiple platforms. A leaker operating under the handle **Cyberleek** has claimed responsibility for the disclosures.

One clip — already difficult to find — reportedly shows co-protagonist Jason at home, followed by a brief basketball minigame sequence that includes an on-screen tutorial explaining the controls. Viewers also noted what appeared to be a revised HUD layout compared to previous trailers.

## Rockstar's Response

Rockstar has not issued a public statement about the content of the leak, but the studio has reportedly filed DMCA takedown requests targeting the circulating material. That legal action is widely interpreted as a signal that at least some of the footage is authentic, though nothing has been officially confirmed. Treat all leaked material as **unverified** until Rockstar says otherwise.

## The Leaker's "Commandments"

Cyberleek accompanied the release with a list of demands directed at Rockstar and other major publishers:

- **No digital pre-orders** for unreleased games
- **No misleading single-player DLC** practices
- **Preservation of single-player content** post-launch

The statement framed these as a minimum standard and warned that further leaks could follow if the demands go unmet. Whether this is a genuine grievance campaign or pure provocation remains unclear.

> **Reminder:** GTA 6 is officially set for release on **November 19, 2026**. Rockstar has made no changes to that date in response to this situation.
