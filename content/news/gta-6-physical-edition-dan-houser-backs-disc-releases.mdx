## Houser Weighs In on Physical vs Digital Debate

Rockstar Games co-founder Dan Houser has shared his view on the ongoing physical versus digital media debate, coming down firmly on the side of player choice. Speaking to IGN, Houser argued that if consumers want physical copies of their games, then the industry has a responsibility to keep making them available.

While Houser departed Rockstar back in 2020 and is no longer involved in day-to-day decisions at the studio, his perspective carries weight given his role in shaping the franchise that GTA 6 will continue.

## What This Could Mean for GTA 6

Rockstar and parent company Take-Two have not yet made a formal announcement about the specific editions planned for GTA 6 ahead of its **November 19, 2026** release. However, Houser's comments reflect a broader sentiment that is worth noting for fans who prefer owning a disc:

- Physical releases remain commercially important, especially for major blockbuster titles
- A game of GTA 6's scale would almost certainly see a standard physical edition at launch
- Collector's or special physical editions are a real possibility, as seen with past Rockstar titles
- The shift toward digital-only consoles adds pressure on publishers to clearly confirm disc availability

> Keep in mind that Houser's remarks are his personal opinion and do not represent an official Take-Two or Rockstar statement about GTA 6's release formats.

## Why It Matters

With Sony and Microsoft continuing to push digital storefronts, many players — particularly those in regions with slower internet infrastructure or those who resell games — still rely heavily on physical media. Any signal that major publishers support disc releases is welcome news for that audience.
