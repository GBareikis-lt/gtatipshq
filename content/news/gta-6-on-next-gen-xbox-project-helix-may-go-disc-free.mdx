## Next-Gen Xbox Reportedly Going All-Digital

According to separate reports from Windows Central's Jez Corden and The Verge, Microsoft's next-generation console — codenamed **Project Helix** — may arrive without a disc drive. This follows a similar trend on the PlayStation side, where Sony has reportedly signalled that disc support could be dropped with the PS6. Neither detail is officially confirmed, so treat both as credible rumours for now.

The two reports don't fully agree: Corden states the decision is made, while The Verge suggests Microsoft is still weighing its options. What both outlets do align on is the direction of travel — physical media is clearly on the way out across the industry.

## Disc-to-Digital Could Soften the Blow

The Verge's report also mentions a **disc-to-digital conversion feature** currently being tested internally at Microsoft. The idea is straightforward: insert a compatible Xbox One or Xbox Series X|S disc into a drive (presumably an external one), and the system links a digital licence to your Microsoft account. Notably, older Xbox 360 and original Xbox discs would not be supported under this scheme.

This matters for players who have built up large physical libraries — converting those games to digital would make the jump to an all-digital console less painful.

## What This Means for GTA 6 Players

With GTA 6 set to launch on **November 19, 2026**, it will almost certainly arrive before Project Helix hits shelves. That said, if you're planning to eventually move to next-gen Xbox hardware, it's worth thinking ahead:

- **Buy digital** from day one if you want guaranteed forward compatibility on an all-digital console.
- **Physical buyers** may be able to use the disc-to-digital feature — assuming it launches and GTA 6 discs are supported.
- Keep an eye on [GTA 6 hardware news](/news) as the next-gen console landscape becomes clearer.

> Neither Project Helix's specs nor its release window have been officially announced. Everything here is based on unconfirmed reporting.
