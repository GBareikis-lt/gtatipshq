## Take-Two Plans an Aggressive GTA 6 Campaign

Even the most recognisable brands in the world still need to earn their audience — that is the core message Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick delivered in a recent interview with GamesBeat. Speaking about GTA 6's marketing strategy ahead of its November 2026 release, Zelnick pointed to the runaway success of the 2023 Barbie movie as the model he wants to follow.

His argument is straightforward: awareness and enthusiasm are two very different things. People already know what GTA is. The job of a marketing campaign is not simply to remind them the game exists, but to make them genuinely excited about what is new and why they need to experience it on day one.

## Why Even a Beloved Franchise Needs a Big Push

Zelnick's Barbie comparison is an interesting one. That film was built on one of the most recognisable toy brands in history, yet Warner Bros. still invested heavily in a marketing blitz that was, as Zelnick put it, "really everywhere." The result was a cultural moment that drove enormous box-office returns.

For GTA 6, Take-Two appears to be thinking along similar lines:

- **Awareness alone is not enough** — fans need a reason to be actively excited.
- **Broad entertainment properties require broad campaigns** — Zelnick described GTA 6 as falling into the "broad-based entertainment" category where significant marketing spend is not optional.
- **The goal is enthusiasm** — converting existing goodwill into day-one momentum.
