## Rockstar Unveils an Official GTA 6 Soundtrack Album

Rockstar Games has partnered with Atlantic Records to release **GTA 6: The Album**, a 34-song collection dropping alongside the game on **November 19, 2026**. The lineup spans a wide range of genres and artists, reflecting the kind of eclectic radio culture the GTA series has always celebrated.

Six debut singles are already available to stream right now:

- **Yung Lean** – "That's It" (feat. Future & Metro Boomin)
- **Travis Scott** – "RHYNO" (prod. by Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo)
- **CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred again.. & Etienne de Crécy** – "Sexy Magic"
- **Morgan Wallen** – "Last Thing You Need"
- **Rauw Alejandro** – "Macacoa 2000"
- **Keith Richards** – "Bright Lights, Big City"

## Physical Media — For the Music, at Least

In a bit of an ironic twist, GTA 6 itself is a digital-only release, but the soundtrack will be available on **vinyl and CD** at retail stores. For collectors and music fans, that makes the album the only physical version of anything GTA 6-branded you'll be able to own on launch day.

Beyond these original compositions, the game will also feature a broad selection of licensed tracks across its in-game radio stations, which has long been one of the franchise's most beloved features.

> The combination of original recordings from artists like Keith Richards and Travis Scott alongside classic licensed music suggests GTA 6's radio lineup could be one of the most ambitious in series history.

## What This Means for Players

A strong original soundtrack signals that Rockstar has invested heavily in the game's audio identity — not just curating existing songs but commissioning new ones. If these six singles are any indication of the overall quality and variety, the in-game radio experience should be a real highlight when GTA 6 launches.
