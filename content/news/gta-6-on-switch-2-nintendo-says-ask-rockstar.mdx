## Nintendo Passes the Buck on Switch 2 Question

When Nintendo of America president Devon Pritchard was asked by Variety whether GTA 6 would be coming to Switch 2, she gave fans precisely zero to work with — in the best possible way. Rather than shutting the door entirely, she pointed the question back to Take-Two and Rockstar, saying the decision is theirs to announce. That careful non-denial is enough to keep speculation alive heading into GTA 6's November 19, 2026 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## What We Actually Know

- GTA 6 is **confirmed only for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S** at launch. No other platforms have been announced.
- A PC version is widely expected based on Rockstar's history, but remains unconfirmed.
- Back in May 2025, reliable industry insider NateTheHate reported that Rockstar had run technical feasibility tests for Switch 2. He was quick to stress that such tests are routine and do not signal a green-lit port.
- Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has said the company sees value in the Switch 2 platform and could bring titles to it — but explicitly noted not every game in its catalogue would make the jump.

> **Bottom line:** Rockstar testing Switch 2 compatibility is standard practice, not a confirmation. Until Take-Two speaks directly, a Switch 2 version of GTA 6 remains firmly in rumour territory.

## Why It Matters

Switch 2 has built a reputation for punching above its weight technically, and a portable version of GTA 6 would be a massive commercial win for Nintendo. That said, the sheer scale of GTA 6's open world makes a faithful port a significant engineering challenge.
