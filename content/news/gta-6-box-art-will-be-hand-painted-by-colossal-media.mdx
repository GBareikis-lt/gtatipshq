## A 22-Year Creative Partnership

According to a post circulating on r/GTA6, Colossal Media — the Brooklyn-based hand-painted advertising company — is once again working with Rockstar Games to produce the official cover art for GTA 6. If accurate, this continues a collaboration that stretches back over two decades, with Rockstar reportedly being Colossal Media's very first client when the sign-painting studio launched.

It's worth noting this comes from a Reddit post rather than an official announcement, so treat it as an unconfirmed rumour for now.

## Why This Is a Big Deal

Colossal Media has built a reputation for large-scale, hand-painted murals and artwork at a time when most advertising has gone fully digital. Their involvement with Rockstar across multiple GTA titles gives the box art a distinctive, crafted quality that sets it apart from standard game covers. A few reasons fans are excited:

- Hand-painted artwork carries a tactile, artistic weight that digital renders rarely match
- The long history between both companies suggests a deep creative trust
- GTA titles have always leaned into bold, stylised visual identity — this fits that tradition perfectly

## What to Expect for GTA 6

GTA 6 is currently scheduled to launch on **November 19, 2026**, and official marketing is expected to ramp up significantly as that date approaches. Box art reveals typically arrive a few months before launch, so an official unveiling could come relatively soon.
