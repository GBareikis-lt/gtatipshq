## A New Mechanic Causing a Stir

One of the more divisive details to emerge about GTA 6 is the apparent inclusion of a vehicle refuelling mechanic — meaning players will need to stop and fill up their tanks during play. It's the kind of realism-focused addition that tends to split opinion sharply in the GTA community.

## How Long Does It Actually Take?

According to recent reports, the refuelling animation runs for roughly **10 seconds** in real time. That's not an enormous chunk out of a session, but in a game where momentum and freedom are central, even a brief pause can feel intrusive to some players.

Rockstar co-studio head Jess Harwood pushed back on the criticism, reportedly framing 10 seconds as "pretty fast for a fill-up" — essentially arguing that the mechanic reflects a grounded, believable world without being a major inconvenience.

## What This Means for Players

- **Strategic awareness** will matter more — keeping an eye on your fuel gauge before long drives or missions could become second nature.
- **Gas stations** on the [map](/map) are likely to take on a more meaningful role than in previous GTA titles.
- Players chasing efficiency might want to keep a vehicle well-fuelled before heading into high-stakes situations.

> Whether you see 10 seconds as a minor immersion detail or an annoying interruption will probably depend on your playstyle — but it's clear Rockstar views it as a deliberate design choice rather than an oversight.

## The Bigger Picture

GTA 6 is shaping up to be significantly more simulation-leaning than its predecessors. Refuelling joins a growing list of mechanics suggesting Rockstar wants the world to feel lived-in and reactive. Whether the community fully warms to it remains to be seen when the game launches on **November 19, 2026**.
