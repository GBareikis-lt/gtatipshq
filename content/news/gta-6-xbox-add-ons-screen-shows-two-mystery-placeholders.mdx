## Two Unnamed Slots Spotted on Xbox

A Reddit user browsing GTA 6's manage game and add-ons page on Xbox noticed something interesting: two placeholder entries sitting alongside the already-known Ultimate Edition Upgrade and the Vintage Vice City Pack. The slots are labelled simply **"Extra"** and **"Story"**, with no pricing, artwork, or release information attached to either.

This is an unconfirmed community observation, so treat it with appropriate scepticism — placeholder labels on digital storefronts can mean many things, and Rockstar has not commented.

## What Could They Mean?

Speculation is naturally running hot, but here are the most plausible interpretations:

- **"Story"** could point toward a single-player story expansion — something the GTA community has been hoping for since the days of GTA 4's *The Ballad of Gay Tony*.
- **"Extra"** is vaguer; it might cover a cosmetic or content bundle, additional vehicles, or even a mode that doesn't fit neatly into the main game.
- Both could simply be internal back-end placeholders that will never surface publicly — platform storefronts routinely reserve slots for content that gets cancelled or restructured.

> **Remember:** placeholder entries are not confirmed products. Until Rockstar makes an official announcement, these remain unverified rumours.

## Why It Still Matters

Even if the labels turn out to be nothing, the fact that a "Story" slot exists alongside a separate "Extra" slot suggests Rockstar may be planning to sell post-launch content in distinct tiers rather than one catch-all season pass. That would be a meaningful shift in how GTA's paid add-ons are structured.
