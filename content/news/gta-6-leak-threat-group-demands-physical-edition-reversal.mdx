## A New Threat Aimed at Rockstar

With GTA 6 still months away from its [November 19, 2026 release](/news), a group identifying itself as 'Cyberleek' has issued what it claims is its most serious threat yet: release a full GTA 6 build to the public unless certain demands around physical game distribution are met. The story was first reported by VideoGamesChronicle, a generally reliable outlet in the gaming space.

It is important to stress that **none of this has been verified**. We do not know whether Cyberleek actually possesses a full or functional build of GTA 6, nor has Rockstar or Take-Two Interactive commented publicly on the threat at the time of writing.

## What Are the Demands?

According to VGC, the group's core grievance centres on what it describes as plans to limit or reverse the physical release of GTA 6 — though the specific details of those alleged plans have not been confirmed by any official source. The threat appears designed to pressure publishers rather than simply expose the game.

- The group is demanding that physical release plans be changed
- No deadline has been publicly confirmed
- No proof of a legitimate build has been provided

## What This Means for Players

> Until Rockstar or Take-Two address this directly, treat any claimed "leaked builds" circulating online with extreme caution — downloading unofficial game files is risky and potentially illegal.

Extortion-style leak threats are not new to the games industry, and they rarely result in actual, playable leaks of major titles. Rockstar has historically been aggressive in pursuing legal action against those who leak proprietary materials.

We will continue to monitor the situation and update our [news section](/news) if any official response emerges. For now, the safest approach is to wait for legitimate information from Rockstar directly.
