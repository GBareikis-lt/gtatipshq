## Another Leak Hits the Internet

A third video claiming to show genuine Grand Theft Auto 6 gameplay has surfaced online, continuing what appears to be a coordinated series of leaks attributed to a group calling itself CyberLeek. All three videos reportedly carry the same watermark, suggesting a single source is behind the releases. Rockstar Games is actively working to have the footage pulled from platforms where it has appeared.

## What We Know So Far

- This is the third video in a short window of time, all connected to the CyberLeek name
- The footage is unverified, though it is described by Eurogamer — a reputable outlet — as "seemingly real"
- Rockstar's swift takedown efforts suggest the studio considers the material sensitive and potentially authentic
- The origin of the leak and how the group obtained the footage remain unknown

> **Heads-up:** We are not linking to or embedding any of the leaked footage out of respect for Rockstar's intellectual property and to avoid spoilers ahead of the game's November 19, 2026 release.

## Why This Matters

Large-scale pre-release leaks can affect the experience for players who want to go in fresh. If you are trying to stay spoiler-free, it is worth being cautious on social media and video platforms over the coming days, as clips may surface in unexpected places.

At the same time, it is worth keeping perspective: pre-release leaks rarely reflect the full, polished state of a finished game. Early footage can be outdated builds, missing final lighting, animation, and audio passes.
