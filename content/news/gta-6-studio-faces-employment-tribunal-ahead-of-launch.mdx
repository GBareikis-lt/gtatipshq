## What's Happening

With GTA 6's November 2026 release now only months away, Rockstar North finds itself fighting a legal battle on a very different front. The final hearing in an employment tribunal brought by 31 former Rockstar North staff members is set to begin this week, according to a report from Eurogamer. The case centres on workers who were let go during the game's lengthy development cycle.

## Background on the Case

The dismissed employees have reportedly pursued this tribunal over what they consider unfair treatment during their exits from the studio. Details of the specific claims have not been fully laid out in public filings available at the time of writing, but the case has been working its way through the UK employment tribunal system for some time. Key points to note:

- Thirty-one former Rockstar North staff are named in the action
- The hearing coincides with a particularly high-profile period for the studio, coming shortly after a Netflix-related GTA 6 reveal event
- Rockstar North is based in Edinburgh, Scotland, and falls under UK employment law

## Timing and Context

The timing is notable. Rockstar is in the final sprint toward one of the most anticipated game launches in history, and managing a public tribunal hearing simultaneously adds an unusual layer of scrutiny to the studio. Employment disputes in the games industry have become increasingly visible in recent years, with workers across multiple major studios pushing back on conditions and terminations.
