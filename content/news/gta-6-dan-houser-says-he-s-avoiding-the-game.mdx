## The Co-Creator Who Won't Watch

Dan Houser, who co-founded Rockstar Games and played a central role in shaping the Grand Theft Auto series, has revealed that he has deliberately avoided watching anything related to GTA 6 — and plans to keep it that way. Speaking publicly on the topic, Houser made clear this is a conscious personal choice rather than an oversight.

Houser departed Rockstar back in 2020 after more than two decades with the studio, and GTA 6 has been developed entirely without his involvement. It appears he prefers to keep a clean emotional distance from the project that the franchise has become.

## Why the Deliberate Distance?

While Houser didn't elaborate extensively, the sentiment is understandable for someone who poured so much creative energy into earlier entries in the series. Watching a beloved franchise evolve under new creative direction — even a talented one — is clearly something he'd rather not do. It's a deeply human response from someone who helped define what GTA means culturally.

> This isn't a controversy or a falling out — just one creator choosing to step back from a chapter of something he helped build.

## What This Means for GTA 6

In practical terms, Houser's absence from the project changes nothing for players. GTA 6 remains on track for its **November 19, 2026** release, and Rockstar has a deep bench of creative talent continuing to shape the game's world, story, and systems.

- The core Rockstar team has been developing GTA 6 for years
- Multiple trailers and reveals have already generated enormous excitement
- Houser's own post-Rockstar studio, Absurd Ventures, is pursuing separate projects
