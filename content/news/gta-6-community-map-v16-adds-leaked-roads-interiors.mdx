## Community Cartographers Keep Busy

The dedicated fan project behind the GTA 6 community map has dropped its sixteenth major update, and it's one of the most detailed revisions yet. Version 16 draws heavily on an August 18 map leak, using that data to sharpen coastlines, adjust the landmass outline, and refine urban boundaries across the fictional state of Leonida. Bear in mind this is an unofficial, fan-made effort — nothing here is sourced directly from Rockstar Games.

## What's New in V16

The changelog is substantial. Highlights include:

- **Roads and buildings** added across Vice City, Key Lento, Hamlet, Capri, Ambrosia, and several other locations, drawing from both the leak and the previously released Extended Look footage.
- **Elevation data** updated using a leaked heightmap, with scale corrections applied throughout.
- **Camera locations** from the Extended Look and leaks have been pinned to the map.
- **New interiors** — the Crosstown safehouse and Effluvia location are now represented.
- **Leonida Penitentiary** received a more accurate layout pass.
- **Alleys** around Vice Beach, Downtown, and Southside have been sketched in.
- The grid system has been standardised to **1 km × 1 km** squares, with the overall map canvas set to 21,000 × 20,000 units.
- Park and Industrial zone designations, rooftop helipads, and new road signage for confirmed routes have also been added.

> **Reminder:** This map is built from leaks and trailer analysis. Details may not reflect the final game when GTA 6 launches on **November 19, 2026**.
