## What the Twitch CEO Actually Said

In a Bloomberg interview, Twitch CEO Dan Clancy commented that his platform expects a significant boost in viewership when GTA 6 launches — but that an "even bigger" surge could come in 2027 "when they launch multiplayer." Clancy noted that Twitch has spent considerable time discussing plans with Rockstar, suggesting this isn't pure speculation on his part.

It's worth emphasising that Rockstar itself has made no announcement about a multiplayer component for GTA 6. Treat this as an informed rumour, not a confirmed roadmap.

## A Familiar Pattern

GTA 5 launched as a single-player game in September 2013, with GTA Online following a few weeks later. What Clancy is implying is a much wider gap this time around — potentially more than a year between the story mode release and any online counterpart.

GTA 6 is officially set to launch on **November 19, 2026**, and Rockstar has so far described it as a single-player experience. A 2027 online rollout would give the studio time to refine the multiplayer side based on how the base game performs.

## Why This Matters

- GTA Online generated billions of dollars for Take-Two and remains active more than a decade after launch.
- A delayed multiplayer release would keep the spotlight on the story mode longer, potentially reducing day-one server strain.
- Content creators and streamers would have renewed motivation to return to the game when online modes eventually drop.

> **Keep in mind:** Clancy is an outsider to Rockstar's internal schedule. His comments carry more weight than random guesswork, but they are still unconfirmed. Check back at [/news](/news) as Rockstar makes official announcements closer to launch.
