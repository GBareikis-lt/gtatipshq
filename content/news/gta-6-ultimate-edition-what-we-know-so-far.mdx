## Rockstar Confirms Two Editions for GTA 6

When GTA 6 launches on **November 19, 2026**, players will have at least two ways to buy in: a standard edition and a pricier "Ultimate Edition." Rockstar and Take-Two made the upgrade tier official this week, joining a long tradition of publishers offering premium bundles around major releases.

A community poll run by IGN suggested strong interest in the Ultimate Edition, with a notable share of respondents leaning toward the higher-priced package — though self-selecting online polls should always be taken with a grain of salt.

## What's Included in the Ultimate Edition?

Based on what has been revealed so far, the extras appear to be **purely cosmetic** — think bonus in-game items, outfits, or vehicle liveries rather than anything that changes how the game actually plays. That's an important distinction: neither edition appears to offer a gameplay advantage over the other.

Full details on every bonus item have not yet been fully confirmed, so treat the specifics as subject to change until Rockstar publishes an official breakdown.

> No pay-to-win mechanics have been spotted in either edition — both versions should deliver the same core GTA 6 experience.

## Which Edition Is Right for You?

Here's a quick way to think about it:

- **Standard Edition** — the full game, nothing missing from the core experience.
- **Ultimate Edition** — the full game plus cosmetic bonuses for players who want extra flair from day one.

If cosmetic items matter to you — especially early in GTA 6 Online where showing off is half the fun — the Ultimate Edition could be worth the premium. If you're primarily a story-mode player, the standard edition likely covers everything you need.
