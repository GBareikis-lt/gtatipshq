## A Massive Leap in Scale

Rockstar has officially confirmed that the GTA 6 map will be three times the size of Red Dead Redemption 2's playable world — itself one of the largest and most detailed open worlds ever built. That's a staggering benchmark, and it gives us a clearer picture of just how ambitious the studio's vision is for its return to Vice City ahead of the [November 19, 2026 release](/news).

The game is set in a fictional version of Miami and its wider surroundings, meaning players can expect dense urban streets alongside swamps, coastlines, and rural outskirts — all stitched together into one continuous open world.

## Detail, Not Just Size

Bigger doesn't always mean better, but Rockstar appears to be matching scale with an extraordinary level of craft. According to a New York Times report drawing on developer interviews, GTA 6 contains well over 600,000 animations. For context:

- **GTA 5** launched with roughly 55,000 animations
- **Red Dead Redemption 2** pushed that to around 300,000
- **GTA 6** more than doubles RDR2's total

Rockstar North director Aaron Garbut described an approach where virtually every interior space has been individually considered — right down to the photographs on a wall reflecting the actual people who live there, complete with accurate tattoos and personal details.

> "Every room in the game, somebody thought about this," Garbut said, highlighting a level of environmental storytelling that goes far beyond what most studios attempt.

## What This Means for Players

A map this size, packed with hand-crafted detail, suggests GTA 6 will reward exploration in ways previous entries simply couldn't. Whether you're chasing [story missions](/tips) or just cruising the coastline, there should be something genuinely new around almost every corner.
