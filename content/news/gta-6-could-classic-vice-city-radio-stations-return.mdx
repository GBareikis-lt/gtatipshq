## A Fan Favourite Feature Worth Revisiting

With GTA 6 heading back to a fictional version of Miami, the community is buzzing about whether Rockstar might bring back some of the legendary radio stations from the original Vice City. A recent discussion on r/GTA6 pointed out an interesting precedent: GTA V quietly resurrected two stations from GTA San Andreas — Radio Los Santos and West Coast Talk Radio — giving them modern makeovers for the Los Santos setting.

If Rockstar followed the same playbook in GTA 6, it would make a lot of sense to dust off at least one or two Vice City classics.

## Which Stations Could Make a Comeback?

Fans have been throwing out some obvious candidates:

- **Flash FM** – the slick pop and new wave station that defined Vice City's 1986 atmosphere
- **V-Rock** – the heavy metal staple with the unforgettable DJ Lazlow
- **Wildstyle** – a fan favourite that played hip-hop and rap, which could easily slot into a modern context with contemporary artists
- **Emotion 98.3** – beloved for its smooth soft-rock and memorable DJ Fernando Martinez

Updating these stations with present-day music in the same genre would be a smart nod to long-time fans while keeping the soundtrack fresh for new players.

## Nothing Confirmed Yet

> It's important to stress that this is purely community speculation at this point. Rockstar has not announced any details about GTA 6's radio lineup.

That said, the idea is far from far-fetched. Rockstar has always treated its in-game radio as a serious creative project, and returning to Vice City gives them a natural excuse to revisit the stations that made the original so memorable.

With the game set to launch on **November 19, 2026**, we'll likely learn more about the soundtrack closer to release. Keep an eye on our [news section](/news) for any official updates.
