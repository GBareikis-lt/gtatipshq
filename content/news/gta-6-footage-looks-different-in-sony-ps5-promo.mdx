## Sony's New PS5 Ad Puts GTA 6 Under the Microscope

Sony recently launched an "It Happens On PS5" promotional campaign, and the short video prominently features Grand Theft Auto VI alongside other high-profile titles. Even though the footage is pulled from previously released trailers, eagle-eyed fans have already spotted several small but notable differences worth talking about.

## What Fans Are Noticing

The community X account DriftRespawn flagged some of the more obvious changes when comparing the PS5 promo to the original Trailer 2 footage released in May 2025:

- **Letterboxing removed:** The iconic scene of Jason hanging off the back of a convertible while Lucia drives appears full-screen in the Sony ad, whereas Trailer 2 had the classic cinematic black bars at the top and bottom.
- **Brighter lighting:** The same scene appears noticeably lighter overall in the new promo.
- **Lucia's appearance:** Her eyebrows look slightly less defined, and her hair reads as a warmer brown tone — though this could simply be a side effect of the adjusted lighting rather than a deliberate character change.

> Keep in mind these are unconfirmed observations based on social media comparisons, and compression differences between video platforms can also affect how colours and details appear.

## What This Probably Means

Game development is a living process, and visual tweaks right up to release are completely normal. Character models, lighting systems, and aspect ratio choices can all shift as a project is refined. None of these differences confirm any major rework — they're more likely the result of ongoing polish as Rockstar moves toward the [confirmed November 19, 2026 launch](/news).

That said, the GTA 6 community has a well-earned reputation for forensic-level trailer analysis, and moments like this are part of what makes the wait interesting. We'll keep an eye out for any further official footage that might clarify what the final product looks like.
