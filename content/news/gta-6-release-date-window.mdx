The wait is almost over. Grand Theft Auto 6 is currently scheduled to launch on
**November 19, 2026**, arriving first on **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S**.
After years of teasers, leaks and a record-breaking first trailer, we finally have
a window worth counting down to — and our [live countdown](/) is ticking toward it
right now.

## What the date means

A late-2026 launch lines up neatly with Rockstar's holiday-season strategy for its
biggest releases. It also gives the studio room to polish what is shaping up to be
the most ambitious open world ever built.

> Release windows can still move. The moment Rockstar confirms any change, we update
> the countdown and this page automatically.

## What to expect at launch

- **Consoles first.** PS5 and Xbox Series X|S are confirmed. A PC version has not
  been dated, but history suggests it follows later.
- **A massive day-one world.** Leonida and a modern Vice City headline the map.
- **Two protagonists.** Lucia and Jason anchor the story from the very first mission.
