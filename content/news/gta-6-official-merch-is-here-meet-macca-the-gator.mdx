## Rockstar Opens the Official GTA 6 Store

With the November 19, 2026 launch of GTA 6 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S drawing closer, Rockstar has rolled out an official merchandise collection through the Rockstar Store. The lineup covers a solid range of everyday items — graphic t-shirts, branded hats in black, pink, and blue, a GTA 6 Zippo lighter, canvas tote bags, and vinyl stickers.

## Say Hello to Macca the Gator

The standout piece in the collection is a t-shirt built around a character called **Macca the Gator** — a sunglasses-wearing alligator relaxing with a cocktail in hand. According to Rockstar, Macca is the star of a fictional in-universe TV show of the same name. Beyond that, details are sparse, but the character is clearly positioned as a fun piece of Vice City world-building — the kind of quirky, sun-soaked Floridian imagery that has always been central to the GTA aesthetic.

> Keep an eye on Rockstar's official channels for more on the Macca the Gator show — it could hint at content you'll actually encounter inside the game.

## A Miami Heat Collab Too

Rockstar also teamed up with the NBA's Miami Heat on a separate **Vice City Collection** of co-branded gear, a pairing that makes obvious thematic sense given the game's Florida setting. That range is available to purchase now alongside the main merch drop.

## A Few Things Worth Knowing Before You Buy

- The t-shirts are **machine washable cold**, but Rockstar specifically advises line-drying rather than tumble-drying.
- The full range is available now at the Rockstar Store.
- GTA 6 launches **November 19, 2026** for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
