## Midnight Everywhere — That's the Plan

Rockstar has confirmed that GTA 6 will go live at midnight local time in most countries on **November 19, 2026**. That's a rolling worldwide launch rather than a single simultaneous drop — and it has an interesting side effect for savvy players.

## The New Zealand Head-Start

Because New Zealand sits well ahead of most of the world on the clock, its midnight arrives hours before players in Europe or the Americas even reach the evening of November 18. Fans have already started discussing how to flip their PlayStation or Xbox console region to New Zealand ahead of launch day — a trick the community has used for years to squeeze extra hours out of major game releases.

The basic idea:

- Change your console's region setting to New Zealand in the system settings.
- Make sure your digital pre-order or purchase is tied to a compatible storefront.
- Download and install as soon as the game unlocks at NZ midnight.
- Switch back to your home region once the game is on your hard drive.

> **Note:** Account and billing rules differ by platform and can change. Do your research before launch day — there's no guarantee this will work without complications.

## Worth the Hassle?

Depending on where you live, the New Zealand trick can get you anywhere from a few hours to nearly a day of early play. For a game with the anticipation level of GTA 6, even a few extra hours sounds pretty appealing to hardcore fans.

Of course, if you prefer the traditional midnight launch experience — staying up until your own local midnight with the rest of your region — that option is fully there too. Either way, the confirmed rolling launch means no one has to wait for a US East Coast unlock to finish trickling around the globe.
