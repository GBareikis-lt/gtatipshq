## Rockstar Starts Unveiling the GTA 6 Soundtrack

With just over a year to go before GTA 6 hits shelves on November 19, 2026, Rockstar Games has begun teasing one of the series' most beloved features: its radio stations. Six stations have now been officially revealed, each accompanied by short audio previews to give players an early taste of what they'll be hearing while cruising through Leonida.

More stations are confirmed to be on the way, so this is very much an opening salvo rather than the complete picture.

## What We Know So Far

The six revealed stations cover a broad musical range, hinting at a diverse and carefully curated soundtrack. Based on the previews, listeners can expect:

- **Classic rock and metal** — Black Sabbath and Slayer signal that harder-edged stations will be well represented
- **Latin sounds** — Bad Bunny's inclusion points to a strong Latin music presence, fitting for GTA 6's Florida-inspired setting
- **Familiar FM vibes** — Flash FM, a name that longtime GTA fans will recognise from Vice City, appears to be making a return
- **The Chamber 106.6** — A newly introduced station that has already caught attention from early listeners

> Rockstar has confirmed additional stations will be announced in the coming weeks, so the full lineup is still taking shape.

## Why the Radio Matters in GTA

GTA's radio has always been more than background noise — it sets the tone, reinforces the world-building, and gives each playthrough a distinct personality. A well-chosen station can make a routine drive feel cinematic. Given the Florida backdrop of GTA 6, a blend of Latin, hip-hop, rock, and pop makes a lot of sense thematically.
