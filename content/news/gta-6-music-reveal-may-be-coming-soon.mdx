## Artists Are Dropping GTA-Themed Teasers

Something appears to be brewing around GTA 6's music. Over the past few days, a wave of artists and labels have posted visuals with an unmistakable GTA aesthetic, fuelling speculation that Rockstar Games is gearing up to announce something related to the game's soundtrack.

Here's what has surfaced so far:

- **Morgan Wallen** shared an image featuring GTA-inspired visuals of a dry, empty pool — a distinctly Vice City-esque scene.
- **Travis Scott** posted his own GTA-styled image and wrote "See yall soon," directly tagging Rockstar Games in the post. Fans had already been connecting dots after spotting what looked like GTA 6 references in one of his earlier music videos.
- **Freebandz**, the record label, dropped an image of a car bearing a Leonida licence plate reading "VCE BBY" — a pretty direct nod to Vice City.
- **Keith Richards** of the Rolling Stones also released a teaser in a similar vein.

None of this is officially confirmed by Rockstar, so treat it as unverified for now.

## What It Could Mean

GTA has always had one of gaming's most celebrated soundtracks. The series' fictional radio stations are packed with real, licensed tracks spanning every genre imaginable, and that tradition is widely expected to continue with GTA 6. A coordinated multi-artist teaser campaign of this scale would be an unusual coincidence if it weren't tied to something real.

> A formal music reveal — whether that means a radio station preview, a soundtrack announcement, or something else entirely — could land very soon if this coordinated activity is any indication.

## What to Watch For

With GTA 6 set to launch on **November 19, 2026**, Rockstar will need to ramp up marketing in the months ahead. A soundtrack reveal would be a natural next step. Keep an eye on Rockstar's official channels and check back at [/news](/news) as we track any developments.
