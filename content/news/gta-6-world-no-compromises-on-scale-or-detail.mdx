## Rockstar Refuses to Trade Scale for Detail

Open-world games routinely face a tough balancing act: build a vast map and risk it feeling shallow, or pack in rich detail and end up with a smaller playground. According to Rockstar's art director Aaron Garbut, GTA 6 sidesteps that trade-off entirely.

Speaking to Game Informer, Garbut explained that the team deliberately pushed in the opposite direction from the usual compromises. Every dimension of the game — scope, density, interactivity, and visual fidelity — has been cranked up simultaneously. He added that Rockstar has never shipped anything remotely comparable in its history, and that the enormous pressure the project carried was met by a conscious effort to over-deliver rather than cut corners.

## What This Means for Leonida

The game is set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional take on Florida, and the studio is promising what it calls the "full Florida experience." That doesn't mean a literal recreation of every real-world landmark — Walt Disney World, for instance, won't appear — but the character and variety of the state are clearly a priority.

A few headline details already confirmed that hint at just how dense this world will be:

- **170+ animal species** roaming the map, including cane toads and monkeys
- Protagonists Lucia and Jason navigating a sprawling, reactive environment
- A world designed for high interaction density, not just visual spectacle

> These aren't just cosmetic flourishes — a richer ecosystem and more interactive environment could meaningfully change how missions, [tips](/tips), and emergent moments play out across the map.
