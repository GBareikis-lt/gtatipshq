## November 19 Is Still the Date

Take-Two Interactive, the parent company of Rockstar Games, has publicly reaffirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI is still scheduled to arrive on **November 19, 2026**. The statement came directly from company leadership and carries significant weight — this is the publisher itself going on record, not a leak or analyst speculation.

For fans who have been anxiously watching for any sign of a delay, this is about as reassuring as it gets short of the game shipping to retailers.

## 'An Extended Look' Is Meant to Fuel the Hype

Alongside the release date confirmation, Take-Two also addressed the upcoming **GTA 6: An Extended Look** presentation. Executives framed it as a deliberate move to strengthen what they called "consumers' passion and anticipation" heading into the holiday launch window.

In plain terms, Rockstar is planning to show more of the game — and the publisher clearly believes that extra footage will do a lot of the marketing heavy lifting in the months ahead. Given how much attention Trailer 1 and Trailer 2 each generated, that confidence seems well placed.

## What This Means for Players

- **No delay to worry about** — at least as of this official statement
- The Extended Look showcase should give us our deepest glimpse yet at gameplay, story, or online features
- With a November launch, a late-summer or early-autumn reveal window makes sense for building momentum
