## No More Strolling Around Armed to the Teeth

In every GTA game to date, you could wander busy streets with a rocket launcher on your back and passersby wouldn't bat an eye. That changes in GTA 6. According to a preview published by The New York Times, NPCs will actively react to players who pull out or visibly carry weapons in public — and their first move will be to call the police.

This is a meaningful shift. Previously, only firing a weapon or committing an obvious crime would trigger civilian panic or a wanted level. Now simply having a weapon out in a crowded area could set off a chain reaction before you've even done anything.

## Six-Star Wanted Level Returns

The timing of this detail matters. As revealed during Netflix's extended GTA 6 preview, Rockstar is bringing back the full six-star wanted level system that was absent from GTA 5. Pair that with NPCs who are quicker to dial 911, and heat can escalate much faster than veteran players might expect.

- Pulling out a shotgun on a busy street? Expect witnesses to react immediately.
- A rocket launcher will almost certainly draw more alarm than a holstered pistol.
- Escalation from NPC report to full police response could happen within seconds.

> Keep your weapons holstered in populated areas if you want to stay under the radar — situational awareness is going to matter a lot more in GTA 6.

## What This Means for How You Play

This NPC behaviour feeds into a broader theme of Rockstar raising the stakes for everyday movement through the world. Planning a mission that requires you to move through a crowded area armed? You may need to think about timing, disguise, or route more carefully than in past games.
