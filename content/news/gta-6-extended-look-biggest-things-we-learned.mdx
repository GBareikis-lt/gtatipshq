## Rockstar Finally Shows GTA 6 in Action

After years of near-silence following the initial announcement, Rockstar Games used a Netflix-exclusive showcase — *Grand Theft Auto 6: An Extended Look* — to pull the curtain back on real gameplay. From open-world activities to story missions and combat, there was a lot to digest. Here are the most notable takeaways.

## Six-Star Wanted Levels Are Back

One of the most talked-about reveals is the return of the six-star wanted system, which was dropped in GTA 5. Details on exactly what triggers the top tiers are still sparse, but one interesting new wrinkle is visible: icons beneath the stars appear to display the specific crimes authorities have clocked against you. That suggests a more reactive, record-keeping law enforcement system compared to previous entries.

## You Can Pet (or Scold) Dogs

On the lighter side, players spotted a button prompt allowing them to interact with dogs found out in the world — and the options go two ways. You can either pet them or, if you're feeling villainous, scold them. It's a small detail, but it signals the kind of world-building depth Rockstar is known for pushing in each new generation.

## Expect a Very Long Playthrough

Rockstar North's Rob Nelson mentioned his own playthrough clocked in at around 80 hours — and that almost certainly doesn't account for all the side content. For context, GTA 5's main story runs roughly 30 hours, so GTA 6 looks to be a significantly larger undertaking. Fans of Red Dead Redemption 2's sprawling pace should feel right at home.
