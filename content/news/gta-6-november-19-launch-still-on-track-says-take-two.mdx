## Take-Two Puts Delay Rumours to Bed

Rumours have been circulating online that GTA 6 had quietly slipped into 2027, but an official Take-Two financial document tells a very different story. The filing references the Grand Theft Auto series outpacing expectations and explicitly points to **November 19th** as the planned launch date — the same date Rockstar announced earlier this year.

For anyone who has been anxious about a potential delay, this is about as close to reassurance as you can get from a corporate filing.

## What the Document Actually Says

A few highlights worth noting from the Take-Two statement:

- The GTA franchise is described as showing **"strong momentum"** heading into the GTA 6 launch window.
- GTA Online's *A Safehouse in the Hills* update was called one of the **best-performing updates in the game's history**, suggesting the live-service ecosystem is healthy going into the new title.
- GTA+ membership saw **notable year-over-year growth**, supported by seasonal content drops.
- Take-Two projects more than **$1 billion in operating cash flow** for fiscal year 2027, giving the company significant room to invest around the launch.

> Keep in mind this comes from a financial filing, not a dedicated press release. The language is corporate, but the November 19 date is stated without any caveats.

## What This Means for Players

The strong performance of GTA Online and GTA+ matters beyond just business metrics. It signals that Rockstar's live-service foundation is solid, which bodes well for how GTA 6 Online might be supported post-launch. A financially healthy Take-Two is also better positioned to handle a major worldwide release.
