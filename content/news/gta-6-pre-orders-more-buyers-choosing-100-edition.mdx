## Stronger Than Expected Pre-Order Numbers

Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick has publicly commented on GTA 6 pre-order performance, describing results as going "much better than expected" in a recent interview with CNBC. That's a notable statement from the head of Rockstar's parent company, given that industry analysts were already projecting record-breaking sales figures ahead of the game's [November 19, 2026 release](/news).

## The $100 Tier Is Winning

Perhaps the most surprising takeaway from Zelnick's comments is which edition players are gravitating toward. Rather than opting for the standard, lower-priced version, the majority of pre-order buyers are choosing the $100 Ultimate Edition. This suggests that for a release as anticipated as GTA 6, a significant portion of the player base is willing to pay a premium price point — something the wider games industry will likely be watching closely.

> Keep in mind these figures reflect early pre-order trends and the final sales breakdown may shift before and after launch day.

## What This Means for Players

For those still deciding which edition to pick up, here are a few things worth considering:

- The Ultimate Edition typically bundles extra in-game content, currency, or cosmetic bonuses — though Rockstar has not fully detailed every perk included
- Paying more upfront can sometimes offer better long-term value if bonus GTA Online currency or items are included
- Pre-ordering locks in your copy ahead of what could be extremely high launch-day demand

Rockstar has yet to release a full breakdown comparing the two editions side by side, so it's worth keeping an eye on [official announcements](/news) before committing. Either way, the pre-order momentum suggests November 2026 is shaping up to be one of gaming's biggest ever launch windows.
