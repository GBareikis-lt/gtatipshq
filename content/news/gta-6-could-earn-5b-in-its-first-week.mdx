## Analysts Are Expecting Something Historic

We already knew GTA 6 was going to be massive, but a new report from games research firm Newzoo puts some genuinely staggering numbers on just how massive. According to their analysis, Rockstar's upcoming open-world title could pull in anywhere between **$3.3 billion and $5.2 billion in global revenue within its first week** of release.

For context, GTA 5 — still one of the best-selling games of all time — hit $1 billion in its first 72 hours back in 2013. Even Newzoo's conservative estimate would far surpass that benchmark.

## What's Driving the Prediction

Newzoo isn't guessing blindly here. The firm is drawing on its proprietary Game Performance Monitor, which tracks real spending data across digital storefronts. Key signals include:

- **Preorder momentum**: Newzoo tracked roughly $180 million in digital GTA 6 spending across the US and five major European markets (England, France, Germany, Italy, and Spain) during just the final week of June alone.
- **Historical player distribution**: Using GTA 5's lifetime console player data, Newzoo estimates those six markets account for around 69% of the global player base. Scaling that $180 million figure up globally puts first-week digital preorder spending at approximately **$260 million** — before the game even launches.

> Keep in mind these are analyst estimates, not figures confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two. The actual numbers could land anywhere within — or outside — the projected range.

## What This Means for Launch Day

With GTA 6 set to arrive on **November 19, 2026**, the preorder data suggests demand is already extraordinary. If Newzoo's projections hold, this could comfortably become the biggest entertainment launch in history, not just gaming.
