## A Step Up From GTA 5's Basic Hunting

GTA 5 included hunting as a Trevor-exclusive activity, but it was fairly bare-bones — no skinning, no selling of animal parts, and no deeper survival loop to speak of. With GTA 6 arriving on November 19, 2026, fans are now asking whether Rockstar might borrow some of the far more sophisticated wildlife mechanics it built for Red Dead Redemption 2.

## What Made RDR 2 Hunting So Compelling

Red Dead Redemption 2's hunting system set a high bar for open-world games:

- Animals reacted realistically to where they were shot, sometimes fleeing or collapsing slowly rather than dying instantly
- Each species had its own dedicated skinning animation
- Pelts and carcasses could be sold or used for crafting, giving the activity genuine economic weight

That level of detail made hunting feel like a meaningful part of the world rather than a side distraction.

## Clues in GTA 6's Promotional Material

One screenshot released by Rockstar appears to show swamp residents transporting alligators in the bed of a pickup truck — a small but intriguing detail. Whether that hints at a player-accessible hunting and trading economy, or is simply environmental storytelling, is unknown. Nothing about skinning, butchering, or selling animal parts has been officially confirmed for GTA 6.

> **Unconfirmed:** All discussion about hunting depth in GTA 6 is currently fan speculation. Rockstar has not detailed any hunting mechanics.

## What Would Actually Fit the Game?

Some fans argue that Leonida's vast swamplands and rural areas make a meaningful hunting system a natural fit. Others point out that GTA's faster, more chaotic tone is a different beast from RDR 2's slow-burn frontier life. Whether both protagonists or just one would participate is another open question.
