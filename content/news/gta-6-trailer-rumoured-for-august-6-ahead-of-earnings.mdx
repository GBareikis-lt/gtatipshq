## Why Everyone Is Watching August 6–7

Take-Two Interactive is set to release its next earnings report on Friday, August 7 — and two details about that timing have the GTA community buzzing. First, the report is scheduled to drop before the market opens at 9:30 AM ET rather than in the more typical after-close slot. Second, Friday earnings releases are genuinely unusual for the company. Together, these quirks have led some to wonder whether something big is planned in the run-up to that call.

Alec Boccanfuso, a portfolio manager at Gabelli Funds, suggested via SeekingAlpha that pre-market timing can be used to give investors room to process a major announcement before trading begins — implying a significant reveal could land *before* August 7 itself.

## A Trailer on Thursday?

That idea lines up neatly with a separate report from Insider Gaming's Tom Henderson, who indicated the next GTA 6 trailer could arrive on Thursday, August 6. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has previously stated that a full marketing push for GTA 6 would get underway this summer, so a trailer drop timed around an earnings event would make strategic sense.

> Keep in mind: none of this is confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two. Treat it as informed speculation until an official announcement appears.

## What's at Stake

Expectations for GTA 6 are sky-high regardless of trailer timing. Analyst Nick McKay of Freedom Capital Markets has projected the game could move roughly 30 million copies within its first three months — a staggering figure that underlines just how much rides on the November 19, 2026 launch for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
