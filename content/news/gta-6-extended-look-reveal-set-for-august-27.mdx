## Rockstar Teams Up With Netflix for a GTA 6 Showcase

Rockstar Games has officially announced an "Extended Look" at GTA 6, set to air on **Thursday, August 27, 2026**. The reveal is being staged in partnership with Netflix, marking an interesting crossover between the streaming giant and one of gaming's biggest upcoming releases.

This is the first time Rockstar has aligned a major GTA 6 showcase with a streaming platform in this way, suggesting the presentation could be more cinematic or documentary-style than a standard gameplay trailer drop.

## What to Expect

The phrase "Extended Look" implies something more substantial than a short teaser — think extended gameplay footage, narrative context, or a deeper dive into the world of Leonida. Here's what we can reasonably anticipate:

- **New gameplay footage** showing mechanics, environments, or characters
- A longer runtime than a typical trailer
- Possibly behind-the-scenes commentary or developer insight, given the Netflix partnership

> Keep in mind this is based on Rockstar's own announcement wording. The exact format hasn't been spelled out beyond "Extended Look."

## Why This Matters

With the game's [release date locked in for November 19, 2026](/news), an August showcase makes perfect sense — it sits right in the marketing sweet spot before the holiday retail push. Fans have been hungry for a proper look at GTA 6 beyond the original trailers, and this event looks like the most significant reveal since those early glimpses.
