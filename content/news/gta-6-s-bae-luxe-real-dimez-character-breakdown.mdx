## Who Is Bae-Luxe?

Among the many characters Rockstar has introduced ahead of GTA 6's November 19, 2026 launch, Bae-Luxe is shaping up to be one of the more intriguing supporting figures. She's one half of Real Dimez, a rap duo that also includes Roxy, and the pair have a backstory that feels very much at home in modern Vice City.

According to Rockstar's official character descriptions, Real Dimez didn't come up through traditional music industry channels. They built their following street-first — hustling, growing a social media presence, and rapping their way to a record deal with Only Raw Records. That blend of digital hustle and street credibility is a very 2020s story, and it suits Vice City's flashy, media-saturated world perfectly.

## Two Very Different Personalities

From what's visible in Trailer 2, the two members of Real Dimez appear to complement each other in contrasting ways:

- **Bae-Luxe** comes across as the louder, more unpredictable half of the duo
- **Roxy** reads as the cooler, more calculated presence

Of course, we won't know how these personalities play out in actual missions and story beats until the game is in our hands — so treat these early impressions as exactly that.

## How Do They Connect to the Wider Story?

Real Dimez are linked to notable GTA 6 figures Dre'Quan Priest and Boobie Ike, which suggests they're woven into a broader Vice City music-scene storyline rather than existing purely as background flavour. Whether that puts them in the orbit of Jason and Lucia remains to be seen, but the connections are there.

> Rockstar has a strong track record of making side characters feel fully realised — think of how memorable recurring figures became in GTA V. Real Dimez could follow that same path.

## What Fans Are Hoping For

Community speculation points to a few features that would make Real Dimez genuinely impactful:

- Original in-game tracks on Vice City radio
- Social media accounts that update dynamically within the game world
- Dedicated missions tied to their music career

None of this is confirmed, but given how much detail Rockstar has already put into these characters, it wouldn't be surprising. Keep an eye on our [news](/news) section as more details emerge closer to launch.
