## A New Heist Hits GTA Online Next Week

Rockstar is keeping GTA Online busy ahead of [GTA 6's November 2026 launch](/news), with the Kortz Center Heist set to go live on **14 July** for PS5 and PS4 players. The update is already rolling out as a free next-gen upgrade on PlayStation 5, so there's no excuse not to get ready before the doors open.

## What to Expect

The Kortz Center — a real Los Santos landmark — becomes the target of an elaborate art theft. Key details confirmed so far:

- **Solo or co-op** — the heist supports both single-player runs and full crew play, giving you flexibility depending on who's online.
- **Mansion required** — you'll need to own a Mansion property to access the content, making it one of the pricier entry points for recent GTA Online updates.
- **Art Studio extension** — a new upgradeable add-on for the Mansion, presumably tied to planning or executing the heist itself.

> The Mansion + Art Studio combo suggests Rockstar is leaning into player-owned bases as long-term content hubs, a design philosophy we're likely to see carried forward into GTA 6.

## Why It Matters Before GTA 6

With less than five months until GTA 6 drops, Rockstar appears committed to giving GTA Online a strong final run of content. Heists that blend property ownership with co-op gameplay could be a preview of the kind of structured criminal ventures we can expect in the next chapter. If you've been away from Los Santos for a while, this is a solid reason to jump back in and top up your in-game funds before the move to Vice City.
