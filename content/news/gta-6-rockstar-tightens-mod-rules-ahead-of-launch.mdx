## Rockstar Drops Stricter Mod Guidelines

With GTA 6 due on November 19, 2026, Rockstar Games has moved to reshape what the modding community can and cannot do — and the new rules are notably tighter than anything the studio has put in writing before. The updated guidelines, reported by Eurogamer, cover several areas that modders have historically treated as fair game.

## What the New Rules Cover

According to the report, Rockstar's refreshed policy restricts:

- **Mission mods** — custom or replacement story missions are now explicitly limited.
- **Map modifications** — adding or substantially altering in-game geography falls under the new restrictions.
- **Updated or expanded storylines** — fan-made narrative content that builds on Rockstar's own story is curtailed.
- **Third-party intellectual property** — bringing in assets from other games, films, or brands is now firmly off the table.

The timing is clearly deliberate. PC versions of GTA titles have always attracted enormous modding scenes, and Rockstar appears to want firm ground rules established before GTA 6 eventually makes its way to computers.

## What This Means for the Community

For console players at launch, none of this changes anything immediately — mods are a PC conversation. But for the long-term PC community, these restrictions could meaningfully narrow the scope of the creative work that flourished around GTA V for over a decade.

> Rockstar has historically tolerated single-player mods while drawing the line at anything touching online gameplay or monetisation. These new rules appear to extend that boundary considerably further.

It is worth noting that enforcement has always been the harder question with Rockstar mod policies. Whether the studio actively pursues projects that breach these guidelines remains to be seen.
