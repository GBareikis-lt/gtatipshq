## The GTA 5 Legacy — Brilliant, But Not Perfect

With GTA 6 arriving on November 19, 2026, anticipation has reached a fever pitch. GTA 5 set an almost impossibly high bar, but more than a decade of hindsight has given fans a clear-eyed view of where Rockstar could do better. A lively thread on r/GTA6 asked players to name the one thing they hope doesn't carry over — and the responses reveal some consistent themes worth paying attention to.

## The Biggest Community Concerns

- **Single-player getting shortchanged** — The most common worry is that GTA Online's enormous commercial success will again pull resources away from the story mode. GTA 5's campaign was widely praised, but many fans felt post-launch support dried up almost immediately in favour of GTA Online updates. Players want GTA 6's narrative to feel fully realised and supported long-term.
- **Repetitive mission design** — Some community members pointed to stretches in GTA 5 where missions felt formulaic: drive here, shoot these people, escape a three-star wanted level. With Rockstar's expanded team and modern tools, fans expect more variety and creativity in mission structure.
- **Shallow character progression** — A portion of the fanbase found GTA 5's RPG-lite skill systems underwhelming. There's hope GTA 6 will offer more meaningful progression that rewards different playstyles.
- **Sparse interiors** — The open world of Los Santos looked incredible from the outside, but many buildings were locked or empty. Players are hoping Leonida delivers a denser, more explorable environment.

> These are community wishes, not confirmed design decisions. Rockstar has shared very little about GTA 6's specific mechanics so far.

## What This Means Going Forward

It's worth remembering that much of GTA 6's design remains under wraps. Whether Rockstar has addressed these pain points won't be clear until we get closer to launch. Keep an eye on our [news hub](/news) for any official updates as November 2026 approaches.
