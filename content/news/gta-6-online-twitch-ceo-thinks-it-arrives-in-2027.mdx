## Rockstar Stays Quiet on Multiplayer

When Grand Theft Auto 6 launches on **November 19, 2026**, only the single-player campaign will be available at launch. Rockstar has not announced a multiplayer component, made any promises about a successor to GTA Online, or even confirmed that one is in development — at least not publicly.

That silence has left fans speculating, and now an unlikely voice has entered the conversation.

## What the Twitch CEO Said

Dan Clancy, CEO of Twitch, has publicly suggested that a GTA 6 multiplayer mode will arrive sometime next year — meaning 2027. It is worth stressing that this is an **unconfirmed personal opinion**, not an industry leak or a Rockstar statement. Clancy has no known insider access to Rockstar's roadmap, so treat this as informed speculation at best.

That said, the comment is still interesting. A major platform executive has clear reasons to watch where large gaming audiences gather, and a GTA 6 Online launch would almost certainly be one of the biggest streaming events in Twitch's history.

## What History Suggests

For context, GTA Online launched roughly **two weeks after** GTA 5's single-player release back in 2013 — though it arrived in a rough state and took months to stabilise. Rockstar has since grown far more cautious about online launches, so a longer gap before any GTA 6 multiplayer component would not be surprising.
