## Miami Heat Goes Vice City the Night Before Launch

Rockstar has teamed up with NBA franchise the Miami Heat for a striking real-world marketing event. On **November 18, 2026** — one day before GTA 6 hits shelves — the Heat will temporarily rebrand as the **Vice City** basketball team for a home game against the Milwaukee Bucks at the Kaseya Center in Miami.

## What to Expect on the Night

Details are still emerging, but here is what has been confirmed so far:

- The court will be dressed in Vice City-themed visuals, drawing on the in-game Miami setting and GTA 6's signature pink colour palette.
- Players will compete in custom Vice City kits, though the jersey designs have not yet been revealed.
- A programme of special events and activities is planned throughout the evening alongside the game itself.

> This is one of the most high-profile real-world tie-ins in GTA history, bringing the fictional Vice City directly into an actual professional sports arena.

## Why It Matters

For GTA 6 fans, this event underlines just how big Rockstar's launch campaign is shaping up to be. The Heat's home arena sits in Miami — the real city that Vice City is famously modelled on — making the crossover feel genuinely fitting rather than a generic sponsorship deal. It also signals that Rockstar is treating the November 19 launch as a cultural moment well beyond traditional gaming marketing.
