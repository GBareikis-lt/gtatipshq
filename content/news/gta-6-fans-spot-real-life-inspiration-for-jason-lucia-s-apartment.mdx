## Fans Play Detective on Vice City's Architecture

The GTA 6 community has developed a well-earned reputation for forensic attention to detail, and a new fan discovery is a prime example. Enthusiasts believe they've identified the real-world building that Rockstar used as the creative basis for the apartment shared by protagonists Jason and Lucia — the one glimpsed in the game's trailers and promotional material.

As is typical with Rockstar's world design, Vice City isn't a pixel-perfect recreation of Miami but rather a carefully crafted fictional remix of it. That said, the studio draws heavily from actual South Florida architecture, streetscapes, and culture, which is exactly what makes these community hunts so compelling.

## What the Community Found

Fans cross-referenced visual details from the trailer footage — things like the building's exterior style, balcony layout, and surrounding environment — with real Miami-area properties to narrow down a likely candidate. While the match looks convincing to many in the community, it's worth stressing this remains **unconfirmed fan research**, not anything Rockstar has acknowledged.

> Keep in mind: Rockstar frequently blends multiple real locations into a single in-game structure, so the apartment may draw from several sources rather than one building alone.
