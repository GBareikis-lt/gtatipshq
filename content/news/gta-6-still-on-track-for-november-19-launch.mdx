## Take-Two Puts Delay Fears to Rest

Every time Take-Two Interactive reports its quarterly earnings, the GTA community holds its breath waiting for bad news. This time, though, the publisher delivered exactly what fans wanted to hear: GTA 6 is still coming on **November 19, 2026**, with no changes to the schedule.

Speaking on the company's August 7 earnings call, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick tied the November 19 launch directly to the publisher's ambitious financial forecasts, projecting net bookings of between $8 billion and $8.2 billion for fiscal year 2027. In other words, the entire outlook rests on GTA 6 shipping on time — which suggests there is genuine internal confidence behind the date.

## Why People Were Worried

The concern isn't irrational. GTA 6 has already shifted its launch window twice:

- An initial target was pushed to **May 2026**
- That date was later moved again to the current **November 19, 2026** slot
- A former Rockstar employee recently commented publicly that another delay wouldn't surprise them

Rockstar's track record of polishing games beyond their originally announced windows makes these jitters understandable, so a direct reaffirmation from Take-Two's CEO carries real weight.

## What This Means for Players

> The November 19 date is now backed by a public financial commitment — Take-Two's $8B+ revenue forecast depends on it.

For PS5 and Xbox Series X|S owners, the message is straightforward: start making plans. With roughly three months to go, marketing, pre-order details, and hands-on coverage should begin ramping up soon.
