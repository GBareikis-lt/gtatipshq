## Separating Official Footage from Community Speculation

Every GTA 6 map discussion seems to blend genuine trailer evidence with fan-made cartography, and the two often get presented as equally reliable. A community member on r/GTA6 recently attempted to fix that by applying a strict rule: if a location can't be traced to a specific moment in official Rockstar footage, it doesn't earn the label "confirmed." It's a useful framework worth unpacking.

## What the Trailers Actually Show

Based on verifiable trailer and promotional imagery, these environments have been clearly visible in official material:

- **Vice City core** — downtown skyline and a recognizable financial district
- **Beach boulevard** — an Ocean Drive-style strip with hotels and promenades
- **Port and industrial waterfront** — large cargo and dock areas
- **Bay bridge / causeway** — connecting landmasses across open water
- **Nightlife district** — clubs, neon signage, entertainment venues
- **International airport** — large-scale, multi-terminal facility
- **Shopping mall interior** — multi-level retail space shown up close
- **Everglades analog** — swampland and wetland wilderness
- **Rural highways** — small towns, motels, trailer communities
- **Prison facility**, **coastal marina**, and **suburban residential sprawl**

That's a substantial and varied map even before considering anything unconfirmed.

## What Remains Unconfirmed Fan Mapping

Several locations circulate constantly as though settled, but they aren't:

- **Offshore island chain** (a Florida Keys equivalent) — inferred from aerial water geometry, never named by Rockstar
- **A northern inland region** — pure fan cartography with no official anchor
- **A second major city** — frequently stated as fact online, but zero official confirmation exists
- **Subway or underground transit network** — referenced in community discussions but not clearly shown in official footage

> Unconfirmed doesn't mean wrong. Fan researchers have a solid track record. It just means treat these as strong possibilities, not guarantees.

## Why This Distinction Matters

With GTA 6 arriving [November 19, 2026](/news), hype naturally outpaces evidence. Keeping confirmed and speculative locations in separate mental columns helps set realistic expectations and makes the genuine reveals hit harder when they come.
