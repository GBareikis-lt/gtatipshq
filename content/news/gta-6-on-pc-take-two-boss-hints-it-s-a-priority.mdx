## Take-Two's CEO Puts PC in the Spotlight

At Take-Two Interactive's recent annual shareholder meeting, CEO Strauss Zelnick fielded a question that virtually every PC gamer has been asking: when is GTA 6 coming to PC? While he stopped short of announcing a date or making any firm commitment, Zelnick did acknowledge that the PC platform is becoming "more and more important" to the company — a statement that at least signals PC is firmly on Take-Two's radar.

It's worth noting that GTA 6 is currently confirmed for a **November 19, 2026** launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. No PC release window has been officially announced.

## What This Actually Means

Zelnick's comments are encouraging but should be taken with a healthy dose of caution. Rockstar has a well-documented history of launching its flagship titles on consoles first and bringing them to PC months — or even years — later. GTA V, for example, hit consoles in September 2013 and didn't arrive on PC until April 2015.

- The console-first strategy helps Rockstar control performance and optimisation on a fixed hardware target
- A later PC release also functions as a second commercial launch, boosting overall lifetime sales
- Growing PC market share makes skipping the platform increasingly costly for publishers

> Keep in mind: nothing here is confirmed. Zelnick's remarks are broadly positive toward PC gaming, not a specific GTA 6 PC announcement.

## What PC Players Should Do Now

If you're a PC-only player hoping to jump into GTA 6 at launch, the honest advice is to manage expectations. Follow our [news section](/news) for any official PC announcements as they happen. In the meantime, it may be worth considering whether a console playthrough at launch is preferable to waiting an unknown length of time for a PC version.
