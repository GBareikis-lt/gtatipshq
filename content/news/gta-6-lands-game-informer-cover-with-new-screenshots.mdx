## Game Informer Dedicates 14 Pages to GTA 6

Game Informer has revealed that its next issue, landing on **Tuesday, September 29, 2026**, puts GTA 6 front and centre — literally. Jason and Lucia, the game's dual protagonists, appear on the cover in a now-familiar stylised pose: armed and riding together in a vehicle. It's a bold statement from the publication, and the content inside promises to match.

The feature runs to 14 pages and includes exclusive interviews conducted directly with Rockstar Games, alongside **twelve new screenshots** that fans haven't seen before. That's a meaningful drop of fresh visual material at a stage when every new look at the game generates significant community discussion.

## What to Expect from the Issue

While the full contents are under wraps until the release date, a multi-page Rockstar interview suggests we could get genuine insight into areas like:

- World design and the scale of Vice City and its surroundings
- How the dual-protagonist structure shapes missions and storytelling
- Details on GTA Online's next chapter

Of course, until the issue is in readers' hands, all of that remains speculation. What's confirmed is the interview access and the screenshot count.

## Why This Matters Ahead of the November 2026 Launch

With GTA 6 scheduled to release on **November 19, 2026**, a major editorial feature dropping in late September fits neatly into what is typically a pre-launch marketing window. Expect the screenshots and interview excerpts to fuel community discussion across forums and social platforms for weeks.
