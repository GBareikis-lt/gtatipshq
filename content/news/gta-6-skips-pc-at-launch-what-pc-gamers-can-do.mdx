## No GTA 6 on PC This November

If you were holding out hope that Rockstar might surprise everyone with a simultaneous PC release on November 19, 2026, it's time to set that aside. GTA 6 is launching exclusively on consoles — PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S — on that date, with no PC release window announced or even hinted at officially.

This isn't unusual for Rockstar. GTA 5 arrived on PC roughly a year and a half after its console debut, and Red Dead Redemption 2 followed a similar pattern. PC players have historically had to wait, and GTA 6 appears to be no different.

## What the PC Gaming Conversation Looks Like Right Now

PC Gamer recently noted, with some humor, that the closest thing PC players are getting on launch day is a Steam title called *Phantom Vice Auto* — a game whose name alone makes the inspiration obvious. It's a reminder of just how much demand exists among PC players for a GTA 6 experience, even if the real thing isn't available yet.

There's no shame in acknowledging the wait will sting. November 19 is going to be a big day for console players, and social media will make it impossible to avoid.

## How to Make the Most of the Wait

- **Stay spoiler-aware.** Story details will spread fast after launch. Consider limiting social media if you want a clean experience when the PC version eventually arrives.
- **Revisit GTA 5 or GTA Online** to scratch the itch — check our [tips section](/tips) for money-making guides and secrets.
- **Watch for PC release news.** Rockstar typically announces PC ports several months in advance, so keep an eye on our [news feed](/news).

> The PC version of GTA 6 is a matter of *when*, not *if* — Rockstar has never permanently skipped the platform.

Patience is the only real option here, but history says it's worth it.
