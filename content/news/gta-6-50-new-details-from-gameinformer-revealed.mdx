## Rockstar's Vision for Leonida

According to details circulating from a GameInformer exclusive with Rockstar's Aaron Garbut, the studio views Florida — the real-world inspiration for GTA 6's fictional state of Leonida — as "America dialed up to 11." Garbut reportedly describes it as a place of striking beauty and deep contradiction, making it an ideal sandbox for a Grand Theft Auto title.

To back that up, Rockstar spent several years making multiple research trips to Miami and South Florida. The team rode around neighbourhoods at night with former law enforcement, visited nightclubs, explored the Everglades, and spent time in the Keys. Writers and designers also spoke directly with local residents to better understand regional culture, crime, and policing — all in service of creating what Rockstar calls its most expressive and diverse NPC population yet.

## Jason and Lucia: Character Origins

The feature reportedly sheds light on both protagonists' backstories:

- **Jason** starts the game working for small-time drug runners in the Leonida Keys. He's an army veteran, which likely shapes both his skills and his outlook.
- **Lucia** hails from Liberty City and was trained in combat sports by her father. A mysterious incident connected to her family is what landed her in prison before the events of the game.

These details add meaningful context to the characters we've glimpsed in trailers, though some specifics remain unconfirmed at this stage.
