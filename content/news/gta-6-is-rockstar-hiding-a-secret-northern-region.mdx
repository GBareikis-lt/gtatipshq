## The Theory Making Rounds on Reddit

A post gaining traction on r/GTA6 is raising an interesting question: could Rockstar be deliberately concealing a significant portion of GTA 6's map, specifically in the northern reaches of Leonida? The theory is unconfirmed speculation, but it draws on some genuinely compelling observations.

The key detail that sparked the discussion is a mention of Jacksonville in a recent Game Informer article about GTA 6. In real life, Jacksonville sits in northeastern Florida and is the state's most populous city as well as one of the largest US cities by land area. Yet fans and the mapping community have noted that northeastern Leonida remains one of the least-documented parts of the map based on everything that has leaked or been officially shown so far.

## The RDR2 Comparison

The Reddit post draws a direct parallel to Rockstar's approach with *Red Dead Redemption 2*, where the New Austin region was kept largely under wraps before launch, surprising players with more explorable territory than they anticipated. Rockstar has a track record of protecting certain surprises even when substantial information has already made it into the public eye.

> Worth noting: this is fan speculation based on circumstantial evidence, not a leak or confirmed report. Treat it as an interesting possibility, not a spoiler.

## What We Actually Know

- The official GTA 6 release date remains **November 19, 2026**.
- Leonida's map has been partially pieced together by the fan community, but coverage of the northeast is notably thin.
- Rockstar has strong motivation to keep at least some areas secret ahead of launch.

Whether or not a hidden northern city exists, Rockstar will almost certainly have a few surprises left. Keep an eye on [our news section](/news) for any official map reveals as launch approaches.
