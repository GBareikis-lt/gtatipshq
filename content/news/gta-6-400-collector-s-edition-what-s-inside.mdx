## Rockstar Reveals The Goodtime State: Vice City Collection

Rockstar Games has officially unveiled a premium collector's bundle tied to GTA 6's launch on November 19, 2026. Priced at $400, **The Goodtime State: Vice City Collection** is a merchandise set themed around Leonida's fictional hit TV show, *Macca the Gator*. Preorders are open now — though there's one significant catch worth knowing upfront.

> The $400 price does **not** include a copy of GTA 6. You'll need to purchase the game separately.

## Everything You Get for $400

Despite the steep price tag, the box does pack in a wide variety of collectibles:

- Six-inch Macca the Gator figure with a hidden stash compartment
- Matte black GTA 6 x Oakley Frogskins sunglasses with Prizm Sapphire lenses
- New Era 9FORTY A-Frame snapback hat with Vice City Leonida detailing
- Macca the Gator magnetic mirror
- Vice City swizzle spoon and razor blade keychain
- Leonida Keys crossbody bag
- Chunkee the Manatee shot glass
- Leonida Keys enamel pin set in a custom metal tin
- Premium stickers in a stash bag
- Double-sided souvenir poster featuring the full Leonida map

## Is It Worth the Price?

Player reaction online has been largely negative, with many pointing out that $400 for lifestyle merchandise — without even a game code included — is a tough sell. That said, the Oakley sunglasses alone retail at a premium, and the in-world branding is genuinely creative, leaning into GTA 6's fictional Leonida culture in a way that feels more considered than a typical merch drop.
