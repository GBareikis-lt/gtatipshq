## PS5 Leading the Pre-Order Race

With GTA 6 set to launch on **November 19, 2026**, pre-order data is starting to paint a clear picture of where players plan to experience the game — and it's heavily leaning toward PlayStation 5. A report from The Verge (behind a paywall) referenced by Push Square indicates that Sony's platform is significantly outpacing Xbox in GTA 6 pre-orders, a trend that comes as little surprise given PlayStation's strong install base this generation.

This is an unconfirmed, third-party data point, so treat the specific numbers with some caution until verified by official sources.

## Sony's Big Investment

Sony has reportedly put serious money behind making GTA 6 feel as closely associated with PlayStation as possible — without it technically being an exclusive. That kind of marketing partnership, common with major third-party releases, can include:

- Exclusive early access to betas or demos
- PlayStation-branded promotional materials
- Priority placement on the PlayStation Store

Whether that spend is the primary driver of the pre-order gap or simply reflects PlayStation's wider audience is hard to say definitively.

## Xbox Responds

An Xbox executive has reportedly commented on the skewed pre-order figures, though the details remain paywalled. This comes at a difficult moment for Microsoft's gaming division, which is navigating significant internal restructuring and layoffs — pressures that may be making it harder to compete for marquee third-party partnerships.
