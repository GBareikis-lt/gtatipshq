## Rockstar Turns Up the Volume

Rockstar Games has pulled back the curtain on some of the radio stations players will be cruising to when GTA 6 launches on **November 19, 2026**. According to an official blog post on the Rockstar website, the game will feature the "biggest and widest-ranging selection of in-game radio yet" — a bold claim for a series already famous for its eclectic soundtracks.

Six stations have been confirmed so far, with previews available to stream directly through the official site right now, giving fans their first real taste of the Leonida airwaves.

## What We Know So Far

- Rockstar is billing this as the most ambitious radio lineup in GTA history
- Six stations have been named and are already available to preview online
- The stations are tied to the game's fictional state of Leonida, suggesting the music selection will reflect the setting's culture and geography
- This follows Rockstar's tradition of using licensed music and original fictional DJs to build atmosphere

> This is an official Rockstar announcement, so the station names and previews are confirmed content — not rumour.

## Why It Matters

Radio has always been one of GTA's secret weapons. From the satirical talk shows to genre-defining music curation, the soundtrack shapes how the open world *feels* to inhabit. If Rockstar is genuinely expanding the breadth of stations beyond anything seen in GTA V or Red Dead Redemption 2, that's a meaningful quality-of-life upgrade for the hundreds of hours players will spend behind the wheel.
