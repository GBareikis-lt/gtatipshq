## From Continent-Spanning Ambition to Focused Florida

A detailed Bloomberg report published in October 2026 sheds new light on just how radically GTA 6's development vision changed over more than a decade. When a small Rockstar scouting team flew to Miami back in 2015 to begin laying the groundwork, the game's working concept — codenamed **Project Americas** — was genuinely enormous: a living world stretching across major regions of both North and South America.

That level of ambition, while exciting on paper, ultimately proved unsustainable. Rockstar gradually narrowed the focus to a single, deeply realised state: **Leonida**, a fictionalised version of Florida. The result is still far from small.

## How Big Is the Final Map?

Even after trimming the original vision, GTA 6's map is reported to be roughly **three times the size** of Red Dead Redemption II's sprawling 1899 frontier — itself one of the largest and most detailed open worlds ever built. Confirmed locations across Leonida currently include:

- **Vice City** — the game's Miami-inspired urban centrepiece
- **Leonida Keys** — a chain of island communities
- **Grassrivers** — swampland and rural stretches
- **Port Gellhorn** — a working port district
- **Ambrosia** — a distinct regional area
- **Mount Kalaga** — elevated terrain offering new traversal possibilities

## Why It Took So Long

The report also touches on an earlier plan to develop GTA 6 **alongside** Red Dead Redemption 2, which could theoretically have brought the game to players years sooner. That parallel development track did not survive contact with reality, and RDR2 ultimately absorbed Rockstar's full attention before shipping in 2018.

> These revelations help explain the long wait — and arguably justify it. A game that once aimed to cover two continents has been reshaped into something more focused, though still historically large.
