## Ongoing Concerns at Rockstar UK

A new report from PC Gamer highlights fresh workplace grievances from employees at Rockstar's UK studios — the teams contributing to what will be one of the most anticipated game launches in history. Staff have reportedly raised issues around three main areas: a persistent gender pay gap, demanding crunch conditions, and a bonus system described as deliberately vague.

## What Employees Are Saying

According to the report, workers have described a bonus structure that can withhold a significant portion of salary — reportedly as much as a fifth — without clear or transparent criteria. That kind of ambiguity, employees argue, leaves staff overly dependent on managerial goodwill rather than objective performance measures.

Crunch — the industry term for extended, often mandatory overtime periods — was also cited as an ongoing problem, suggesting development pressure remains high as GTA 6 moves toward its **November 19, 2026** release date. Gender pay disparity rounded out the key concerns raised.

## Why This Matters for GTA 6 Fans

It's worth being clear: none of this suggests GTA 6's development is in trouble, and Rockstar has not publicly responded to these specific claims at the time of writing. However, workplace conditions at major studios are a legitimate topic for the broader gaming community to follow.

> These are employee accounts reported by a trusted outlet. They reflect an ongoing industry-wide conversation about fair treatment in game development — not a signal about GTA 6's quality or release schedule.

## The Bigger Picture

Rockstar has faced scrutiny over working conditions before, most notably around Red Dead Redemption 2's development in 2018. The studio has since made public commitments to improve culture. Whether those changes have fully taken hold at its UK offices remains, based on this report, an open question.
