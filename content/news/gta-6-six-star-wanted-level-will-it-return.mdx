## A Classic Feature Fans Want Back

One of the longest-running debates in the GTA 6 community has resurfaced: will Rockstar bring back the iconic six-star wanted level? GTA 5 capped the system at five stars, but older entries in the series — including the original Vice City — went all the way to six. With GTA 6 returning to a Miami-inspired Vice City setting, many players feel it would be a natural fit to restore that sixth, terrifying tier of law enforcement chaos.

## Why Six Stars Mattered

The six-star wanted level wasn't just a number — it represented an escalation point where the game essentially declared all-out war on the player. Military units, helicopters, and maximum aggression made it feel genuinely dangerous. Reducing the cap to five in GTA 5 made the top tier feel somewhat routine once players got comfortable with the system. A six-star ceiling could keep veteran players on their toes far longer.

## What We Actually Know

Rockstar has not officially confirmed anything about the wanted system in GTA 6. The trailers have shown police chases and pursuit mechanics that appear more dynamic and realistic than previous entries, but the maximum star count has not been revealed. This remains firmly in speculation territory for now.

> Until Rockstar shares more gameplay details ahead of the November 19, 2026 launch, the exact shape of the wanted system is still an open question.

## What It Could Mean for Gameplay

- A sixth star could introduce a brand-new threat type, such as private military contractors or federal agents
- It would give high-level players a fresh challenge ceiling to aim for
- Combined with GTA 6's reportedly expanded world, six-star chases could play out very differently across diverse terrain
