## The Rumour Making the Rounds

As GTA 6's November 2026 launch draws closer, performance expectations are heating up across all platforms. A circulating rumour suggests there could be a viable path to 60 frames per second on Xbox Series X — a prospect that would delight a huge portion of the player base. However, the claim remains unverified and should be treated with appropriate scepticism.

## What Digital Foundry Thinks

The respected technical analysts at Digital Foundry have weighed in, and their take is cautious. According to reporting by Pure Xbox, DF experts consider 60FPS on Xbox Series X to be **unlikely**, presumably due to the sheer visual and simulation complexity Rockstar is targeting with GTA 6. Their reasoning isn't surprising — if the game is pushing the boundaries of open-world density, NPC behaviour, and graphical fidelity, hitting a locked 60FPS on current-gen hardware is a tall order.

> Keep in mind that neither Rockstar nor Take-Two have officially confirmed any performance targets for any platform. Everything here is rumour and third-party analysis.

## What This Could Mean for Players

If 60FPS does prove out of reach on Xbox Series X, players will likely be looking at options similar to what we've seen on other demanding titles:

- A **Performance mode** targeting 60FPS with reduced resolution or visual settings
- A **Quality/Fidelity mode** at 30FPS with full graphical fidelity
- Potentially a middle-ground **Performance RT** option

Rockstar has not announced any mode breakdown yet, so nothing is confirmed either way.

## The Bigger Picture

With the [GTA 6 release](/news) locked in for November 19, 2026, Rockstar still has time to optimise across platforms. Whether Series X owners get a smooth 60FPS experience or need to choose between modes remains one of the more interesting unanswered questions heading into launch.
