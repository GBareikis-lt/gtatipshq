## Why Fake GTA 6 Videos Are Everywhere Right Now

With GTA 6 still several months away from its [November 19, 2026 release](/news) and no proper gameplay trailer on the horizon, fans are understandably hungry for anything new. That hunger has created the perfect conditions for a wave of AI-generated fake footage to thrive on platforms like YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram — and some of these videos are pulling in millions of views.

According to a report from Eurogamer, the volume of this so-called "AI slop" has grown significantly as anticipation builds. The clips typically mimic real game footage closely enough to fool casual viewers scrolling through their feeds, but they are entirely fabricated using AI video generation tools.

## How to Spot a Fake

If you come across a GTA 6 video making big claims, here are a few quick checks before you share it:

- **Check the source.** Official GTA 6 content comes only from Rockstar Games' verified accounts.
- **Look for visual glitches.** AI-generated footage often struggles with hands, text, logos, and fine environmental detail.
- **Ignore unverified "leaks."** If it isn't backed by a credible outlet with a named source, treat it as fiction.
- **Check the upload date and channel history.** Brand-new channels posting "exclusive" footage are a major red flag.

> The only confirmed GTA 6 gameplay you have seen is what Rockstar officially released. Everything else should be treated with serious scepticism.

## Why It Matters

Beyond simple misinformation, this trend can genuinely distort community expectations. When fans build hype around fabricated features or visuals, the real game — however impressive — risks an unfair backlash at launch.
