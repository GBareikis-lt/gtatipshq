## Vice City Leads the Pack

Rockstar has confirmed that Vice City will be roughly twice the size of Los Santos from GTA 5 — making it easily the largest single city in the series to date. That alone is a significant promise, but the broader Leonida map extends well beyond Vice City's neon-lit streets.

## The Rest of Leonida's Settlements

Beyond Vice City, the GTA 6 map is expected to include several additional towns and regions. Based on community comparisons of trailer footage and map speculation, the rough size estimates (relative to Los Santos) are:

- **Capri** — around 40–50% of Los Santos, potentially a sizeable coastal hub
- **Port Gellhorn** — estimated at 25–40%, likely an industrial or port-focused area
- **Ambrosia** — roughly 10–20%, a smaller inland settlement
- **Leonida Keys** — 5–15%, island communities strung along the southern coast
- **Grassrivers** — 5–15%, probably rural wetlands territory
- **Mount Kalaga** — 5–15%, a mountainous region rather than a dense urban area

It's worth stressing that only the Vice City figure comes from Rockstar directly. Every other estimate is community-driven speculation based on trailer analysis and map leaks, so treat them as educated guesses rather than confirmed facts.

> Keep in mind that raw size doesn't always equal content density — a compact town packed with missions and secrets can feel larger than an empty open landscape.

## What This Means for Players

Even if the smaller settlements turn out to be modest in scale, the sheer variety of environments — urban sprawl, island chains, wetlands, mountain terrain — suggests Leonida will offer a genuinely diverse playground.
