## A Living, Breathing Leonida

According to details reportedly shared by Game Informer, GTA 6's version of Leonida is shaping up to be one of the most richly simulated open worlds ever built. Take everything below as unverified until Rockstar confirms it officially, but the scope described is genuinely impressive.

## Dynamic Weather That Actually Matters

The wind system in GTA 6 is said to be a major step forward. After dark, expect sea breezes, building-influenced gusts, and rising updrafts to kick in — subtle details that make the world feel more alive. Wind reportedly affects:

- Hair on characters and pedestrians
- Trash and debris on rooftops
- Water surface movement
- Swamp vegetation

Beyond everyday wind, tropical storms and full hurricanes are confirmed for Leonida. These events bring dense cloud cover, heavy downpours, water streaming off rooftops and streetlights, and full lightning and thunder displays.

## Over 170 Animal Species

The reported wildlife count is staggering — more than 170 species in total, including:

- 60+ bird species (flamingos and pelicans among them)
- 30+ terrestrial mammals
- Fish, reptiles, and manatees
- Alligators in the swamplands

For fans who love exploring off the beaten path, the [map](/map) is going to reward curiosity like never before.

## NPC Detail Pushed to a New Level

Pedestrian variety has apparently received serious attention. Height, body type, muscle mass, and weight distribution will vary far more than in previous GTA titles. Skin details like freckles, moles, and tan lines are modelled individually, while tattoos are said to reflect specific regional communities within Leonida. Hairstyles and body hair also feature greater ethnic diversity.

> Leonida is reportedly around twice the size of the GTA V map — making it the largest world Rockstar has ever built.
