## What the Fan Theory Covers

A detailed post on r/GTA6 has been making the rounds, offering a deep-dive into the possible backstories of GTA 6's two protagonists — Jason and Lucia. It's worth noting upfront that this is fan speculation built on trailer analysis, not confirmed information from Rockstar Games.

## Jason's Background

According to the theory, Jason has a history as a ranger before the events of the game. The poster suggests this past might influence his special ability, potentially leaning toward sharpshooting or precision aiming. By the time the story begins, he's working in the Leonida Keys alongside characters named Brian, Cal, and Donnie — a crew setup that echoes classic GTA ensemble dynamics.

## Lucia's Story

The speculation around Lucia is arguably more detailed. The theory posits she grew up in Liberty City, was taught to fight by her father, and ended up imprisoned after a violent incident tied to protecting her family. The poster points to what appears to be a cage-fighting scene in trailer footage as a potential prologue set in LC — drawing comparisons to underground fight scenes from *The Ballad of Gay Tony*.

After her release on parole — complete with an ankle monitor — Lucia is picked up by Jason himself, and the two head to the Leonida Keys for what the trailer frames as a fresh start.

> The theory also raises an interesting question: do Jason and Lucia already know each other before the story begins, or is their familiarity in the trailer a deliberate misdirect by Rockstar?

## Worth Following

None of this is confirmed, and Rockstar has shared very little about the actual plot beyond the trailers. Still, theories like this are fun ways to engage with the material ahead of the [November 19, 2026 release](/news).
