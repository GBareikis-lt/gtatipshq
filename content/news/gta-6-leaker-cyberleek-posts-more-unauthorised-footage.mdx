## CyberLeek Returns With New Clips

The leaker operating under the name CyberLeek has once again posted unauthorised GTA 6 footage online. According to a report from Eurogamer, the latest material features protagonist Lucia and has been circulating on X (formerly Twitter), where it is being shared natively — meaning it can appear in feeds without clicking a link.

This follows earlier activity from the same account, suggesting CyberLeek has ongoing access to some form of pre-release build or assets, though the exact source remains entirely unverified.

## What We Know (and Don't)

- The footage is **unconfirmed** as authentic by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
- CyberLeek's material carries a watermark overlay, making independent verification difficult.
- Eurogamer has chosen not to link directly to the videos, and we are following the same approach here.
- Whether this represents a new internal breach or edited/fabricated content is unknown.

> **Heads up:** If you want to avoid potential story or character spoilers before GTA 6's November 19, 2026 release, consider muting CyberLeek-related search terms on social media for now.

## Why It Matters

Leaks of this nature put Rockstar in an uncomfortable position, as the studio has historically pursued legal action against those who distribute pre-release material without authorisation — most notably following the 2022 source-code leak. Whether Take-Two will respond formally remains to be seen.
