## Sony Drops Physical Discs — Xbox Hasn't Decided Yet

Sony recently confirmed it will end all physical disc support from January 2028 onwards, a move that all but rules out a disc drive in the PS6. That announcement left one obvious question hanging: will Microsoft follow suit with Project Helix, its next-generation Xbox console?

According to a rumour reported by Push Square, the answer is not yet settled. Microsoft is apparently still weighing whether to include physical disc support in Project Helix, and with Sony firmly out of that space, there could be a meaningful opportunity to appeal to players who still prefer owning games on disc.

## Why This Matters for GTA 6 Players

GTA 6 is scheduled to launch on **November 19, 2026**, well ahead of either next-gen console arriving. However, Rockstar's next title has an almost certain future on whatever hardware follows the PS5 and Xbox Series X. Physical vs. digital availability could influence:

- **How players purchase and resell** their copy of GTA 6 on next-gen hardware
- **Game preservation** — a physical edition can be kept indefinitely without relying on online storefronts
- **Price competition** — physical retail often offers launch-day discounts that digital stores don't match

## Just a Rumour — Treat It That Way

> Nothing here is confirmed. Both Project Helix and PS6 hardware details remain unannounced, so take all of this with a healthy dose of scepticism.

What is clear is that the disc-versus-digital debate is shaping up to be a defining hardware story heading into the next console generation. If Project Helix does ship with a disc drive, it could stand out as the only next-gen option for players who want a physical copy.
