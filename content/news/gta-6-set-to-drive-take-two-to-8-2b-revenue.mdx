## Take-Two's Biggest Financial Year on Record

With GTA 6 still on track for its **November 19, 2026** launch, publisher Take-Two Interactive is projecting a landmark financial year. The company expects revenue to climb roughly 20% by mid-fiscal 2027, reaching an estimated **$8.2 billion** across its full portfolio — a figure that would comfortably eclipse any previous year in its history.

## How the Revenue Breaks Down

Take-Two has given a rough breakdown of where that money is expected to come from:

- **Rockstar Games (~37%)** — GTA 6 will be the headline earner, but legacy titles like GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, along with their ongoing online microtransaction ecosystems, will contribute meaningfully too.
- **Zynga (~34%)** — The mobile gaming giant remains a steady revenue machine, with its broad portfolio of casual titles delivering consistent sales.
- **2K Games (~29%)** — Annual sports franchises like NBA 2K, PGA Tour 2K, and WWE 2K carry significant post-launch monetisation strategies. NBA 2K26 was already highlighted internally as an "outstanding" performer this year.

> It's worth noting these are company forecasts, not guaranteed figures — though with GTA 6 in the lineup, the confidence behind them is easy to understand.

## What This Means for GTA 6 Players

The sheer scale of Take-Two's financial expectations underlines just how much is riding on GTA 6 at a corporate level. For players, that translates to a heavily resourced launch — extensive post-launch support, a robust GTA Online successor, and likely years of continued content updates are all but certain.
