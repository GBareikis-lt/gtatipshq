## A New GTA 6 Trailer Is Almost Here

The long wait between major GTA 6 reveals is nearly over. Rockstar Games has confirmed an "extended look" at GTA 6 will premiere on **August 27**, marking the first significant new footage since the trailer released back in May 2025. It's shaping up to be a big moment for fans who have been counting the days.

Rockstar has also refreshed the official GTA 6 website with new artwork ahead of the event, giving fans a small but welcome taste of what's to come.

## Netflix Gets the Exclusive First Window

In a notable partnership, the extended look will debut exclusively on Netflix at **3 PM ET** on August 27. Later that same evening, at **9 PM ET**, it will go live on Rockstar's own YouTube channel for everyone to watch.

Netflix VP Brandon Riegg described the collaboration as a reflection of the platform's ambition to host major cultural moments beyond traditional film and TV. Rockstar and Netflix have history together — several GTA titles were previously available through Netflix Games, and Red Dead Redemption is still playable there today.

> If you want to catch the reveal the moment it drops, a Netflix subscription will get you in six hours ahead of the YouTube premiere.

## What to Expect

Rockstar hasn't detailed exactly what the extended look will cover, so treat any specific speculation as just that — speculation. Based on the "extended" label, fans are hopeful this goes deeper than a standard trailer, potentially showcasing more of the world, characters, or gameplay systems.
