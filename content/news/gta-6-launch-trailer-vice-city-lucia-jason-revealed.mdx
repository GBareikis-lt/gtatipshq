## Rockstar Drops the GTA 6 Launch Trailer

With just over a month to go before launch, Rockstar Games has released the official launch trailer for GTA 6, and it does not disappoint. Captured on a base PS5, the footage gives us the most expansive look yet at the world, characters, and tone that have been quietly taking shape over more than a decade of development.

## Vice City in Full Swing

The trailer leans hard into the colour and chaos of Vice City — its beaches, back alleys, and larger-than-life residents all on display. Criminal activity is woven throughout, but so is a real sense of place, suggesting Rockstar has built a living city rather than just a sandbox for mayhem. If you want a closer look at the confirmed locations shown, head over to our [interactive map](/map).

## Lucia and Jason: Partners, Lovers, or Both

The trailer features a notably intimate moment between the two protagonists, Lucia and Jason. The pair are confirmed to be romantically involved as well as criminal collaborators, but the game apparently lets players shape that dynamic — keeping them together as a couple or splitting them into strictly professional partners. That kind of relationship flexibility is a fresh wrinkle for the series.

## A Big-Name Soundtrack

Rockstar has tapped French electronic duo Justice to compose the entire original score for GTA 6 — a significant creative choice. The launch trailer itself is set to a new track called **"Flower in a Hurricane"**, a collaborative effort between Justice, Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. It's an eclectic lineup that hints at a genuinely ambitious in-game radio experience.

> The trailer also carries PlayStation branding and features a DualSense-style in-game controller, reflecting an ongoing promotional partnership between Sony and Rockstar.

GTA 6 arrives on **November 19, 2026** for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. A PC version is expected sometime in early 2027. Check our [news hub](/news) for ongoing coverage as launch approaches.
