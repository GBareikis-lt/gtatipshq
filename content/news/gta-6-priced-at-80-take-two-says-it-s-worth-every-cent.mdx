## GTA 6 Will Cost $80 at Launch

When GTA 6 arrives on **November 19, 2026**, it will carry an $80 price tag — $10 more than the $70 that has become the standard for most major releases this generation, including the rest of Take-Two Interactive's catalogue. That premium has raised eyebrows, and Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick addressed it head-on during a recent investor call following the company's quarterly earnings report.

## What Zelnick Actually Said

Speaking to investors, Zelnick made the case that the real-world cost of buying a AAA game is actually lower today than it was two decades ago once inflation is factored in. His argument is straightforward: game prices have not kept pace with rising costs elsewhere, so a modest increase is overdue.

He framed GTA 6's value proposition around Rockstar's reputation for releasing dense, content-rich games, suggesting players will feel the $80 is well spent once they experience what's on offer.

> Zelnick described GTA 6 as an "incredible bargain as experiences go" — high praise from a CEO, though investors are the intended audience here.

## What This Means for Players

A few things worth keeping in mind before launch:

- **$80 is for the standard edition.** Premium or collector's editions will almost certainly cost more — watch for those announcements closer to release.
- **GTA Online will come bundled**, just as it did with GTA 5, meaning the multiplayer component adds to the overall value argument.
- **Discounts will happen.** If $80 feels steep, retailers and digital storefronts typically run promotions within the first few months.

Whether you think $80 is fair will ultimately depend on what Rockstar delivers. Given the studio's history, the expectations are about as high as they get. Keep an eye on our [news](/news) section for any further pricing or edition details as launch approaches.
