## Nintendo Stays Quiet on GTA 6 Switch 2 Hopes

Fans hoping for a definitive answer on whether GTA 6 will land on Nintendo's Switch 2 came away empty-handed after Nintendo of America's president was asked directly about the possibility. Rather than confirm or deny any plans, the executive simply deferred to Rockstar Games and parent company Take-Two Interactive as the parties who would make that call.

It's a diplomatic non-answer, but not a surprising one. Publishers rarely allow platform holders to speak on their behalf about unannounced ports, so the deflection alone doesn't tell us much either way.

## What Would a Switch 2 Port Take?

The Switch 2 is a meaningful hardware step up from its predecessor, but GTA 6 is being built primarily for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC. Bridging that gap would be a significant technical undertaking. A few things worth keeping in mind:

- Rockstar has not acknowledged any Switch 2 version publicly
- Take-Two has historically supported Nintendo platforms selectively — GTA titles have appeared on Nintendo hardware before, but never at launch
- With GTA 6 targeting **November 19, 2026**, a day-one Switch 2 release seems highly unlikely even if a port is eventually planned

> This is unconfirmed territory. Until Rockstar or Take-Two speaks directly, treat any Switch 2 GTA 6 talk as rumour only.

## Why It Still Matters

Switch 2's install base is growing quickly, and Take-Two will want to maximise revenue from one of the most anticipated games in history. A port further down the line — perhaps in 2027 or beyond — remains plausible, even if launch day is out of reach.
