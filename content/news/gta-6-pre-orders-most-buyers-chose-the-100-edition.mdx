## Most Pre-Orders Are for the Pricier Option

Despite plenty of debate online about whether a $100 video game is ever justified, it turns out that most people putting money down on GTA 6 are choosing exactly that price point. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick shared the detail publicly, noting that the Ultimate Edition — priced at $100 (£90) — is outselling the Standard Edition at $80 (£70) among pre-order customers.

That's a striking data point, even if it isn't entirely surprising. Rockstar titles tend to attract dedicated fans who want everything on day one, and a premium bundle typically sweetens the deal with bonus content or early access perks.

## What Does This Mean for Players?

A few things are worth keeping in mind here:

- **The base game is still $80.** You don't need to spend $100 to play GTA 6 on release day, November 19, 2026.
- **Majority doesn't mean everyone.** Pre-order buyers skew toward the most enthusiastic fans, so this sample may not reflect the broader audience that picks up the game post-launch.
- **Value is subjective.** If the Ultimate Edition includes meaningful content — story add-ons, GTA Online currency, or cosmetics — the premium might genuinely pay off depending on how much time you plan to invest.

> Take-Two hasn't detailed exactly what the Ultimate Edition includes beyond its price tag, so hold off on upgrading until the full contents are confirmed.

## Should You Pre-Order?

There's no rush. Pre-ordering early locks in your copy but rarely offers price advantages. It's worth waiting to see the complete breakdown of what each edition contains before committing to the higher tier.
