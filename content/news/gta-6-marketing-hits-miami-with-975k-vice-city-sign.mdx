## Rockstar Brings Vice City to Real-Life Miami

With GTA 6 set to launch on November 19, 2026, Rockstar's promotional push is now very much physical. A large "Welcome to Vice City" sign — complete with the iconic VI logo — has been lifted onto the roof of the Kaseya Center, home of the NBA's Miami Heat, marking one of the most visible pieces of real-world GTA marketing fans have seen yet.

The installation was no small feat. Footage captured by Instagram photographer @officialjoelfranco shows the sign being airlifted into position by helicopter across several hours.

## What It Cost

According to contract documents shared by the fan research account SourceVI, Rockstar agreed to pay Miami-Dade County a total of **$975,000** for the placement, structured in three payments:

- **$243,750** on signing by the County Mayor
- **$243,750** once all materials were delivered to the roof
- **$487,500** once the sign was deemed "Substantially Functional"

The arena's exterior screen is also already displaying "VICE," reinforcing the campaign's street-level presence.

## A Minor Hiccup

Early photos revealed a small installation error — the wrap on the lowercase "i" in "Vice" was misapplied, with material folded over the adjacent "c." According to the Reddit thread where this was first widely discussed, the issue has since been corrected, though official finished photos from Rockstar have not yet been released.

> This kind of real-world marketing suggests Rockstar is ramping up its promotional campaign significantly as the release window approaches.
