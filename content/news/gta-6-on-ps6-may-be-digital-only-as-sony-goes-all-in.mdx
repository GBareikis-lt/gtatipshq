## The End of Physical PlayStation Games

A notable shift is brewing in the console hardware space that could directly affect how players pick up GTA 6 on future platforms. According to industry analyst Mat Piscatella, speaking to VGC, both Sony's next-generation PlayStation and Microsoft's rumoured Project Helix console are expected to go fully digital — meaning no disc drives, no physical copies.

Sony has apparently signalled plans to move away from physical game sales entirely by 2028. Since GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026, the base PS5 release should still be available physically. However, if a PS6 version of the game arrives down the line, it may be digital-only.

## What This Means for GTA 6 Players

- **PS5 launch copies** should remain unaffected — physical editions are still expected for the November 2026 release
- **Future next-gen upgrades** on PS6 or Project Helix could be tied to digital storefronts only
- **Resale and sharing** of game copies would no longer be possible on those platforms
- **Internet connectivity** would become a hard requirement for accessing your library

> Keep in mind these are industry analyst predictions, not confirmed policies from Sony or Microsoft. Treat them as informed rumours for now.

## Nintendo Holds the Physical Fort

Interestingly, Piscatella believes Nintendo will continue doing its own thing regardless of what Sony and Microsoft decide. The Switch 2 already commands a strong share of physical software sales, and retailers have increasingly leaned into Nintendo support. That trend looks set to continue.

For GTA 6 specifically, the practical takeaway is simple: if owning a physical copy matters to you, your best bet remains the PS5 or Xbox Series version at launch. Keep an eye on our [news section](/news) for updates as next-gen hardware details firm up.
