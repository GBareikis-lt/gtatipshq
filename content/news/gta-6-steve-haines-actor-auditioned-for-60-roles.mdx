## A GTA 5 Veteran Tries His Luck

Robert Bogue, the actor behind one of GTA 5's most memorable antagonists — corrupt FIB agent Steve Haines — has revealed that he made a serious push to land a role in GTA 6. According to a report from IGN, Bogue submitted auditions for an impressive 60 different parts in the upcoming game, only to receive complete silence from Rockstar Games in return.

It's a striking anecdote that highlights just how competitive and opaque the casting process for a title of this scale can be, even for actors with an established history in the franchise.

## What This Tells Us About GTA 6's Casting

While Bogue's situation is specific to him, it does shed some light on how Rockstar approaches new entries in the series:

- **Fresh slate mentality** — Rockstar appears to be building a largely new cast for GTA 6, even when veteran performers express strong interest.
- **Scale of the project** — The fact that Bogue could identify and audition for 60 distinct roles suggests GTA 6 has an enormous number of named characters, which fits what we'd expect from a game set across a massive open world.
- **No guarantees from GTA 5 credits** — Past work on the franchise clearly doesn't secure a callback, reinforcing that each mainline GTA title tends to stand on its own terms.

> Keep in mind this is Bogue's personal account of events. Rockstar has not commented publicly on its casting decisions for GTA 6.

## Looking Ahead

With GTA 6 scheduled to launch on **November 19, 2026**, Rockstar is presumably deep into post-production, meaning the core cast is almost certainly locked in at this point. Whether any familiar voices from GTA 5 make the final cut remains to be seen.
